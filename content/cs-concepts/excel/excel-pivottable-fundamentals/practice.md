# PivotTable Fundamentals — Practice

### P1. Pick the feature

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Type:** PivotTable · **Concepts:** when to use

You need to summarise total sales by region. Which Excel feature is most appropriate?

- A) Conditional Formatting
- B) PivotTable
- C) Find & Replace
- D) Freeze Panes

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) PivotTable

Region in Rows and Sales in Values gives the total for each region instantly.

</details>

### P2. Which area?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Type:** PivotTable · **Concepts:** field placement

You want each product to appear as a separate row label down the left side of the PivotTable. Where do you put the Product field?

- A) Filters
- B) Columns
- C) Rows
- D) Values

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) Rows

Rows lists each unique value of the field down the left.

</details>

### P3. Read the PivotTable

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Type:** PivotTable · **Concepts:** result interpretation

| Sum of Sales | North | South | Grand Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| Laptop | 136000 | 157000 | 293000 |
| Mobile | 91000 | 111000 | 202000 |
| Grand Total | 227000 | 268000 | 495000 |

Which region sold more, and what were the South region's Mobile sales?

- A) North; 91000
- B) South; 111000
- C) South; 157000
- D) North; 111000

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) South; 111000

The Grand Total row shows South 268000 > North 227000. The Mobile row under South is 111000.

</details>

### P4. Only one month

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Type:** PivotTable · **Concepts:** filters area

The PivotTable above should show only March figures, without adding a Month row or column. What do you do?

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

Drag **Month** into the **Filters** area and select **Mar** in the drop-down above the PivotTable. Every value then uses March rows only (Grand Total 172000).

</details>
