# PivotChart Basics — Interview Questions

## Beginner

### Q1. What is a PivotChart?

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

A PivotChart is a chart linked to a PivotTable. It plots the PivotTable's summary — for example Sum of Sales by product and region — and updates automatically when the PivotTable is rearranged, filtered or refreshed. You create it from inside a PivotTable with PivotTable Analyze → PivotChart.

</details>

### Q2. What is the difference between a PivotTable and a PivotChart?

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

The PivotTable shows the summary as numbers in a grid; the PivotChart shows the same summary as a picture. The chart depends on the table: they share fields and filters, so filtering one filters the other. Use the PivotTable for exact values and the PivotChart to show the pattern at a glance.

</details>

## Intermediate

### Q3. Why use a PivotChart instead of a normal chart?

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

A normal chart plots a fixed set of cells, so you must first build and maintain a summary table. A PivotChart plots the PivotTable directly, follows every change in its layout, can be filtered with field buttons on the chart itself, and updates when the PivotTable is refreshed with new data. That makes it the quicker choice for summarised, changing data.

</details>
