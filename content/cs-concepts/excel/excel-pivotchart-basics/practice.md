# PivotChart Basics — Practice

### P1. Linked chart

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Type:** MCQ · **Concepts:** PivotChart

Which chart updates automatically when you drag a different field into a PivotTable's Rows area?

- A) A normal column chart made from other cells
- B) A PivotChart created from that PivotTable
- C) A picture of a chart pasted into the sheet
- D) A sparkline

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) A PivotChart created from that PivotTable

A PivotChart is linked to its PivotTable and follows its layout.

</details>

### P2. Choose the chart

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Type:** Chart · **Concepts:** chart type, PivotChart

Your PivotTable has Month in Rows and Sum of Sales in Values. The manager asks whether sales are rising. Which PivotChart type do you choose?

- A) Pie
- B) Line
- C) Clustered Bar with Month in Filters
- D) Doughnut

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) Line

The question is about a trend over months; a line chart shows the direction clearly (160000 → 163000 → 172000).

</details>

### P3. Filter from the chart

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Type:** Scenario · **Concepts:** field buttons

In a meeting you want the PivotChart to show only the North region. How do you do it, and what else changes?

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

Open the **Region** field button on the chart and keep only North. The connected PivotTable is filtered to North as well, because the PivotChart and PivotTable share their filters.

</details>
