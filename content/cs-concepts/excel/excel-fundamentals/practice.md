# Excel Fundamentals — Practice

### P1. File or sheet?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Type:** MCQ · **Concepts:** workbook, worksheet

You save your work as `Attendance.xlsx`. Inside it are tabs named `June` and `July`. What is `Attendance.xlsx`?

- A) A worksheet
- B) A workbook
- C) A range
- D) A cell

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) A workbook

The file is the workbook; `June` and `July` are worksheets inside it.

</details>

### P2. Count the cells

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Type:** MCQ · **Concepts:** range

How many cells does the range `B2:D5` contain?

- A) 4
- B) 8
- C) 12
- D) 15

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) 12

Columns B, C, D = 3 columns; rows 2 to 5 = 4 rows; 3 × 4 = 12.

</details>

### P3. Where is the formula?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Type:** MCQ · **Concepts:** formula bar

Cell D2 shows `9000`. How can you tell whether `9000` was typed in or calculated by a formula?

- A) Look at the sheet tab
- B) Select D2 and look at the formula bar
- C) Look at the column heading
- D) Check the status bar

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) Select D2 and look at the formula bar

The cell shows the result; the formula bar shows the real content, such as `=B2*0.18`.

</details>
