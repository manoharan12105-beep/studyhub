# Rapid-Fire Database Questions

**Module:** Interview · **Interview priority:** Core

## What Is It?

Short questions with one- or two-sentence answers, for the rapid-fire round at the start or end of an interview and for last-minute revision. The [questions](interview-questions.md) cover DBMS theory, SQL, PostgreSQL, transactions, indexing and optimization. Each answer is deliberately short; follow the lesson links in the other interview banks for depth.

## Why It Matters

- Rapid-fire rounds check breadth and precision under time pressure.
- Answering in one accurate sentence is a skill you can practise.

## Core Concept

### How to answer

- Lead with the exact answer ("No — …", "`DENSE_RANK`").
- Add one qualifier that shows understanding ("…because `NULL` comparisons are unknown").
- Stop. Offer an example only if asked.

## Revision

Read the questions aloud and answer before opening each answer. Re-read the ones you missed until they are automatic.

## Quick Revision

Exact answer first, one qualifier, stop.
