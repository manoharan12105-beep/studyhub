# Interview Questions: Foundations and Communication

**Module:** Interview Preparation · **Interview priority:** Core

## What Is It?

A mixed [question bank](interview-questions.md) covering the Foundations and Communication modules: requirements and estimation, availability, architecture styles, scaling and statelessness, the request path, DNS, HTTP and APIs, rate limiting, real-time communication, proxies, gateways and CDNs.

## Why It Matters

Interviewers rarely ask "define X" in isolation. They combine topics ("your API is slow for users in Asia — why?") and follow up on your answer. These questions mix modules so you practise connecting ideas, in the order an interviewer might.

## Core Concept

### How to answer

1. **Answer directly** in one sentence.
2. **Explain the mechanism** — why it works or fails.
3. **Give a concrete example or number.**
4. **State the trade-off** or the condition that would change the answer.

### Where to revise

Each question's topic has a full lesson in the [Foundations](../../foundations/what-is-system-design/content.md) or [Communication](../../communication/request-journey/content.md) modules. Lesson-level questions are in each topic's own interview tab; this bank deliberately mixes them.

## Key Takeaways

- Expect combined, scenario-style questions rather than definitions.
- Answer → mechanism → example → trade-off.
- Use numbers and name what you would measure.
