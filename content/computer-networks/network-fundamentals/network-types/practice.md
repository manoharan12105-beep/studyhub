# Network Types — Practice

### P1. Classify by size

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Type:** MCQ · **Concepts:** PAN, LAN, MAN, WAN

A smartwatch syncs with a phone over Bluetooth. Which network type is this?

- A) LAN
- B) PAN
- C) MAN
- D) WAN

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) PAN

**Explanation:** A personal area network covers a few metres around one person.

</details>

### P2. Classify by access

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Type:** Conceptual · **Concepts:** intranet, extranet

A car manufacturer runs (a) an internal HR portal for employees, (b) a parts-ordering portal for its dealers, (c) its public marketing website. Classify each.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

(a) Intranet, (b) extranet, (c) Internet.

</details>

### P3. Which is a WAN?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Type:** Scenario · **Concepts:** WAN

A company has offices in Chennai, Pune and Delhi. Each office has Ethernet and Wi-Fi. They are linked by leased MPLS circuits. Identify the LANs and the WAN, and the device type at each office boundary.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

Each office network is a LAN; the MPLS circuits joining them form a WAN. A router at each office connects its LAN to the WAN.

</details>

### P4. Latency estimate

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Type:** Calculation · **Concepts:** WAN latency

Light in fibre travels about 200,000 km/s. Estimate the minimum one-way propagation delay over 2,000 km of fibre between Chennai and Delhi.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

2,000 km ÷ 200,000 km/s = 0.01 s = **10 ms** one way (about 20 ms round trip), before any queuing or processing delay. A LAN hop is far below 1 ms.

</details>
