# Bits, Signals and Transmission Media — Practice

### P1. Choose the medium

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Type:** MCQ · **Concepts:** media selection

You must connect two buildings 2 km apart at 10 Gbit/s next to a factory with heavy electrical equipment. Which medium fits best?

- A) Cat6 twisted pair
- B) Single-mode optical fibre
- C) 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi
- D) Coaxial cable

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) Single-mode optical fibre

**Explanation:** It covers kilometres at high speed and ignores electrical interference. Copper is limited to about 100 m.

</details>

### P2. Duplex mode

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Type:** Conceptual · **Concepts:** duplex

Classify: (a) a TV broadcast, (b) a hub-based LAN, (c) a switch port talking to a server.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

(a) Simplex, (b) half duplex, (c) full duplex.

</details>

### P3. Layer 1 or not?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Type:** Troubleshooting · **Concepts:** physical-layer symptoms

Which of these point to a Physical-layer problem: (a) link light off on the port, (b) DNS returns NXDOMAIN, (c) Wi-Fi drops in one room only, (d) many CRC errors on an interface after a cable was moved?

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

(a), (c) and (d). (b) is an application-layer DNS answer. CRC errors are detected at Layer 2 but are usually caused by Layer 1 damage or interference.

</details>
