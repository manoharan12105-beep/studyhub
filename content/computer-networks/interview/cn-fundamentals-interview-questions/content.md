# Interview Questions: Fundamentals, Devices and Models

**Module:** Interview Preparation · **Interview priority:** Core

## What Is It?

A cross-topic bank of [interview questions](interview-questions.md) on network fundamentals, devices, the OSI and TCP/IP models, Ethernet, switching and ARP — the questions that open almost every networking round in placement and backend interviews.

## Why It Matters

These questions test whether you think in **layers**. A strong answer names the layer, the address or PDU involved, and a concrete example. Weak answers recite definitions; strong answers compare and explain *why*.

## Core Concept

### How to answer

1. **One-line definition** — what it is.
2. **Layer and mechanism** — where it works and what it reads (MAC, IP, port, HTTP).
3. **Comparison or example** — "unlike a hub…", "for example, your home router…".
4. **Practical consequence** — a symptom or a design choice it explains.

## Key Takeaways

- Always place the concept in a layer and name what it reads.
- Comparisons are the most common format: hub/switch/router, MAC/IP, OSI/TCP-IP, bandwidth/throughput/latency.
- Give a real-world example in every answer.
