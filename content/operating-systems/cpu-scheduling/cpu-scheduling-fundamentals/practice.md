### P1. Preemptive or not?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Type:** MCQ · **Concepts:** preemptive scheduling

Which algorithm is preemptive?

- A) FCFS
- B) SJF (non-preemptive)
- C) Round Robin
- D) Non-preemptive Priority

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) Round Robin

</details>

### P2. Which scheduler?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Type:** MCQ · **Concepts:** schedulers

The scheduler that controls the degree of multiprogramming is the:

- A) Short-term scheduler
- B) Long-term scheduler
- C) Dispatcher
- D) I/O scheduler

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) Long-term scheduler

</details>

### P3. Match the criterion

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Type:** Conceptual · **Concepts:** scheduling criteria

Which criterion matters most for (a) a desktop where users type and click, (b) a nightly batch system processing payroll jobs?

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

(a) **Response time** — users notice delay before the first reaction. (b) **Throughput** (and turnaround time) — what matters is completing as many jobs as possible in the night.

</details>

### P4. Utilisation with idle time

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Type:** Calculation · **Concepts:** CPU utilisation, throughput

Over 20 ms, the CPU runs processes for 16 ms and is idle for 4 ms, and 5 processes complete. What are the CPU utilisation and the throughput?

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

Utilisation = 16 ÷ 20 = **80 %**. Throughput = 5 processes ÷ 20 ms = **0.25 processes per ms** (250 per second).

</details>
