# Fill in the Blanks — Practice

### P1. The weather was so ___ that we decided to stay indoors.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Degree

- A) pleasant
- B) harsh
- C) mild
- D) sunny

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) harsh

**Explanation:** "So … that we stayed indoors" needs weather bad enough to keep people in.

</details>

### P2. He was so ___ that he fell asleep during the lecture.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Degree

- A) tired
- B) energetic
- C) alert
- D) excited

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** A) tired

**Explanation:** Falling asleep is the result of being tired.

</details>

### P3. Despite his wealth, he lived a ___ life.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Contrast

- A) luxurious
- B) simple
- C) lavish
- D) grand

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) simple

**Explanation:** "Despite" signals contrast with wealth; the other options match wealth.

</details>

### P4. The scientist's ___ research led to an important discovery.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Cause

- A) careless
- B) meticulous
- C) hasty
- D) casual

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) meticulous

**Explanation:** Research that leads to a discovery is careful and thorough — "meticulous".

</details>

### P5. The manager ___ the proposal because it was too expensive.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Cause

- A) accepted
- B) rejected
- C) approved
- D) praised

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) rejected

**Explanation:** Being too expensive is a reason to reject.

</details>

### P6. Although the task was difficult, she ___ it in an hour.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Contrast

- A) abandoned
- B) completed
- C) avoided
- D) postponed

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) completed

**Explanation:** "Although … difficult" sets up a contrast: she succeeded anyway.

</details>

### P7. His ___ remarks offended many people at the meeting.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Cause

- A) tactful
- B) rude
- C) polite
- D) gentle

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) rude

**Explanation:** Remarks that offend people are rude.

</details>

### P8. The new policy was ___ by the staff because it ___ their workload.

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Double blanks

- A) welcomed, increased
- B) opposed, increased
- C) opposed, reduced
- D) welcomed, doubled

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) opposed, increased

**Explanation:** The two blanks must agree: staff oppose a policy that increases their workload. A and D pair "welcomed" with a heavier workload; C pairs "opposed" with a lighter one.

</details>

### P9. Her ___ to detail made her an excellent editor.

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Collocation

- A) attention
- B) indifference
- C) aversion
- D) neglect

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** A) attention

**Explanation:** "Attention to detail" is the fixed expression and explains why she is an excellent editor.

</details>

### P10. The evidence was ___, so the judge could not convict the accused.

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Cause

- A) conclusive
- B) inadequate
- C) overwhelming
- D) compelling

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) inadequate

**Explanation:** The judge could not convict because the evidence was not enough. The other options describe strong evidence.

</details>

### P11. The speaker was so ___ that the audience hung on every word.

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Degree

- A) monotonous
- B) boring
- C) eloquent
- D) tedious

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) eloquent

**Explanation:** "Hung on every word" means the audience listened eagerly — the speaker was fluent and persuasive (eloquent).

</details>
