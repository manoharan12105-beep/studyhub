# Pronouns — Practice

Choose the correct pronoun.

### P1. This secret is between you and ___.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Case after prepositions

- A) I
- B) me
- C) myself
- D) mine

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) me

**Explanation:** "Between" is a preposition, so it takes the object form.

</details>

### P2. She can run faster than ___ can.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Case after than

- A) me
- B) I
- C) myself
- D) mine

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) I

**Explanation:** The verb "can" follows, so the pronoun is its subject: "than I can".

</details>

### P3. ___ is calling you?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Who / whom

- A) Who
- B) Whom
- C) Whose
- D) Which

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** A) Who

**Explanation:** The pronoun is the subject of "is calling" ("He is calling" → who).

</details>

### P4. To ___ did you give the book?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Who / whom

- A) who
- B) whom
- C) whose
- D) which

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) whom

**Explanation:** The pronoun is the object of "to" ("You gave it to him" → whom).

</details>

### P5. Ravi and ___ went to the market.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Subject case

- A) I
- B) me
- C) myself
- D) mine

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** A) I

**Explanation:** Remove "Ravi and": "I went to the market".

</details>

### P6. Let you and ___ finish the work together.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Object case

- A) I
- B) me
- C) myself
- D) mine

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) me

**Explanation:** "Let" takes an object: "let me".

</details>

### P7. The boy ___ won the prize is my cousin.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Relative pronouns

- A) which
- B) who
- C) whom
- D) whose

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) who

**Explanation:** For a person who is the subject of "won", use "who".

</details>

### P8. One should respect ___ parents.

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Agreement

- A) his
- B) their
- C) one's
- D) your

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) one's

**Explanation:** In formal English, "one" is followed by "one's".

</details>

### P9. Each of the girls has submitted ___ assignment.

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Agreement

- A) her
- B) their
- C) hers
- D) its

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** A) her

**Explanation:** "Each" is singular and refers to girls → "her".

</details>

### P10. The company has changed ___ leave policy.

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Agreement

- A) its
- B) it's
- C) their
- D) his

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** A) its

**Explanation:** "Company" is a singular collective noun; possession uses "its" (not "it's", which means "it is").

</details>

### P11. Please send the report to Anita or ___.

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Reflexive misuse

- A) I
- B) me
- C) myself
- D) mine

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) me

**Explanation:** Object of the preposition "to" → "me". "Myself" is reflexive and needs the subject "I" in the same clause.

</details>
