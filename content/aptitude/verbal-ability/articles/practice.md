# Articles — Practice

Choose the correct article. "No article" means the blank should be left empty.

### P1. She is ___ honest woman.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** A or an

- A) a
- B) an
- C) the
- D) No article

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) an

**Explanation:** "Honest" begins with a vowel sound (the h is silent).

</details>

### P2. He is ___ university professor.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** A or an

- A) a
- B) an
- C) the
- D) No article

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** A) a

**Explanation:** "University" begins with a "yoo" consonant sound.

</details>

### P3. ___ sun rises in the east.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Unique things

- A) A
- B) An
- C) The
- D) No article

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) The

**Explanation:** The sun is unique.

</details>

### P4. He plays ___ guitar very well.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Musical instruments

- A) a
- B) an
- C) the
- D) No article

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) the

**Explanation:** "Play the" + musical instrument.

</details>

### P5. Mount Everest is ___ highest mountain in the world.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Superlatives

- A) a
- B) an
- C) the
- D) No article

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) the

**Explanation:** Superlatives take "the".

</details>

### P6. She goes to ___ church every Sunday to pray.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Institutions

- A) a
- B) an
- C) the
- D) No article

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** D) No article

**Explanation:** "Church" is used for its main purpose (worship), so no article.

</details>

### P7. We should always help ___ poor.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** The + adjective

- A) a
- B) an
- C) the
- D) No article

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) the

**Explanation:** "The poor" means poor people as a group.

</details>

### P8. He is ___ MBA from a reputed institute.

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** A or an

- A) a
- B) an
- C) the
- D) No article

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) an

**Explanation:** "MBA" is read "em-bee-ay", which begins with a vowel sound.

</details>

### P9. ___ Ganga is a holy river.

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Rivers

- A) A
- B) An
- C) The
- D) No article

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) The

**Explanation:** Names of rivers take "the".

</details>

### P10. I had ___ breakfast at seven this morning.

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Meals

- A) a
- B) an
- C) the
- D) No article

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** D) No article

**Explanation:** Meals in general take no article (compare "a heavy breakfast", where the adjective brings back the article).

</details>

### P11. She speaks ___ French fluently.

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Languages

- A) a
- B) an
- C) the
- D) No article

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** D) No article

**Explanation:** Names of languages take no article ("the French" would mean the French people).

</details>
