# Idioms and Phrases — Practice

Choose the meaning of the idiom in bold.

### P1. The exam was **a piece of cake**.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Common idioms

- A) Very difficult
- B) Very easy
- C) Very long
- D) Very boring

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) Very easy

**Explanation:** "A piece of cake" means something very easy to do.

</details>

### P2. He visits his hometown **once in a blue moon**.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Common idioms

- A) Every month
- B) Very rarely
- C) Every full moon
- D) Never

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) Very rarely

**Explanation:** It means very seldom, not never.

</details>

### P3. I'm feeling a little **under the weather** today.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Common idioms

- A) Slightly ill
- B) Very happy
- C) Wet from the rain
- D) Too hot

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** A) Slightly ill

**Explanation:** "Under the weather" means unwell; the weather option is the literal trap.

</details>

### P4. She **let the cat out of the bag** about the surprise party.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Common idioms

- A) Cancelled the plan
- B) Revealed the secret
- C) Made a mess
- D) Arrived late

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) Revealed the secret

**Explanation:** The idiom means revealing a secret, usually by mistake.

</details>

### P5. After years of rivalry, the two leaders decided to **bury the hatchet**.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Common idioms

- A) Hide their weapons
- B) Make peace
- C) Start a fight
- D) Retire

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) Make peace

**Explanation:** "Bury the hatchet" means to end a quarrel.

</details>

### P6. He submitted his application **at the eleventh hour**.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Common idioms

- A) At 11 o'clock
- B) Very early
- C) At the last possible moment
- D) After the deadline

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) At the last possible moment

**Explanation:** It means just before it is too late — still before the deadline.

</details>

### P7. Her refusal to compromise only **added fuel to the fire**.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Common idioms

- A) Solved the problem
- B) Made the situation worse
- C) Cooled things down
- D) Ended the argument

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) Made the situation worse

**Explanation:** Adding fuel to a fire makes it bigger — the dispute grew worse.

</details>

### P8. The newly built stadium turned out to be **a white elephant**.

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Common idioms

- A) A great success
- B) A costly possession that is a burden
- C) A rare and sacred object
- D) A popular tourist spot

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) A costly possession that is a burden

**Explanation:** A white elephant costs a lot to keep but gives little benefit.

</details>

### P9. Mathematics has always been his **Achilles' heel**.

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Common idioms

- A) Strongest subject
- B) Weak point
- C) Favourite hobby
- D) Main ambition

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) Weak point

**Explanation:** From the Greek hero Achilles, whose heel was his only vulnerable spot.

</details>

### P10. The corrupt official was found to be **hand in glove** with the smugglers.

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Common idioms

- A) Fighting against
- B) Closely associated with
- C) Unaware of
- D) Investigating

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) Closely associated with

**Explanation:** "Hand in glove" means in close partnership, usually in wrongdoing.

</details>

### P11. When the manager arrived, the office was **at sixes and sevens**.

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Common idioms

- A) Well organised
- B) In confusion and disorder
- C) Nearly empty
- D) Working overtime

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) In confusion and disorder

**Explanation:** "At sixes and sevens" means in a state of confusion.

</details>
