# Error Spotting — Practice

Find the part of the sentence that contains an error. If there is no error, choose D.

### P1. He go to (A) / the gym (B) / every morning. (C) / No error (D)

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Subject–verb agreement

- A) He go to
- B) the gym
- C) every morning
- D) No error

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** A) He go to

**Explanation:** "He" takes "goes".

</details>

### P2. She is (A) / more taller (B) / than her sister. (C) / No error (D)

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Comparisons

- A) She is
- B) more taller
- C) than her sister
- D) No error

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) more taller

**Explanation:** Double comparative; use "taller".

</details>

### P3. I have seen (A) / that film (B) / last week. (C) / No error (D)

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Tense

- A) I have seen
- B) that film
- C) last week
- D) No error

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** A) I have seen

**Explanation:** "Last week" is a definite past time, so the verb must be simple past: "I saw". The part that must change is A.

</details>

### P4. Each of the boys (A) / have completed (B) / the assignment. (C) / No error (D)

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Subject–verb agreement

- A) Each of the boys
- B) have completed
- C) the assignment
- D) No error

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) have completed

**Explanation:** "Each" is singular → "has completed".

</details>

### P5. The furniture (A) / in this room (B) / are very old. (C) / No error (D)

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Uncountable nouns

- A) The furniture
- B) in this room
- C) are very old
- D) No error

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) are very old

**Explanation:** "Furniture" is uncountable and singular → "is very old".

</details>

### P6. He is senior (A) / than me (B) / in the office. (C) / No error (D)

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Prepositions

- A) He is senior
- B) than me
- C) in the office
- D) No error

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) than me

**Explanation:** "Senior" takes "to": "senior to me".

</details>

### P7. She has been (A) / living in Delhi (B) / since ten years. (C) / No error (D)

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Since / for

- A) She has been
- B) living in Delhi
- C) since ten years
- D) No error

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) since ten years

**Explanation:** A period takes "for": "for ten years".

</details>

### P8. One of my friend (A) / is coming (B) / to visit us today. (C) / No error (D)

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Noun number

- A) One of my friend
- B) is coming
- C) to visit us today
- D) No error

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** A) One of my friend

**Explanation:** "One of" is followed by a plural noun: "one of my friends". The verb "is" correctly agrees with "one".

</details>

### P9. Unless you do not work hard (A) / you will not (B) / pass the exam. (C) / No error (D)

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Double negatives

- A) Unless you do not work hard
- B) you will not
- C) pass the exam
- D) No error

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** A) Unless you do not work hard

**Explanation:** "Unless" already means "if not": "Unless you work hard, you will not pass."

</details>

### P10. The committee has (A) / submitted its (B) / report to the director. (C) / No error (D)

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** No error

- A) The committee has
- B) submitted its
- C) report to the director
- D) No error

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** D) No error

**Explanation:** "Committee" acting as one body takes "has" and "its". The sentence is correct.

</details>

### P11. He gave me (A) / an useful (B) / piece of advice. (C) / No error (D)

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Articles

- A) He gave me
- B) an useful
- C) piece of advice
- D) No error

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) an useful

**Explanation:** "Useful" begins with a "yoo" consonant sound → "a useful".

</details>
