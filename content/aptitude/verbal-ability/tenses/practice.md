# Tenses — Practice

Choose the correct form of the verb.

### P1. She ___ to school every day.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Simple present

- A) goes
- B) is going
- C) has gone
- D) had gone

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** A) goes

**Explanation:** "Every day" signals a habit → simple present.

</details>

### P2. Look! The baby ___.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Present continuous

- A) sleeps
- B) is sleeping
- C) slept
- D) has slept

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) is sleeping

**Explanation:** "Look!" signals an action happening now.

</details>

### P3. I ___ him yesterday.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Simple past

- A) have met
- B) met
- C) meet
- D) had met

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) met

**Explanation:** "Yesterday" is a definite past time → simple past. Present perfect cannot be used with it.

</details>

### P4. They ___ in this city since 2015.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Since / for

- A) live
- B) are living
- C) have been living
- D) lived

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) have been living

**Explanation:** "Since 2015" with an action continuing to now → present perfect continuous.

</details>

### P5. By the time we reached the station, the train ___.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Two past actions

- A) left
- B) has left
- C) had left
- D) leaves

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) had left

**Explanation:** The train left before we reached → the earlier past action takes past perfect.

</details>

### P6. I ___ the answer to this question.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Stative verbs

- A) am knowing
- B) know
- C) was knowing
- D) have been knowing

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) know

**Explanation:** "Know" is a stative verb and is not used in continuous forms.

</details>

### P7. If it rains tomorrow, we ___ the match.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Conditionals

- A) cancelled
- B) would cancel
- C) will cancel
- D) had cancelled

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) will cancel

**Explanation:** A real future possibility → first conditional: if + present, will + verb.

</details>

### P8. When he ___, I will give him the message.

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Future time clauses

- A) will come
- B) comes
- C) came
- D) would come

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) comes

**Explanation:** In a future time clause after "when", English uses the present tense.

</details>

### P9. If I ___ you, I would accept the offer.

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Conditionals

- A) am
- B) were
- C) will be
- D) had been

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) were

**Explanation:** Second conditional (imaginary situation): if + past form; formal English uses "were" for all persons.

</details>

### P10. She ___ for two hours when the power went out.

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Past perfect continuous

- A) studied
- B) has been studying
- C) had been studying
- D) is studying

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) had been studying

**Explanation:** An action that continued for a period up to a past moment ("when the power went out") → past perfect continuous.

</details>

### P11. If you had told me earlier, I ___ you.

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Conditionals

- A) would help
- B) will help
- C) would have helped
- D) had helped

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) would have helped

**Explanation:** Third conditional (imaginary past): if + past perfect, would have + past participle.

</details>
