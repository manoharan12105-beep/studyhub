# Parts of Speech — Practice

Identify the part of speech of the word in bold.

### P1. The **happy** child laughed loudly.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Identify the part of speech

- A) Noun
- B) Adjective
- C) Adverb
- D) Verb

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) Adjective

**Explanation:** "Happy" describes the noun "child".

</details>

### P2. She sang **beautifully** at the concert.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Identify the part of speech

- A) Adjective
- B) Adverb
- C) Noun
- D) Preposition

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) Adverb

**Explanation:** It tells how she sang — it describes the verb.

</details>

### P3. **Wow**, what a beautiful view!

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Identify the part of speech

- A) Interjection
- B) Adverb
- C) Conjunction
- D) Pronoun

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** A) Interjection

**Explanation:** "Wow" expresses sudden emotion.

</details>

### P4. She runs **fast**.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Same word, different roles

- A) Adjective
- B) Adverb
- C) Noun
- D) Verb

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) Adverb

**Explanation:** "Fast" tells how she runs. In "a fast runner" it would be an adjective.

</details>

### P5. Many people **fast** during the festival.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Same word, different roles

- A) Adjective
- B) Adverb
- C) Noun
- D) Verb

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** D) Verb

**Explanation:** Here "fast" is the action the people perform.

</details>

### P6. The book is **on** the table.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Identify the part of speech

- A) Adverb
- B) Preposition
- C) Conjunction
- D) Adjective

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) Preposition

**Explanation:** "On" relates "book" to "the table" and is followed by a noun.

</details>

### P7. I stayed at home **because** it was raining.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Identify the part of speech

- A) Preposition
- B) Adverb
- C) Conjunction
- D) Pronoun

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) Conjunction

**Explanation:** "Because" joins two clauses: "I stayed at home" and "it was raining".

</details>

### P8. **Swimming** is good exercise.

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Words ending in -ing

- A) Verb
- B) Adjective
- C) Noun
- D) Adverb

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) Noun

**Explanation:** "Swimming" is the subject of the sentence — a gerund, which works as a noun.

</details>

### P9. Wash the vegetables in **running** water.

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Words ending in -ing

- A) Noun
- B) Verb
- C) Adjective
- D) Adverb

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) Adjective

**Explanation:** "Running" describes the noun "water" (a participle used as an adjective).

</details>

### P10. He left **after** the meeting ended.

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Same word, different roles

- A) Preposition
- B) Conjunction
- C) Adverb
- D) Adjective

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) Conjunction

**Explanation:** "After" introduces a clause with its own verb ("the meeting ended"). In "after the meeting" it would be a preposition.

</details>

### P11. **Those** books on the shelf are mine.

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Same word, different roles

- A) Pronoun
- B) Adjective
- C) Adverb
- D) Noun

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) Adjective

**Explanation:** "Those" comes before the noun "books" and points it out (a demonstrative adjective). In "Those are mine" it would be a pronoun.

</details>
