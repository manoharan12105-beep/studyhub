# Direct and Indirect Speech — Practice

Choose the correct indirect form.

### P1. He said, "I am busy."

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Statements

- A) He said that he is busy.
- B) He said that he was busy.
- C) He said that I was busy.
- D) He told that he was busy.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) He said that he was busy.

**Explanation:** Past reporting verb → "am" becomes "was"; "I" becomes "he". "Told" needs an object ("told me").

</details>

### P2. She said, "I will come tomorrow."

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Statements

- A) She said that she will come tomorrow.
- B) She said that she would come the next day.
- C) She said that she would come tomorrow.
- D) She said that she will come the next day.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) She said that she would come the next day.

**Explanation:** "Will" → "would"; "tomorrow" → "the next day".

</details>

### P3. The teacher said, "The earth goes round the sun."

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Universal truths

- A) The teacher said that the earth went round the sun.
- B) The teacher said that the earth goes round the sun.
- C) The teacher said that the earth had gone round the sun.
- D) The teacher told that the earth goes round the sun.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) The teacher said that the earth goes round the sun.

**Explanation:** Universal truths keep the present tense.

</details>

### P4. He said to me, "Where do you live?"

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Wh- questions

- A) He asked me where did I live.
- B) He asked me where I lived.
- C) He told me where I lived.
- D) He asked me where do I live.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) He asked me where I lived.

**Explanation:** Questions → "asked"; statement word order (I lived); present → past.

</details>

### P5. She said to him, "Are you coming?"

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Yes/no questions

- A) She asked him that he was coming.
- B) She asked him if he was coming.
- C) She asked him if was he coming.
- D) She told him whether he is coming.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) She asked him if he was coming.

**Explanation:** Yes/no questions use "if/whether" with statement word order and backshift.

</details>

### P6. The officer said to the soldiers, "Stand still."

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Commands

- A) The officer ordered the soldiers that they stand still.
- B) The officer ordered the soldiers to stand still.
- C) The officer said the soldiers to stand still.
- D) The officer ordered the soldiers stand still.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) The officer ordered the soldiers to stand still.

**Explanation:** Commands → ordered/told + object + to + verb.

</details>

### P7. He said, "I met her yesterday."

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Statements

- A) He said that he met her yesterday.
- B) He said that he had met her the previous day.
- C) He said that he has met her the previous day.
- D) He said that I had met her yesterday.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) He said that he had met her the previous day.

**Explanation:** Simple past → past perfect; "yesterday" → "the previous day".

</details>

### P8. She said to me, "Please help me."

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Requests

- A) She requested me to help her.
- B) She requested me to help me.
- C) She told me please help her.
- D) She requested that I helped her.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** A) She requested me to help her.

**Explanation:** Requests → requested + object + to + verb; "me" (the speaker) becomes "her".

</details>

### P9. He said, "Let's go for a walk."

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Suggestions

- A) He said let's go for a walk.
- B) He suggested that they should go for a walk.
- C) He suggested them to go for a walk.
- D) He told that they go for a walk.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) He suggested that they should go for a walk.

**Explanation:** "Let's" → "suggested that … should". "Suggest" does not take "someone to do".

</details>

### P10. The mother said to her son, "Don't play in the sun."

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Commands

- A) The mother told her son to not play in the sun.
- B) The mother told her son not to play in the sun.
- C) The mother told her son that don't play in the sun.
- D) The mother said her son not to play in the sun.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) The mother told her son not to play in the sun.

**Explanation:** Negative commands → told + object + **not to** + verb.

</details>

### P11. He says, "I am tired."

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Present reporting verb

- A) He says that he was tired.
- B) He says that he is tired.
- C) He said that he is tired.
- D) He says that I am tired.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) He says that he is tired.

**Explanation:** The reporting verb "says" is present, so the tense does not change.

</details>
