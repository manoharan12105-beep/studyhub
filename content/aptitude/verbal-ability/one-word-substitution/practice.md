# One Word Substitution — Practice

Choose the single word for the description.

### P1. The life history of a person written by that person

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Roots

- A) Biography
- B) Autobiography
- C) Memoir
- D) Diary

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) Autobiography

**Explanation:** *Auto-* = self. A biography is written by someone else.

</details>

### P2. One who cannot read or write

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Frequently tested words

- A) Ignorant
- B) Illiterate
- C) Illegible
- D) Innocent

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) Illiterate

**Explanation:** Illegible describes writing that cannot be read, not a person.

</details>

### P3. A person who is 100 years old or more

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Frequently tested words

- A) Veteran
- B) Centenarian
- C) Octogenarian
- D) Senior

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) Centenarian

**Explanation:** *Centum* = hundred. An octogenarian is in their eighties.

</details>

### P4. One who knows everything

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Roots

- A) Omnipotent
- B) Omnipresent
- C) Omniscient
- D) Omnivorous

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) Omniscient

**Explanation:** *Omni-* (all) + *scient* (knowing). Omnipotent = all-powerful; omnipresent = present everywhere.

</details>

### P5. A disease that spreads over a whole country or the world

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Near-pairs

- A) Epidemic
- B) Pandemic
- C) Endemic
- D) Contagion

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) Pandemic

**Explanation:** *Pan-* = all. An epidemic spreads within a community; an endemic disease is regularly found in a particular area.

</details>

### P6. One who collects stamps

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Frequently tested words

- A) Numismatist
- B) Philatelist
- C) Cartographer
- D) Archivist

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) Philatelist

**Explanation:** A numismatist collects coins; a cartographer draws maps.

</details>

### P7. Fear of closed spaces

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Roots

- A) Acrophobia
- B) Hydrophobia
- C) Claustrophobia
- D) Xenophobia

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) Claustrophobia

**Explanation:** Acrophobia = fear of heights; hydrophobia = fear of water; xenophobia = fear or dislike of foreigners.

</details>

### P8. Killing of a whole race or group of people

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Roots

- A) Homicide
- B) Regicide
- C) Genocide
- D) Fratricide

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) Genocide

**Explanation:** *Genos* = race/kind. Regicide = killing a king; fratricide = killing a brother.

</details>

### P9. A speech made without preparation

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Frequently tested words

- A) Extempore
- B) Soliloquy
- C) Monologue
- D) Eulogy

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** A) Extempore

**Explanation:** A soliloquy is speaking one's thoughts aloud alone; a eulogy is a speech praising someone, usually after death.

</details>

### P10. Something published after the death of its author

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Frequently tested words

- A) Posthumous
- B) Anonymous
- C) Premature
- D) Obsolete

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** A) Posthumous

**Explanation:** Posthumous means occurring after death.

</details>

### P11. A person who cannot be reformed or corrected

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Frequently tested words

- A) Incorrigible
- B) Invincible
- C) Inevitable
- D) Infallible

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** A) Incorrigible

**Explanation:** Invincible = cannot be defeated; inevitable = cannot be avoided; infallible = incapable of error.

</details>
