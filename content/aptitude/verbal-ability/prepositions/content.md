# Prepositions

## Concept

A **preposition** links a noun or pronoun to the rest of the sentence, showing relationships of **time** (*at* 5 p.m.), **place** (*on* the table), **direction** (*into* the room) and many fixed meanings (*interested **in***, *good **at***).

Prepositions are hard because many are decided by **convention**, not logic: we are *afraid **of*** something but *angry **with*** a person. Tests focus on three things: time and place prepositions, commonly confused pairs, and **fixed prepositions** that go with particular words.

## Rules

### Time: at, on, in

| Preposition | Used with | Examples |
|-------------|-----------|----------|
| at | Exact times, festivals as moments, "night" | at 5 p.m., at noon, at night, at Diwali |
| on | Days and dates | on Monday, on 15 August, on my birthday |
| in | Months, years, seasons, parts of the day, longer periods | in May, in 1998, in summer, in the morning |

### Time: since, for, by, until, during

- **since** + point of time (with perfect tenses): *since Monday, since 2015*.
- **for** + period: *for three days*.
- **by** = not later than: *Submit it **by** Friday.*
- **until/till** = up to that time: *Wait **until** six.*
- **during** = within a period: ***during** the meeting*.

### Place and direction

| Pair | Difference | Example |
|------|------------|---------|
| in / into | *in* = position; *into* = movement inside | She is **in** the room. She walked **into** the room. |
| on / onto | *on* = position on a surface; *onto* = movement to a surface | The cat jumped **onto** the table. |
| between / among | *between* = two (or clearly separate items); *among* = more than two | Divide it **between** the two. Divide it **among** the five. |
| beside / besides | *beside* = next to; *besides* = in addition to | Sit **beside** me. **Besides** Maths, he teaches Physics. |
| over / above | *over* = directly higher, often covering or moving across; *above* = higher than | a bridge **over** the river; the temperature **above** 40° |
| below / under | *under* = directly beneath, often covered; *below* = lower than | **under** the table; **below** sea level |
| at / in (place) | *at* = a point; *in* = an enclosed area or city | **at** the bus stop; **in** Mumbai |

### Fixed prepositions (common in tests)

| Word | Preposition | Word | Preposition |
|------|-------------|------|-------------|
| accused | of | interested | in |
| afraid | of | insist | on |
| agree | with (a person), to (a proposal) | married | to |
| angry | with (a person), at (a thing) | prefer | to |
| aware | of | prevent | from |
| capable | of | proud | of |
| comply | with | rely | on |
| congratulate | on | senior / junior / superior | to |
| consist | of | similar | to |
| depend | on | succeed | in |
| different | from | suffer | from |
| good | at | full | of |

### No preposition after these verbs

*discuss, enter, reach, marry, resemble, order, request, attack, accompany*:
"discuss **about** the plan" ✗ → "discuss the plan" ✓; "reached **to** Delhi" ✗ → "reached Delhi" ✓.

## Solving Approach

1. Decide the meaning type: time, place, direction or fixed expression.
2. For time/place, apply the at–on–in and pair rules above.
3. For fixed expressions, recall the word + preposition pair.
4. Check whether the verb needs a preposition at all.

## Common Mistakes

- "Since five years" (use *for*); "on the morning" (use *in*).
- "Senior than", "prefer than" (use *to*).
- "Discuss about", "reach to", "marry with".
- "Between" for more than two separate groups in a set, and "among" for two.

## Placement Tips

- Learn the fixed-preposition table — it covers most preposition questions.
- In error spotting, look for prepositions after *discuss, reach, senior, prefer, consist, comprise* — classic planted errors.
- *Comprise* takes no preposition (*The team comprises five members*); *consist* takes *of*.

## Key Takeaways

- at (time point), on (day/date), in (month/year/period).
- since + point, for + period; by = deadline, until = up to.
- Fixed pairs: different from, senior to, prefer to, interested in, insist on, accused of.
- No preposition after discuss, enter, reach, marry, resemble.
