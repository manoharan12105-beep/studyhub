# Prepositions — Practice

Choose the correct preposition. "No preposition" means the blank should be left empty.

### P1. The meeting is ___ Monday.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Time

- A) at
- B) in
- C) on
- D) by

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) on

**Explanation:** Days take "on".

</details>

### P2. She was born ___ 1998.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Time

- A) at
- B) in
- C) on
- D) since

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) in

**Explanation:** Years take "in".

</details>

### P3. Meet me ___ 5 p.m.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Time

- A) at
- B) in
- C) on
- D) during

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** A) at

**Explanation:** Exact clock times take "at".

</details>

### P4. He has been ill ___ last week.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Since / for

- A) for
- B) from
- C) since
- D) by

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) since

**Explanation:** "Last week" is a point of time and the tense is present perfect → "since".

</details>

### P5. Divide the sweets ___ the three children.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Between / among

- A) between
- B) among
- C) with
- D) along

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) among

**Explanation:** More than two people → "among".

</details>

### P6. She is very good ___ mathematics.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Fixed prepositions

- A) in
- B) at
- C) on
- D) with

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) at

**Explanation:** The fixed expression is "good at".

</details>

### P7. This book is different ___ that one.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Fixed prepositions

- A) by
- B) of
- C) from
- D) with

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) from

**Explanation:** The standard, safest form is "different from". ("Different than" and "different to" occur informally, which is why they are not offered as options here.)

</details>

### P8. We discussed ___ the problem for an hour.

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** No preposition

- A) about
- B) on
- C) over
- D) No preposition

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** D) No preposition

**Explanation:** "Discuss" takes a direct object: "We discussed the problem."

</details>

### P9. He was accused ___ theft.

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Fixed prepositions

- A) for
- B) with
- C) of
- D) about

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) of

**Explanation:** The fixed expression is "accused of" (compare "charged with").

</details>

### P10. I prefer tea ___ coffee.

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Fixed prepositions

- A) than
- B) to
- C) over than
- D) from

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) to

**Explanation:** "Prefer X to Y".

</details>

### P11. She insisted ___ paying the bill herself.

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Fixed prepositions

- A) for
- B) to
- C) on
- D) at

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) on

**Explanation:** The fixed expression is "insist on".

</details>

### P12. He is senior ___ me in the office.

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Fixed prepositions

- A) than
- B) to
- C) from
- D) over

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) to

**Explanation:** Latin comparatives (senior, junior, superior, inferior, prior) take "to", not "than".

</details>
