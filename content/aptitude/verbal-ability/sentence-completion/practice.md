# Sentence Completion — Practice

Choose the option that best completes the sentence.

### P1. Since it was raining heavily, ___.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Cause and result

- A) we went for a picnic
- B) the match was postponed
- C) the sun was shining brightly
- D) the ground was dry

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) the match was postponed

**Explanation:** "Since" introduces a reason; postponing the match is a natural result of heavy rain.

</details>

### P2. Although she studied hard, ___.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Contrast

- A) she passed with distinction
- B) she could not clear the exam
- C) she was a good student
- D) she studied every day

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) she could not clear the exam

**Explanation:** "Although" requires a contrast with the expected result of studying hard.

</details>

### P3. If you heat ice, ___.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Conditionals

- A) it melts
- B) it freezes
- C) it becomes harder
- D) it had melted

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** A) it melts

**Explanation:** A general truth (zero conditional): if + present, present.

</details>

### P4. Not only did he finish the project on time, ___.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Fixed pairs

- A) but he also stayed within the budget
- B) and he was late
- C) but also he within the budget
- D) he also not stayed within the budget

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** A) but he also stayed within the budget

**Explanation:** "Not only … but also" with a complete clause; B contradicts the first part; C has no verb; D is ungrammatical.

</details>

### P5. The roads were slippery; ___.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Consequence

- A) therefore, drivers were asked to slow down
- B) however, drivers were asked to slow down
- C) because drivers slowed down
- D) although drivers slowed down

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** A) therefore, drivers were asked to slow down

**Explanation:** Slowing down is a consequence of slippery roads → "therefore". "However" wrongly signals contrast.

</details>

### P6. She would have caught the train ___.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Conditionals

- A) if she had left earlier
- B) if she leaves earlier
- C) if she will leave earlier
- D) unless she left earlier

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** A) if she had left earlier

**Explanation:** "Would have + V3" pairs with "if + past perfect" (third conditional).

</details>

### P7. The company's profits fell this year ___.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Cause and result

- A) because demand for its products declined
- B) so demand for its products increased
- C) although its sales dropped sharply
- D) therefore its costs fell

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** A) because demand for its products declined

**Explanation:** Falling demand explains falling profits. C uses "although" for two ideas that agree, which is illogical.

</details>

### P8. Scarcely had the guests arrived ___.

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Fixed pairs

- A) than the power failed
- B) when the power failed
- C) then the power failed
- D) that the power failed

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) when the power failed

**Explanation:** The fixed pair is "scarcely … when".

</details>

### P9. The more you practise, ___.

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Comparative pattern

- A) the better you become
- B) better you become
- C) the best you become
- D) you become the better

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** A) the better you become

**Explanation:** The pattern is "the + comparative …, the + comparative …".

</details>

### P10. Despite being the favourite, ___.

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Contrast

- A) the team won easily
- B) the team lost the final
- C) the team was strong
- D) the team practised hard

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) the team lost the final

**Explanation:** "Despite" requires a contrast with the expected outcome of being the favourite.

</details>
