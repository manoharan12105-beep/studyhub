# Subject-Verb Agreement — Practice

Choose the correct verb.

### P1. The list of items ___ on the desk.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Words between subject and verb

- A) are
- B) is
- C) were
- D) have been

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) is

**Explanation:** The subject is "list" (singular), not "items".

</details>

### P2. Each of the students ___ a laptop.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Indefinite pronouns

- A) have
- B) has
- C) are having
- D) were having

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) has

**Explanation:** "Each" is singular.

</details>

### P3. My brother and my sister ___ in Pune.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Subjects joined by "and"

- A) lives
- B) live
- C) is living
- D) has lived

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) live

**Explanation:** Two subjects joined by "and" take a plural verb.

</details>

### P4. Neither the teacher nor the students ___ ready.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Or / nor

- A) was
- B) is
- C) were
- D) has been

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) were

**Explanation:** With "neither…nor", the verb agrees with the nearer subject, "students" (plural).

</details>

### P5. The news ___ good.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Plural form, singular meaning

- A) are
- B) is
- C) were
- D) have been

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) is

**Explanation:** "News" is singular in meaning.

</details>

### P6. A number of students ___ absent today.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** A number of / the number of

- A) is
- B) was
- C) are
- D) has been

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) are

**Explanation:** "A number of" means "many" and takes a plural verb.

</details>

### P7. The number of road accidents ___ increasing every year.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** A number of / the number of

- A) is
- B) are
- C) were
- D) have been

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** A) is

**Explanation:** "The number" (the count) is singular.

</details>

### P8. The captain, along with his players, ___ arrived.

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Along with / as well as

- A) have
- B) has
- C) are
- D) were

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) has

**Explanation:** "Along with his players" does not change the subject "captain" (singular).

</details>

### P9. Many a student ___ failed to answer this question.

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Many a

- A) have
- B) has
- C) are
- D) were

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) has

**Explanation:** "Many a" is followed by a singular noun and a singular verb.

</details>

### P10. She is one of those singers who ___ never missed a concert.

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** One of those who

- A) has
- B) have
- C) is
- D) was

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) have

**Explanation:** "Who" refers to "singers" (plural), so the verb in the relative clause is plural.

</details>

### P11. Ten kilometres ___ a long distance to walk.

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Amounts as a unit

- A) are
- B) is
- C) were
- D) have been

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) is

**Explanation:** A distance treated as one amount takes a singular verb.

</details>

### P12. The furniture in these rooms ___ very old.

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Uncountable nouns

- A) are
- B) is
- C) were
- D) have been

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) is

**Explanation:** "Furniture" is uncountable and singular; "these rooms" is inside a phrase and does not affect the verb.

</details>
