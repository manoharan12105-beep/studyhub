# Conjunctions — Practice

### P1. She was very tired, ___ she finished the work.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Choosing by logic

- A) so
- B) because
- C) but
- D) or

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) but

**Explanation:** Finishing the work despite being tired is a contrast.

</details>

### P2. I stayed at home ___ it was raining.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Choosing by logic

- A) because
- B) although
- C) unless
- D) but

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** A) because

**Explanation:** The rain is the reason (cause).

</details>

### P3. Hurry up, ___ you will miss the bus.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Choosing by logic

- A) and
- B) or
- C) but
- D) so

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) or

**Explanation:** "Or" here means "otherwise" — a warning about the alternative.

</details>

### P7. She is not only intelligent ___ hard-working.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Fixed pairs

- A) and also
- B) but also
- C) as well
- D) so also

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) but also

</details>

### P8. Work hard ___ you should fail.

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Fixed pairs

- A) unless
- B) lest
- C) until
- D) so that

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) lest

**Explanation:** "Lest" means "so that … not" and pairs with "should": work hard so that you do not fail. "So that you should fail" would reverse the meaning.

</details>

### P9. Which sentence is incorrect?

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Doubled conjunctions

- A) Although he tried hard, he failed.
- B) Although he tried hard, but he failed.
- C) He tried hard but failed.
- D) Despite trying hard, he failed.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) Although he tried hard, but he failed.

**Explanation:** "Although" and "but" both mark the same contrast; using both is an error.

</details>

### P10. You will not pass ___ you work hard.

- A) unless
- B) because
- C) lest
- D) if

<details>

</details>
