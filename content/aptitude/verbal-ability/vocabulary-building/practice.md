# Vocabulary Building — Practice

### P1. The root "aud" in "audible" means:

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Roots

- A) see
- B) hear
- C) speak
- D) write

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) hear

**Explanation:** Audible = able to be heard (audience, audio).

</details>

### P2. The rising cost of fuel will ___ the price of vegetables.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Confused words

- A) affect
- B) effect
- C) affection
- D) effective

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** A) affect

**Explanation:** A verb meaning "influence" is needed → affect. "Effect" is normally a noun (a result).

</details>

### P3. She bought pens, notebooks and other ___ for the new term.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Confused words

- A) stationary
- B) stationery
- C) station
- D) stationed

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) stationery

**Explanation:** Stationery = writing materials; stationary = not moving.

</details>

### P4. Using the roots, "incredible" most nearly means:

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Roots

- A) Not believable
- B) Not visible
- C) Very kind
- D) Not audible

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** A) Not believable

**Explanation:** in- (not) + cred (believe) + -ible (can be).

</details>

### P5. The ___ of the school addressed the students at the assembly.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Confused words

- A) principle
- B) principal
- C) principled
- D) principally

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) principal

**Explanation:** Principal = head of a school; principle = a rule or belief.

</details>

### P6. Which word means "to send across or pass on"?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Roots

- A) Transmit
- B) Transport
- C) Translate
- D) Transform

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** A) Transmit

**Explanation:** trans- (across) + mit (send). Transport is to carry across; transform is to change form.

</details>

### P7. The economist warned that a recession was ___.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Confused words

- A) eminent
- B) imminent
- C) prominent
- D) dominant

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) imminent

**Explanation:** Imminent = about to happen. Eminent = distinguished.

</details>

### P8. From her silence, I ___ that she was unhappy with the decision.

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Confused words

- A) implied
- B) inferred
- C) insured
- D) ensured

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) inferred

**Explanation:** The listener draws a conclusion → infer. The speaker suggests → imply.

</details>

### P9. The machine made a ___ humming noise all night without a single break.

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Confused words

- A) continual
- B) continuous
- C) contiguous
- D) continued

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) continuous

**Explanation:** "Without a single break" → continuous. Continual means repeated with interruptions.

</details>

### P10. Buying in bulk is more ___ for a large family.

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Confused words

- A) economic
- B) economical
- C) economics
- D) economy

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) economical

**Explanation:** Economical = saving money, not wasteful. Economic = relating to the economy.

</details>

### P11. Please ___ the files on my desk before you leave.

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Confused words

- A) lie
- B) lay
- C) lain
- D) laid

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) lay

**Explanation:** "Lay" means to put something down and takes an object (the files). "Lie" (recline) takes no object; "laid" is past tense, which does not fit "please … before you leave".

</details>
