# Active and Passive Voice — Practice

Choose the correct passive (or active) form.

### P1. The cat chased the mouse.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Simple past

- A) The mouse is chased by the cat.
- B) The mouse was chased by the cat.
- C) The mouse was being chased by the cat.
- D) The mouse had been chased by the cat.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) The mouse was chased by the cat.

**Explanation:** Simple past (chased) → was + V3.

</details>

### P2. She writes a letter.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Simple present

- A) A letter is written by her.
- B) A letter was written by her.
- C) A letter is written by she.
- D) A letter has been written by her.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** A) A letter is written by her.

**Explanation:** Simple present → is + V3; "by" takes the object pronoun "her".

</details>

### P3. They are building a bridge.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Present continuous

- A) A bridge is built by them.
- B) A bridge is being built by them.
- C) A bridge was being built by them.
- D) A bridge has been built by them.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) A bridge is being built by them.

**Explanation:** Present continuous → is being + V3.

</details>

### P4. He has finished the work.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Present perfect

- A) The work was finished by him.
- B) The work has been finished by him.
- C) The work had been finished by him.
- D) The work is being finished by him.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) The work has been finished by him.

**Explanation:** Present perfect → has been + V3.

</details>

### P5. Who wrote this poem?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Questions

- A) By whom was this poem written?
- B) Who was this poem written?
- C) By whom this poem was written?
- D) By whom is this poem written?

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** A) By whom was this poem written?

**Explanation:** "Who" becomes "by whom"; simple past → was + V3; question word order puts "was" before the subject.

</details>

### P6. Close the door.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Commands

- A) The door is closed.
- B) Let the door be closed.
- C) The door should closed.
- D) Let the door closed.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) Let the door be closed.

**Explanation:** Commands → Let + object + be + V3.

</details>

### P7. You must obey the rules.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Modals

- A) The rules must obeyed by you.
- B) The rules must be obeyed by you.
- C) The rules must have obeyed by you.
- D) The rules must being obeyed by you.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) The rules must be obeyed by you.

**Explanation:** Modal → modal + be + V3.

</details>

### P8. The teacher gave the students a test.

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Two objects

- A) The students were given a test by the teacher.
- B) The students are given a test by the teacher.
- C) The students were gave a test by the teacher.
- D) The students had given a test by the teacher.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** A) The students were given a test by the teacher.

**Explanation:** Either object can become the subject; simple past → were + V3 (given). "A test was given to the students by the teacher" would also be correct but is not offered.

</details>

### P9. They will have completed the project by June.

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Future perfect

- A) The project will be completed by them by June.
- B) The project will have been completed by them by June.
- C) The project would have been completed by them by June.
- D) The project will have completed by them by June.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) The project will have been completed by them by June.

**Explanation:** Future perfect → will have been + V3.

</details>

### P10. Someone has stolen my bag.

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Vague agents

- A) My bag was stolen by someone.
- B) My bag has been stolen.
- C) My bag had been stolen.
- D) My bag is stolen by someone.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) My bag has been stolen.

**Explanation:** Present perfect → has been + V3; the vague agent "someone" is dropped. Option A changes the tense.

</details>

### P11. People say that he is honest.

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Reporting verbs

- A) He is said to be honest.
- B) He was said to be honest.
- C) He is saying to be honest.
- D) It said that he is honest.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** A) He is said to be honest.

**Explanation:** "People say that…" → "He is said to be…" (or "It is said that he is honest"). Tense stays present.

</details>
