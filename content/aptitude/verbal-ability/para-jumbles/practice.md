# Para Jumbles — Practice

Arrange the sentences to form a coherent paragraph.

### P1. Arrange: P. She packed her bag and left for the station. Q. Riya woke up early on the day of her interview. R. After a quick breakfast, she revised her notes. S. She reached the office half an hour before time.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Time sequence

- A) QRPS
- B) QPRS
- C) RQPS
- D) QRSP

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** A) QRPS

**Explanation:** Q introduces Riya; the morning proceeds in order: breakfast and revision (R), leaving (P), arriving (S).

</details>

### P2. Arrange: P. Then add the tea leaves and let them boil for a minute. Q. First, boil a cup of water in a pan. R. Finally, strain the tea into a cup and serve. S. Once the leaves have coloured the water, pour in the milk and sugar.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Time sequence

- A) QSPR
- B) QPSR
- C) PQSR
- D) QPRS

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) QPSR

**Explanation:** First (Q) → Then (P) → "Once the leaves have coloured the water" needs the leaves already added (S after P) → Finally (R).

</details>

### P3. Arrange: P. Work on it began soon after, and it took about twenty years and thousands of workers to complete. Q. The Taj Mahal is one of the most famous monuments in the world. R. Today, it attracts millions of visitors every year. S. The emperor Shah Jahan commissioned it in memory of his wife.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Pronoun links

- A) QPSR
- B) QSPR
- C) SQPR
- D) QSRP

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) QSPR

**Explanation:** Q names the Taj Mahal (opening). "Work on it began soon after" in P needs an earlier event — the commissioning in S — so S–P is a mandatory pair. R ("Today") ends the history.

</details>

### P4. Arrange: P. For example, a ten-minute walk after meals is one such change, and it can help digestion. Q. Regular physical activity has many benefits for health. R. Even small changes in daily routine can make a difference. S. Therefore, experts recommend staying active throughout the day.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Example links

- A) QPRS
- B) QRPS
- C) RQPS
- D) QRSP

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) QRPS

**Explanation:** "One such change" in P refers back to the "small changes" in R, so R–P is a mandatory pair. Q opens; S ("Therefore") concludes.

</details>

### P5. Arrange: P. The organisation was founded in 1945, after the Second World War. Q. The United Nations (UN) is an international organisation of member states. R. Since then, its main aim has been to maintain international peace and security. S. Today, the UN has 193 member states.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Full form first

- A) QRPS
- B) QPRS
- C) PQRS
- D) QPSR

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) QPRS

**Explanation:** Q gives the full name (opening). "Since then" in R refers to the founding in P → P–R. S ("Today") ends.

</details>

### P6. Arrange: P. As a result, many farmers have started using drip irrigation. Q. Water scarcity is a growing problem in many parts of India. R. This method delivers water directly to the roots of plants. S. Traditional flood irrigation wastes a large amount of water. T. It can reduce water use considerably while improving crop yields.

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Pronoun and result links

- A) QSPRT
- B) QPSRT
- C) SQPRT
- D) QSRPT

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** A) QSPRT

**Explanation:** Q states the problem; S gives the cause of waste; P ("As a result") follows S; R ("This method" = drip irrigation) follows P; T ("It") follows R.

</details>

### P7. The first sentence is given. Arrange the rest. Many people believe that success depends only on talent. P. Hard work turns that potential into real achievement. Q. However, talent alone is rarely enough. R. It gives a person potential, but nothing more. S. That is why the most successful people are often the most persistent.

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Contrast and pronoun links

- A) QPRS
- B) QRPS
- C) RQPS
- D) QRSP

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) QRPS

**Explanation:** Q ("However") contrasts with the given belief. R ("It" = talent) explains why; P picks up "that potential" from R; S concludes.

</details>

### P8. Arrange: P. Within minutes, the message had reached thousands of people. Q. Someone posted a photograph of the flooded street on social media. R. Heavy rain had flooded the main street of the town overnight. S. By noon, the municipal team had arrived to clear the drains.

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Time sequence

- A) RQPS
- B) QRPS
- C) RPQS
- D) RQSP

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** A) RQPS

**Explanation:** R sets the scene (flood). Q (a photograph is posted) must come before P ("the message" spreads). S ("By noon") comes last.

</details>
