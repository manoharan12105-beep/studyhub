# Synonyms — Practice

Choose the word closest in meaning to the word in capitals.

### P1. ABUNDANT

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** High-frequency words

- A) Scarce
- B) Plentiful
- C) Rare
- D) Empty

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) Plentiful

**Explanation:** Abundant means existing in large quantities. Scarce and rare are antonyms.

</details>

### P2. DILIGENT

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** High-frequency words

- A) Lazy
- B) Careless
- C) Hardworking
- D) Slow

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) Hardworking

**Explanation:** Diligent means showing care and effort in work.

</details>

### P3. TRANQUIL

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** High-frequency words

- A) Noisy
- B) Peaceful
- C) Busy
- D) Angry

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) Peaceful

**Explanation:** Tranquil means calm and free from disturbance.

</details>

### P4. CANDID

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** High-frequency words

- A) Secretive
- B) Frank
- C) Rude
- D) Shy

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) Frank

**Explanation:** Candid means honest and open. "Rude" is a trap — candour is not necessarily impolite.

</details>

### P5. FRUGAL

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** High-frequency words

- A) Wasteful
- B) Generous
- C) Thrifty
- D) Greedy

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) Thrifty

**Explanation:** Frugal means careful with money and resources. Wasteful is the antonym.

</details>

### P6. OBSTINATE

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** High-frequency words

- A) Flexible
- B) Stubborn
- C) Obedient
- D) Gentle

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) Stubborn

**Explanation:** Obstinate means refusing to change one's opinion.

</details>

### P7. LUCID

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** High-frequency words

- A) Vague
- B) Clear
- C) Dark
- D) Complex

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) Clear

**Explanation:** Lucid means expressed clearly and easy to understand. Vague is the opposite.

</details>

### P8. GREGARIOUS

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** High-frequency words

- A) Shy
- B) Sociable
- C) Aggressive
- D) Quiet

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) Sociable

**Explanation:** Gregarious means fond of company. Root *greg-* = flock, group.

</details>

### P9. IMPECCABLE

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Roots and prefixes

- A) Faulty
- B) Flawless
- C) Average
- D) Careless

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) Flawless

**Explanation:** *Im-* (not) + *peccare* (to sin, err) → without fault.

</details>

### P10. SCRUTINIZE

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** High-frequency words

- A) Ignore
- B) Examine closely
- C) Praise
- D) Approve

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) Examine closely

**Explanation:** To scrutinize is to inspect thoroughly.

</details>

### P11. EPHEMERAL

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Roots and prefixes

- A) Eternal
- B) Short-lived
- C) Ancient
- D) Important

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) Short-lived

**Explanation:** Ephemeral means lasting a very short time. Eternal is the antonym.

</details>
