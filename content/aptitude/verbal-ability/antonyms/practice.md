# Antonyms — Practice

Choose the word most nearly opposite in meaning to the word in capitals.

### P1. ANCIENT

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** High-frequency words

- A) Old
- B) Modern
- C) Historic
- D) Antique

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) Modern

**Explanation:** Ancient means very old; the others are synonyms or near-synonyms.

</details>

### P2. ACCEPT

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** High-frequency words

- A) Receive
- B) Reject
- C) Admit
- D) Welcome

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) Reject

**Explanation:** To accept is to agree to take; to reject is to refuse.

</details>

### P3. EXPAND

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** High-frequency words

- A) Enlarge
- B) Grow
- C) Contract
- D) Extend

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) Contract

**Explanation:** Expand means become larger; contract means become smaller.

</details>

### P4. HUMBLE

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** High-frequency words

- A) Modest
- B) Arrogant
- C) Polite
- D) Simple

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) Arrogant

**Explanation:** Humble means modest; arrogant means having an exaggerated sense of one's importance. "Modest" is a synonym trap.

</details>

### P5. TRANSPARENT

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** High-frequency words

- A) Clear
- B) Opaque
- C) Bright
- D) Thin

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) Opaque

**Explanation:** Transparent lets light through; opaque does not.

</details>

### P6. PERMANENT

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** High-frequency words

- A) Lasting
- B) Stable
- C) Temporary
- D) Fixed

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) Temporary

**Explanation:** Permanent lasts indefinitely; temporary lasts a limited time.

</details>

### P7. VERBOSE

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** High-frequency words

- A) Wordy
- B) Lengthy
- C) Concise
- D) Talkative

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) Concise

**Explanation:** Verbose means using too many words; concise means brief and to the point.

</details>

### P8. BENEVOLENT

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Roots and prefixes

- A) Kind
- B) Generous
- C) Malevolent
- D) Charitable

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) Malevolent

**Explanation:** *Bene-* (good) vs *male-* (bad): benevolent = well-meaning; malevolent = wishing harm.

</details>

### P9. ZENITH

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** High-frequency words

- A) Peak
- B) Summit
- C) Nadir
- D) Height

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) Nadir

**Explanation:** Zenith is the highest point; nadir is the lowest point.

</details>

### P10. PRUDENT

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** High-frequency words

- A) Wise
- B) Careful
- C) Rash
- D) Sensible

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) Rash

**Explanation:** Prudent means acting with care and thought; rash means acting without thinking.

</details>

### P11. AUGMENT

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** High-frequency words

- A) Increase
- B) Diminish
- C) Enlarge
- D) Supplement

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) Diminish

**Explanation:** Augment means increase; diminish means decrease.

</details>
