# Concepts

The ideas you must have clear in your head before the test. One line each.

## Quantitative

- **Percentages:** always ask "percent of what?" — the base follows *of* or *than*.
- **Changes multiply:** +20% then −20% is −4%, not 0.
- **Profit/loss on CP; discount on MP.**
- **Ratios:** a : b means ax and bx — solve for x.
- **Averages:** convert to totals first.
- **Ages:** the age difference never changes.
- **SI is linear; CI compounds** (same in year 1).
- **Work:** rates add, times don't.
- **Speed:** opposite directions add; same direction subtract.
- **Trains** cover their own length.
- **P&C:** order matters → P; doesn't → C. AND multiplies, OR adds.
- **Probability:** at least one = 1 − none; without replacement changes the next probability.
- **Inequalities:** multiplying by a negative flips the sign.
- **Divisibility:** test composites with co-prime factors (12 = 3 × 4).
- **Unit digit:** depends only on last digits; cycles of 4.

## Logical

- **Series:** differences → ratios → squares/cubes → alternate terms.
- **Blood relations:** draw the tree; gender only from the question's words, never from names.
- **Directions:** right = clockwise from the current facing. Morning shadows west, evening shadows east.
- **Syllogisms:** a conclusion must hold in every diagram; "some" = at least one, possibly all.
- **Either–or:** neither follows alone, but one of a complementary pair must.
- **Conclusion** = forced by the statement. **Assumption** = taken for granted (negation test).
- **Cause** comes first and explains the effect; similar simultaneous events → common cause.
- **Seating facing centre:** right = anticlockwise, left = clockwise.
- **Data sufficiency:** I alone, II alone, then together; sufficient = one answer.
- **Clocks:** hands meet 22 times a day, not 24.
- **Calendars:** centuries are leap only if divisible by 400.

## Verbal

- **Agreement:** find the real subject; *each, every, neither, the number of, news, furniture* → singular.
- **Tense:** *yesterday/ago* → simple past; *since* + point, *for* + period.
- **Future time clause** uses the present: "when he **comes**".
- **Conditionals:** if + present → will; if + past → would; if + had → would have.
- **Articles by sound:** an hour, a university, an MBA.
- **Prepositions:** senior **to**, prefer **to**, different **from**; no preposition after *discuss, reach*.
- **Pronouns:** between you and **me**; let you and **me**; *its* for a company.
- **Pairs:** no sooner … **than**; hardly … **when**; not only … **but also**.
- **Passive:** same tense; *being* for continuous, *been* for perfect.
- **Reported speech:** backshift after a past reporting verb; universal truths stay present.
- **RC:** answer from the passage only.
