# Important Shortcuts

Speed techniques that save the most time, with the condition under which each is safe. Every shortcut is explained in full in its topic.

## Calculation

| Shortcut | How | Safe when |
|----------|-----|-----------|
| Fraction equivalents | 12.5% = 1/8, 16.67% = 1/6, 37.5% = 3/8, 62.5% = 5/8, 83.33% = 5/6 | Percentage matches a table fraction |
| 10% and 1% building blocks | 37% of 850 = 3 × 85 + 7 × 8.5 | Any percentage, mental work |
| x% of y = y% of x | 16% of 25 = 25% of 16 = 4 | One side is easier |
| × 5, 25, 125 | × 10 ÷ 2; × 100 ÷ 4; × 1000 ÷ 8 | One factor is 5, 25 or 125 |
| ab × 11 | a \| a + b \| b (carry) | Two-digit × 11 |
| n5² | n(n + 1) then 25 | Number ends in 5 |
| Near-base product | 97 × 96 = (97 − 4) \| 3 × 4 = 9312 | Both near 100 or 1000 |
| Symmetric pair | 47 × 53 = 50² − 3² | Round midpoint |
| Square roots | last digit → two candidates; leading digits → tens; compare with (tens)5² | Perfect squares only |
| Digit-sum check | reduce to single digits; compare with the answer's digit sum | Rejecting options (cannot confirm) |
| Approximation | round, compute, pick nearest option | Options well separated |

## Number System

- **Unit digit:** last digit's cycle at position n mod 4 (0 → 4th term). 4 and 9: odd power → 4/9, even → 6/1.
- **Remainder of a power:** find where the remainder cycle returns to 1 (Fermat for prime divisors: aᵖ⁻¹ ≡ 1).
- **Negative remainders:** 98 × 99 mod 100 = (−2)(−1) = 2.
- **Same remainder:** HCF of differences. **Divisor − remainder constant:** LCM − k.

## Percentages and Profit

- **Multiplying factors:** +r% → ×(1 + r/100); undo by dividing.
- **Successive change:** a + b + ab/100 (for discounts: d₁ + d₂ − d₁d₂/100).
- **Base 100:** with no actual numbers, take the starting value as 100.
- **Same SP, x% gain and x% loss:** x²/100 % loss — no calculation.
- **Required markup:** MP/CP = (100 + profit%)/(100 − discount%).

## Ratios, Averages, Mixtures

- **Parts method:** a : b → ax, bx.
- **Combine ratios:** equalise the common term with its LCM.
- **Coins:** convert count ratio to value ratio first.
- **Deviation method:** average = assumed mean + average deviation.
- **New member:** old average + new count × increase.
- **Alligation cross:** cheaper : dearer = (d − m) : (m − c). Also works for profit %, interest rates and group averages.

## Interest

- **SI doubling:** RT = 100(k − 1).
- **CI two years at 10%:** 21%; at 20%: 44%; three years at 10%: 33.1%.
- **CI − SI (2 years):** P(R/100)².
- **Growth multiples:** k times in n years → k² times in 2n years.

## Work and Motion

- **LCM method:** total work = LCM of times; rates become integers.
- **Two together:** ab/(a + b).
- **Leak time:** ab/(b − a).
- **km/h ↔ m/s:** × 5/18; multiples of 18 km/h convert cleanly (36 → 10, 54 → 15, 72 → 20, 90 → 25).
- **Equal-distance average speed:** 2xy/(x + y).
- **Race margins combine multiplicatively:** 100 → 90 → 81, not 80.

## Counting and Probability

- **Glue** items that must be together; **gaps** for items that must be apart.
- **At least one:** total − none.
- **Two dice:** sum 7 has 6 ways; sums n and 14 − n have the same number of ways.

## Reasoning

- **EJOTY** for letter positions; **opposite letters** add to 27; k-th from the right = (27 − k)-th from the left.
- **Ranking:** total = L + R − 1. After a swap, A's new position = B's old position.
- **Directions:** track net (x, y); 3-4-5 and 6-8-10 triangles are common.
- **Clocks:** angle = |30H − 5.5M|; times with angle θ: M = (2/11)(30H ± θ); mirror = 11:60 − time.
- **Calendars:** century odd days 5, 3, 1, 0; month odd days 3, 0/1, 3, 2, 3, 2, 3, 3, 2, 3, 2, 3. Use a reference date when one is given.
- **Syllogisms:** memorise the combination table (All + All, All + No, Some + All, Some + No give definite results; All + Some, Some + Some give none).
- **Data sufficiency:** decide "needed information" before reading the statements; never calculate the final value.

## Verbal

- **Error spotting order:** agreement → tense → pronoun → article → preposition → adjective/adverb → comparison → nouns → conjunction pairs → redundancy.
- **Remove-the-other-person test** for pronoun case: "Ravi and (I/me) went" → "I went".
- **he/him test** for who/whom.
- **Clue words** in blanks: despite/although → contrast; because/so → cause; so … that → degree.
- **Para jumbles:** opening sentence + one mandatory pair usually leaves one option.
- **RC:** read questions' types first; scan for detail questions instead of re-reading everything.
