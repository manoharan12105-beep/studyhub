# Ratio and Proportion — Practice

### P1. Express 0.75 : 1.25 in its simplest form.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Simplifying ratios

- A) 3 : 4
- B) 3 : 5
- C) 5 : 3
- D) 15 : 4

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) 3 : 5

**Explanation:** Multiply by 100: 75 : 125. Divide by 25: 3 : 5.

</details>

### P2. ₹960 is divided between two people in the ratio 5 : 7. What is the larger share?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Dividing in a ratio

- A) ₹400
- B) ₹480
- C) ₹560
- D) ₹672

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) ₹560

**Explanation:** One part = 960/12 = 80. Larger share = 7 × 80 = ₹560.

</details>

### P3. Find the fourth proportional to 3, 5 and 12.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Proportionals

- A) 7.2
- B) 15
- C) 20
- D) 36

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) 20

**Explanation:** 3 : 5 = 12 : x → x = (5 × 12)/3 = 20.

</details>

### P4. If A : B = 3 : 4 and B : C = 6 : 7, find A : B : C.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Combining ratios

- A) 3 : 4 : 7
- B) 9 : 12 : 14
- C) 18 : 24 : 28
- D) 3 : 6 : 7

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) 9 : 12 : 14

**Explanation:** LCM of B's terms (4 and 6) is 12. A : B = 9 : 12; B : C = 12 : 14 → 9 : 12 : 14. Option C is the same ratio unsimplified — always reduce.

</details>

### P5. Find the mean proportional between 8 and 18.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Proportionals

- A) 12
- B) 13
- C) 40.5
- D) 144

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** A) 12

**Explanation:** √(8 × 18) = √144 = 12. Option C is the third proportional (18²/8); option D forgets the square root.

</details>

### P6. Two numbers are in the ratio 3 : 5. If 9 is subtracted from each, the ratio becomes 12 : 23. Find the smaller number.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Change in a ratio

- A) 27
- B) 33
- C) 36
- D) 55

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) 33

**Explanation:** (3x − 9)/(5x − 9) = 12/23 → 69x − 207 = 60x − 108 → 9x = 99 → x = 11. Numbers 33 and 55; check 24 : 46 = 12 : 23 ✓.

</details>

### P7. What is the sub-duplicate ratio of 49 : 64?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Types of ratio

- A) 7 : 8
- B) 49 : 64
- C) 2401 : 4096
- D) 64 : 49

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** A) 7 : 8

**Explanation:** Sub-duplicate means square roots: √49 : √64 = 7 : 8. Option C is the duplicate ratio (squares).

</details>

### P8. ₹1,400 is divided among A, B and C so that A gets half of what B gets, and B gets half of what C gets. What is C's share?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Dividing in a ratio

- A) ₹400
- B) ₹600
- C) ₹700
- D) ₹800

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** D) ₹800

**Explanation:** Let A = 1, then B = 2 and C = 4 → ratio 1 : 2 : 4 (7 parts). One part = 200 → C = ₹800.

</details>

### P9. A box contains ₹1, 50 paise and 25 paise coins in the ratio 2 : 3 : 4 by number. If the total value is ₹180, how many 25 paise coins are there?

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Count ratio vs value ratio

- A) 40
- B) 80
- C) 120
- D) 160

<details>
<summary>Hint</summary>

Convert the count ratio into a value ratio first.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** D) 160

**Explanation:** Value ratio = 2 × 1 : 3 × 0.5 : 4 × 0.25 = 2 : 1.5 : 1 = 4 : 3 : 2. One part = 180/9 = 20 → 25 paise coins are worth ₹40 → 40/0.25 = 160 coins. Option A is their value, not their count.

</details>

### P10. The incomes of A and B are in the ratio 5 : 4 and their expenditures in the ratio 3 : 2. If each saves ₹8,000, what is A's income?

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Income, expenditure and savings

- A) ₹16,000
- B) ₹20,000
- C) ₹24,000
- D) ₹40,000

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) ₹20,000

**Explanation:** 5x − 3y = 8000 and 4x − 2y = 8000. Multiply the first by 2 and the second by 3: 10x − 6y = 16000 and 12x − 6y = 24000 → 2x = 8000 → x = 4000. A's income = 5 × 4000 = ₹20,000. Check: y = 4000; A spends 12,000, B earns 16,000 and spends 8,000 — both save 8,000 ✓.

</details>

### P11. If (a + b) : (b + c) : (c + a) = 6 : 7 : 8 and a + b + c = 14, find c.

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Sum of pairs

- A) 4
- B) 5
- C) 6
- D) 7

<details>
<summary>Hint</summary>

Add the three pair sums.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) 6

**Explanation:** (a + b) + (b + c) + (c + a) = 2 × 14 = 28 = (6 + 7 + 8)k = 21k → k = 4/3. a + b = 6k = 8 → c = 14 − 8 = 6.

</details>

### P12. If x : y = 3 : 4, find (4x + 5y) : (5x − 2y).

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Expression in a ratio

- A) 32 : 7
- B) 7 : 32
- C) 16 : 7
- D) 32 : 23

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** A) 32 : 7

**Explanation:** Put x = 3, y = 4: (12 + 20) : (15 − 8) = 32 : 7.

</details>
