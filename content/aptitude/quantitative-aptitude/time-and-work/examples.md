# Time and Work — Solved Examples

### E1. Working together

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Work Efficiency

**Problem:** A can finish a job in 10 days and B in 15 days. How long will they take together?

**Solution:**

1. Total work = LCM(10, 15) = 30 units.
2. A = 3 units/day, B = 2 units/day → together 5 units/day.
3. Time = 30/5 = 6 days.

**Answer:** **6 days**

### E2. One person's time from a pair

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Work Efficiency

**Problem:** A and B together finish a job in 12 days. A alone takes 20 days. How long does B take alone?

**Solution:**

1. Total work = LCM(12, 20) = 60 units.
2. A + B = 5 units/day; A = 3 units/day → B = 2 units/day.
3. B alone = 60/2 = 30 days.

**Answer:** **30 days**

### E3. Efficiency ratio

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Work Efficiency

**Problem:** A is twice as efficient as B. Together they finish a job in 14 days. How long would A take alone?

**Solution:**

1. Efficiencies A : B = 2 : 1 → take rates 2 and 1 units/day.
2. Total work = (2 + 1) × 14 = 42 units.
3. A alone = 42/2 = 21 days.

**Answer:** **21 days**

### E4. Man-days with hours

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Man-days

**Problem:** 15 men working 8 hours a day complete a job in 20 days. How many days will 20 men working 6 hours a day take to complete the same job?

**Solution:**

1. M₁D₁H₁ = M₂D₂H₂ (same work).
2. 15 × 20 × 8 = 20 × D × 6 → 2,400 = 120D → D = 20.

**Answer:** **20 days**

### E5. Two inlets and an outlet

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Pipes and Cisterns

**Problem:** Pipe A fills a tank in 20 minutes, pipe B in 30 minutes, and pipe C empties it in 15 minutes. If all three are opened together on an empty tank, how long will it take to fill?

**Solution:**

1. Tank = LCM(20, 30, 15) = 60 units.
2. A = +3, B = +2, C = −4 → net +1 unit/min.
3. Time = 60 minutes.

**Answer:** **60 minutes**

### E6. Wages with a helper

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Work and Wages

**Problem:** A can do a job in 6 days and B in 8 days. With the help of C, they finish it in 3 days and are paid ₹3,200. How should the money be divided?

**Solution:**

1. Total work = 24. A = 4/day, B = 3/day.
2. All three: 24/3 = 8/day → C = 8 − 7 = 1/day.
3. Work done in 3 days: A 12, B 9, C 3 → ratio 4 : 3 : 1.
4. Shares: 3,200 × 4/8 = 1,600; × 3/8 = 1,200; × 1/8 = 400.

**Answer:** A **₹1,600**, B **₹1,200**, C **₹400**

### E7. Someone leaves midway

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Work Efficiency

**Problem:** A can do a job in 12 days and B in 18 days. They work together for 4 days, then A leaves. How many more days will B take to finish?

**Solution:**

1. Total work = LCM(12, 18) = 36. A = 3/day, B = 2/day.
2. 4 days together = 4 × 5 = 20 units. Remaining = 16.
3. B alone: 16/2 = 8 days.

**Answer:** **8 more days** (12 days in total)

### E8. Alternate days

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Work Efficiency

**Problem:** A can do a job in 8 days and B in 12 days. They work on alternate days, starting with A. In how many days will the job be finished?

**Solution:**

1. Total work = 24. A = 3/day, B = 2/day. One 2-day cycle = 5 units.
2. 4 cycles (8 days) = 20 units; 4 units left.
3. Day 9 (A): 3 units → 1 unit left.
4. Day 10 (B): 1 unit at 2/day → half a day.
5. Total = 9 + 0.5 = 9.5 days.

**Answer:** **9½ days**

> [!WARNING]
> 24/5 × 2 = 9.6 days is wrong: it assumes the last cycle is shared at the average rate, but A works first.

### E9. Leak

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Pipes and Cisterns

**Problem:** A pipe can fill a tank in 6 hours. Because of a leak at the bottom, it takes 8 hours. How long would the leak take to empty the full tank?

**Solution:**

1. Tank = LCM(6, 8) = 24 units.
2. Pipe = 4 units/h; with leak, net = 3 units/h → leak = 1 unit/h.
3. Leak empties 24 units in 24 hours.

**Answer:** **24 hours** (formula: ab/(b − a) = 48/2)
