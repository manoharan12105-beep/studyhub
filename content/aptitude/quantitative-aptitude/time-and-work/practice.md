# Time and Work — Practice

### P1. A can complete a job in 12 days and B in 24 days. How long will they take working together?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Work Efficiency

- A) 6 days
- B) 8 days
- C) 9 days
- D) 18 days

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) 8 days

**Explanation:** (12 × 24)/(12 + 24) = 288/36 = 8. Option D averages the times.

</details>

### P2. 8 men can finish a job in 15 days. How many days will 12 men take?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Man-days

- A) 10
- B) 12
- C) 20
- D) 22.5

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** A) 10

**Explanation:** Work = 8 × 15 = 120 man-days → 120/12 = 10 days. Option D uses direct instead of inverse proportion.

</details>

### P3. Pipe A fills a tank in 12 hours and pipe B in 18 hours. How long do both pipes take together?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Pipes and Cisterns

- A) 6 h
- B) 7 h 12 min
- C) 7 h 30 min
- D) 15 h

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) 7 h 12 min

**Explanation:** Tank = 36 units; A = 3/h, B = 2/h → 36/5 = 7.2 h = 7 h 12 min. (0.2 h = 12 min, not 20 min.)

</details>

### P4. A is 50% more efficient than B. B alone can do a job in 30 days. How long will A and B take together?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Work Efficiency

- A) 10 days
- B) 12 days
- C) 15 days
- D) 18 days

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) 12 days

**Explanation:** Efficiency A : B = 3 : 2. Work = 2 × 30 = 60 units. Together 5 units/day → 12 days.

</details>

### P5. A and B can do a job in 10 days, B and C in 15 days, and A and C in 12 days. How long will A, B and C take together?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Work Efficiency

- A) 6 days
- B) 8 days
- C) 9 days
- D) 12⅓ days

<details>
<summary>Hint</summary>

Adding the three pair rates counts each person twice.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) 8 days

**Explanation:** Work = LCM(10, 15, 12) = 60. Pair rates: 6, 4, 5 → sum 15 = 2(A + B + C) → A + B + C = 7.5/day → 60/7.5 = 8 days.

</details>

### P6. A pipe can fill a tank in 4 hours. A leak can empty the full tank in 12 hours. If both are open, how long will the tank take to fill?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Pipes and Cisterns

- A) 3 h
- B) 5 h
- C) 6 h
- D) 8 h

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) 6 h

**Explanation:** Tank = 12 units. Pipe +3/h, leak −1/h → net 2/h → 6 hours. Option A adds the leak.

</details>

### P7. A can do a job in 10 days and B in 15 days. Working together, they earn ₹4,500. What is A's share?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Work and Wages

- A) ₹1,800
- B) ₹2,250
- C) ₹2,700
- D) ₹3,000

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) ₹2,700

**Explanation:** Efficiency ratio = 15 : 10 = 3 : 2 → A gets 3/5 × 4,500 = 2,700. Option A splits in the ratio of days (2 : 3), paying the slower worker more.

</details>

### P8. 20 men working 6 hours a day build a 120 m wall in 12 days. How many days will 24 men working 5 hours a day take to build a 200 m wall?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Man-days

- A) 15
- B) 18
- C) 20
- D) 24

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) 20

**Explanation:** (20 × 12 × 6)/120 = (24 × D × 5)/200 → 12 = 120D/200 → D = 20.

</details>

### P9. A alone can do a job in 20 days. A works for 5 days, and then B finishes the remaining work in 9 days. How long would B take alone to do the whole job?

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Work Efficiency

- A) 9 days
- B) 12 days
- C) 15 days
- D) 18 days

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) 12 days

**Explanation:** A does 5/20 = 1/4. B does the remaining 3/4 in 9 days → full job in 12 days.

</details>

### P10. Pipes A and B can fill a tank in 12 minutes and 16 minutes respectively. Both are opened together. After how many minutes should B be closed so that the tank is full in exactly 9 minutes?

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Pipes and Cisterns

- A) 3 min
- B) 4 min
- C) 5 min
- D) 6 min

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) 4 min

**Explanation:** A runs all 9 minutes: 9/12 = 3/4 of the tank. B must fill 1/4 → 16 × 1/4 = 4 minutes.

</details>

### P11. A can do a job in 12 days and B in 16 days. With the help of C, they finish it in 4 days. If the total payment is ₹4,800, what is C's share?

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Work and Wages

- A) ₹1,200
- B) ₹1,600
- C) ₹2,000
- D) ₹2,400

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) ₹2,000

**Explanation:** Work = 48; A = 4/day, B = 3/day. All three = 48/4 = 12/day → C = 5/day. In 4 days: A 16, B 12, C 20 → C's share = 20/48 × 4,800 = 2,000.

</details>

### P12. 4 men or 6 women can do a job in 12 days. How long will 2 men and 9 women take?

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Different worker types

- A) 4 days
- B) 6 days
- C) 8 days
- D) 9 days

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) 6 days

**Explanation:** 4 men = 6 women → 1 man = 1.5 women. Work = 6 × 12 = 72 woman-days. 2 men + 9 women = 3 + 9 = 12 women → 72/12 = 6 days.

</details>
