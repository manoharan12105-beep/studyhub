# Geometry — Practice

### P1. What is the supplement of 65°?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Lines and angles

- A) 25°
- B) 115°
- C) 125°
- D) 295°

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) 115°

**Explanation:** Supplementary angles sum to 180°: 180 − 65 = 115. Option A is the complement.

</details>

### P4. An exterior angle of a triangle is 110° and one of the interior opposite angles is 45°. Find the other interior opposite angle.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Triangles

- A) 35°
- B) 55°
- C) 65°
- D) 70°

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) 65°

**Explanation:** Exterior angle = sum of the interior opposite angles → 110 − 45 = 65. Option D is the adjacent interior angle (180 − 110).

</details>

### P5. Which set of lengths cannot form a triangle?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Triangle inequality

- A) 3, 4, 5
- B) 5, 5, 9
- C) 3, 4, 8
- D) 6, 7, 12

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) 3, 4, 8

**Explanation:** 3 + 4 = 7 < 8, so the two shorter sides cannot reach. Every other set has the two shorter sides summing to more than the longest.

</details>

### P8. A median of a triangle is 12 cm long. What is the distance from the vertex to the centroid along this median?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Special lines

- A) 4 cm
- B) 6 cm
- C) 8 cm
- D) 9 cm

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) 8 cm

**Explanation:** The centroid divides the median 2 : 1 from the vertex → 2/3 × 12 = 8 cm. Option A is the distance from the centroid to the side.

</details>

### P9. A chord of a circle of radius 13 cm is 5 cm from the centre. Find the length of the chord.

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Circles

- A) 12 cm
- B) 18 cm
- C) 24 cm
- D) 26 cm

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) 24 cm

**Explanation:** The perpendicular from the centre bisects the chord: half-chord = √(169 − 25) = 12 → chord = 24. Option A forgets to double.

</details>

### P10. Two tangents PA and PB are drawn from an external point P to a circle with centre O. If ∠APB = 70°, find ∠AOB.

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Circles

- A) 70°
- B) 100°
- C) 110°
- D) 140°

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) 110°

**Explanation:** In quadrilateral OAPB, ∠OAP = ∠OBP = 90° (tangent ⟂ radius). So ∠AOB = 360 − 90 − 90 − 70 = 110°.

</details>

### P11. The areas of two similar triangles are in the ratio 16 : 81. A side of the smaller triangle is 8 cm. Find the corresponding side of the larger triangle.

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Similar triangles

- A) 12 cm
- B) 18 cm
- C) 40.5 cm
- D) 81 cm

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) 18 cm

**Explanation:** Side ratio = √(16/81) = 4/9 → 8 × 9/4 = 18 cm. Option C uses the area ratio as the side ratio.

</details>

### P12. Find the area of the triangle with vertices (1, 1), (4, 1) and (4, 5).

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Coordinate geometry

- A) 5
- B) 6
- C) 10
- D) 12

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) 6

**Explanation:** Right angle at (4, 1): base 3, height 4 → ½ × 3 × 4 = 6. Formula: ½ |1(1 − 5) + 4(5 − 1) + 4(1 − 1)| = ½ |−4 + 16| = 6. Option D forgets the ½.

</details>

### P13. A triangle is inscribed in a circle with one side as the diameter, which is 10 cm. Another side is 6 cm. Find the third side.

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Circles

- A) 4 cm
- B) 7 cm
- C) 8 cm
- D) 11.66 cm

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) 8 cm

**Explanation:** The angle in a semicircle is 90°, so the diameter is the hypotenuse: √(100 − 36) = 8 cm (6-8-10).

</details>
