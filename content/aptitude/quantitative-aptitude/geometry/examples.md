# Geometry — Solved Examples

### E1. Angles in a ratio

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Angles in a ratio

**Problem:** The angles of a triangle are in the ratio 2 : 3 : 4. Find them.

**Solution:**

1. Total parts = 9; angle sum = 180° → one part = 20°.
2. Angles = 40°, 60°, 80°.

**Answer:** **40°, 60°, 80°**

### E2. Regular hexagon

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Polygons

**Problem:** Find each interior angle of a regular hexagon.

**Solution:**

1. Each exterior angle = 360°/6 = 60°.
2. Interior = 180° − 60° = 120°.
3. Check: sum = (6 − 2) × 180° = 720° = 6 × 120° ✓.

**Answer:** **120°**

### E3. Distance and midpoint

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Coordinate geometry

**Problem:** Find the distance between (1, 2) and (4, 6), and the midpoint of the segment.

**Solution:**

1. Distance = √[(4 − 1)² + (6 − 2)²] = √(9 + 16) = 5.
2. Midpoint = ((1 + 4)/2, (2 + 6)/2) = (2.5, 4).

**Answer:** Distance **5**, midpoint **(2.5, 4)**

### E4. Number of sides from an exterior angle

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Regular polygon from an angle

**Problem:** Each exterior angle of a regular polygon is 24°. How many sides does it have?

**Solution:**

1. Exterior angles of any polygon sum to 360°.
2. n = 360/24 = 15.

**Answer:** **15**

### E5. Possible third side

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Triangle inequality

**Problem:** Two sides of a triangle are 4 cm and 7 cm. How many integer lengths are possible for the third side?

**Solution:**

1. 7 − 4 < x < 7 + 4 → 3 < x < 11.
2. Integers 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10 → 7 values.

**Answer:** **7**

### E6. Pythagorean triplet

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Pythagoras

**Problem:** The legs of a right triangle are 9 cm and 12 cm. Find the hypotenuse.

**Solution:**

1. 9 : 12 = 3 × 3 : 3 × 4 → a multiple of 3-4-5.
2. Hypotenuse = 3 × 5 = 15 cm. Check: 81 + 144 = 225 = 15² ✓.

**Answer:** **15 cm**

### E7. Areas of similar triangles

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Similar triangles

**Problem:** Two similar triangles have corresponding sides in the ratio 3 : 5. What is the ratio of their areas?

**Solution:**

1. Area ratio = (side ratio)² = 9 : 25.

**Answer:** **9 : 25**

### E8. Angle at the centre

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Circle angle chasing

**Problem:** Chord AB subtends an angle of 110° at the centre O of a circle. C is a point on the major arc. Find ∠ACB.

**Solution:**

1. The angle at the circumference is half the angle at the centre on the same arc.
2. ∠ACB = 110°/2 = 55°.

**Answer:** **55°**

### E9. Tangent length

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Circles

**Problem:** A point P is 13 cm from the centre of a circle of radius 5 cm. Find the length of the tangent from P.

**Solution:**

1. Tangent ⟂ radius → right triangle with hypotenuse OP = 13 and leg r = 5.
2. Tangent = √(13² − 5²) = √144 = 12 cm (5-12-13 triplet).

**Answer:** **12 cm**

### E10. Cyclic quadrilateral

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Circle angle chasing

**Problem:** ABCD is a cyclic quadrilateral with ∠A = 75° and ∠B = 95°. Find ∠C and ∠D.

**Solution:**

1. Opposite angles of a cyclic quadrilateral sum to 180°.
2. ∠C = 180° − 75° = 105°; ∠D = 180° − 95° = 85°.
3. Check: 75 + 95 + 105 + 85 = 360° ✓.

**Answer:** ∠C = **105°**, ∠D = **85°**
