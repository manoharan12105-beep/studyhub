# Profit, Loss and Discount — Practice

### P1. An article costing ₹250 is sold at a 20% profit. What is the selling price?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** SP from CP

- A) ₹270
- B) ₹290
- C) ₹300
- D) ₹312.50

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) ₹300

**Explanation:** SP = 250 × 1.2 = ₹300.

</details>

### P2. A watch bought for ₹1,200 is sold for ₹1,050. What is the loss percentage?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Profit and loss percentage

- A) 12.5%
- B) 14.29%
- C) 15%
- D) 10%

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** A) 12.5%

**Explanation:** Loss = 150; 150/1,200 × 100 = 12.5%. Option B divides by SP (150/1,050).

</details>

### P3. The marked price of a shirt is ₹800 and a 15% discount is offered. What is the selling price?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Discount

- A) ₹650
- B) ₹680
- C) ₹685
- D) ₹700

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) ₹680

**Explanation:** SP = 800 × 0.85 = ₹680.

</details>

### P4. Selling an item for ₹720 gives a 20% profit. At what price should it be sold to gain 30%?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** New profit target

- A) ₹760
- B) ₹780
- C) ₹792
- D) ₹810

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) ₹780

**Explanation:** CP = 720/1.2 = 600. SP for 30% profit = 600 × 1.3 = ₹780. Option C (720 × 1.1) adds the extra 10% to the old SP instead of the CP.

</details>

### P5. Successive discounts of 25% and 20% are equivalent to a single discount of:

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Successive discounts

- A) 40%
- B) 42%
- C) 45%
- D) 50%

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** A) 40%

**Explanation:** 25 + 20 − (25 × 20)/100 = 45 − 5 = 40%. Check: 100 → 75 → 60.

</details>

### P6. A man buys oranges at 12 for ₹10 and sells them at 10 for ₹12. Find his profit percentage.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Different rates per article

- A) 20%
- B) 40%
- C) 44%
- D) 50%

<details>
<summary>Hint</summary>

Find CP and SP of one orange.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) 44%

**Explanation:** CP per orange = 10/12 = 5/6; SP per orange = 12/10 = 6/5. SP/CP = (6/5) ÷ (5/6) = 36/25 = 1.44 → 44% profit.

</details>

### P7. A shop offers "Buy 3, Get 1 Free". What is the effective discount?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Buy x get y free

- A) 20%
- B) 25%
- C) 30%
- D) 33.33%

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) 25%

**Explanation:** The customer gets 4 items for the price of 3. Discount = 1/4 = 25%. Option D (1/3) uses the paid items as the base.

</details>

### P8. A trader marks his goods 40% above cost price and allows a 25% discount. What is his profit or loss percentage?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Markup and discount

- A) 15% profit
- B) 5% profit
- C) 5% loss
- D) No profit, no loss

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) 5% profit

**Explanation:** 1.40 × 0.75 = 1.05 → 5% profit. Option A subtracts 40 − 25.

</details>

### P9. The loss incurred by selling an article for ₹1,920 equals the profit earned by selling it for ₹2,080. At what price should it be sold to make a 25% profit?

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Equal profit and loss

- A) ₹2,400
- B) ₹2,500
- C) ₹2,600
- D) ₹2,750

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) ₹2,500

**Explanation:** CP − 1,920 = 2,080 − CP → CP = (1,920 + 2,080)/2 = 2,000. SP = 2,000 × 1.25 = ₹2,500.

</details>

### P10. A dealer uses a weight 20% lighter than the true weight and also sells at 10% above cost price. Find his total gain percentage.

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Dishonest dealer

- A) 30%
- B) 32%
- C) 35%
- D) 37.5%

<details>
<summary>Hint</summary>

For "1 kg" he gives 800 g but charges 1.1 times the cost of 1 kg.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** D) 37.5%

**Explanation:** Let 1 kg cost ₹1. He gives 0.8 kg (cost ₹0.80) and charges ₹1.10. Gain = 0.30/0.80 × 100 = 37.5%. Option A adds 20 + 10.

</details>

### P11. Two articles are sold for ₹1,200 each — one at a 20% profit and the other at a 20% loss. What is the overall result?

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Same SP, equal gain and loss

- A) No profit, no loss
- B) Loss of ₹100
- C) Profit of ₹100
- D) Loss of ₹200

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) Loss of ₹100

**Explanation:** CP₁ = 1,200/1.2 = 1,000; CP₂ = 1,200/0.8 = 1,500. Total CP = 2,500, total SP = 2,400 → loss ₹100, which is 4% = 20²/100 %.

</details>

### P12. A sells a bicycle to B at a 10% profit; B sells it to C at a 20% profit. If C pays ₹2,640, what did A pay for it?

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Chain of sales

- A) ₹1,848
- B) ₹2,000
- C) ₹2,112
- D) ₹2,200

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) ₹2,000

**Explanation:** 2,640 = A's CP × 1.1 × 1.2 = A's CP × 1.32 → A's CP = ₹2,000. Option A subtracts 30% of 2,640 (2,640 × 0.7) — a percentage change is undone by dividing by its factor, not by subtracting.

</details>
