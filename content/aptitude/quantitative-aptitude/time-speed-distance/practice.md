# Time, Speed and Distance — Practice

### P1. A speed of 90 km/h is equal to:

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Core formulas

- A) 15 m/s
- B) 20 m/s
- C) 25 m/s
- D) 324 m/s

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) 25 m/s

**Explanation:** 90 × 5/18 = 25. Option D multiplies by 18/5 (the reverse conversion).

</details>

### P2. A car covers 360 km in 4 hours 30 minutes. What is its speed?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Core formulas

- A) 72 km/h
- B) 78 km/h
- C) 80 km/h
- D) 90 km/h

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) 80 km/h

**Explanation:** 360 / 4.5 = 80. (4 h 30 min is 4.5 h, not 4.3 h.)

</details>

### P3. A 240 m long train crosses a pole in 12 seconds. What is its speed in km/h?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Trains

- A) 20 km/h
- B) 60 km/h
- C) 72 km/h
- D) 80 km/h

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) 72 km/h

**Explanation:** 240/12 = 20 m/s = 20 × 18/5 = 72 km/h. Option A is the speed in m/s.

</details>

### P4. A man travels to his office at 30 km/h and returns along the same route at 20 km/h. What is his average speed?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Average speed

- A) 24 km/h
- B) 25 km/h
- C) 26 km/h
- D) 50 km/h

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** A) 24 km/h

**Explanation:** 2 × 30 × 20 / 50 = 24. Option B is the simple average, which is wrong for equal distances.

</details>

### P5. Two people start 18 km apart and walk towards each other at 4 km/h and 5 km/h. After how long do they meet?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Relative Speed

- A) 1.5 h
- B) 2 h
- C) 3 h
- D) 18 h

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) 2 h

**Explanation:** Relative speed = 9 km/h → 18/9 = 2 h. Option D uses the difference in speeds (1 km/h).

</details>

### P6. A 300 m long train crosses a 450 m long bridge in 30 seconds. What is the speed of the train?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Trains

- A) 36 km/h
- B) 54 km/h
- C) 72 km/h
- D) 90 km/h

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** D) 90 km/h

**Explanation:** Distance = 300 + 450 = 750 m → 750/30 = 25 m/s = 90 km/h. Option A uses only the train's length (10 m/s).

</details>

### P7. A boat's downstream speed is 20 km/h and its upstream speed is 12 km/h. What is the speed of the stream?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Boats and Streams

- A) 4 km/h
- B) 8 km/h
- C) 16 km/h
- D) 2 km/h

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** A) 4 km/h

**Explanation:** v = (20 − 12)/2 = 4 km/h. Option C is the boat's speed in still water; option B forgets to halve.

</details>

### P8. A 150 m long train running at 60 km/h passes a man running at 6 km/h in the same direction. How long does it take to pass him?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Trains

- A) 8 s
- B) 9 s
- C) 10 s
- D) 12 s

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) 10 s

**Explanation:** Relative speed = 54 km/h = 15 m/s → 150/15 = 10 s. Option B uses 66 km/h (opposite directions).

</details>

### P9. Two trains, 140 m and 160 m long, run in opposite directions at 60 km/h and 48 km/h. How long do they take to cross each other?

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Trains

- A) 9 s
- B) 10 s
- C) 12 s
- D) 90 s

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) 10 s

**Explanation:** Distance = 300 m; relative speed = 108 km/h = 30 m/s → 10 s. Option D uses 12 km/h (same direction).

</details>

### P10. A man can row 4.5 km/h in still water. It takes him twice as long to row upstream as to row the same distance downstream. What is the speed of the stream?

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Boats and Streams

- A) 1 km/h
- B) 1.5 km/h
- C) 2 km/h
- D) 2.25 km/h

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) 1.5 km/h

**Explanation:** Same distance, time ratio 2 : 1 → speed ratio 1 : 2 → 2(4.5 − v) = 4.5 + v → 9 − 2v = 4.5 + v → v = 1.5.

</details>

### P11. In a 100 m race, A beats B by 10 m and B beats C by 10 m. By how many metres does A beat C?

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Races

- A) 18 m
- B) 19 m
- C) 20 m
- D) 21 m

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) 19 m

**Explanation:** When A runs 100, B runs 90. When B runs 90, C runs 90 × 90/100 = 81. A beats C by 19 m. Option C adds the margins.

</details>

### P12. Two trains start at the same time from stations X and Y towards each other. After meeting, they take 9 hours and 16 hours respectively to reach their destinations. What is the ratio of their speeds (train from X : train from Y)?

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Relative Speed

- A) 3 : 4
- B) 4 : 3
- C) 9 : 16
- D) 16 : 9

<details>
<summary>Hint</summary>

S₁ / S₂ = √(t₂ / t₁).

</details>

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) 4 : 3

**Explanation:** S_X / S_Y = √(16/9) = 4/3. The train that needs less time after the meeting is the faster one, which rules out A and C.

</details>

### P13. In a 200 m race, A gives B a start of 20 m and the race ends in a dead heat. What is the ratio of A's speed to B's speed?

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Races

- A) 9 : 10
- B) 10 : 9
- C) 11 : 10
- D) 20 : 19

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) 10 : 9

**Explanation:** In the same time, A runs 200 m and B runs 180 m → 200 : 180 = 10 : 9.

</details>
