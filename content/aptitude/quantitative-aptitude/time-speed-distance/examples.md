# Time, Speed and Distance — Solved Examples

### E1. Unit conversion

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Core formulas

**Problem:** Convert 72 km/h into m/s.

**Solution:**

1. 72 × 5/18 = 4 × 5 = 20.

**Answer:** **20 m/s**

### E2. Train crossing a pole

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Trains

**Problem:** A 150 m long train runs at 54 km/h. How long does it take to cross a pole?

**Solution:**

1. Speed = 54 × 5/18 = 15 m/s.
2. Distance = train length = 150 m.
3. Time = 150/15 = 10 s.

**Answer:** **10 seconds**

### E3. Meeting head-on

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Relative Speed

**Problem:** Two cars are 300 km apart and drive towards each other at 40 km/h and 60 km/h. When do they meet?

**Solution:**

1. Opposite directions → relative speed = 40 + 60 = 100 km/h.
2. Time = 300/100 = 3 hours.

**Answer:** **3 hours**

### E4. Average speed over equal distances

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Average speed

**Problem:** A person drives to a town at 40 km/h and returns along the same road at 60 km/h. Find the average speed.

**Solution:**

1. Equal distances → average = 2xy/(x + y).
2. = 2 × 40 × 60 / 100 = 48 km/h.
3. Check with a distance of 120 km: times 3 h and 2 h → 240 km in 5 h = 48 km/h ✓.

**Answer:** **48 km/h**

### E5. Chasing

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Relative Speed

**Problem:** A thief is 200 m ahead of a policeman. The thief runs at 10 km/h and the policeman at 12 km/h. How long will the policeman take to catch the thief?

**Solution:**

1. Same direction → relative speed = 2 km/h = 2 × 5/18 = 5/9 m/s.
2. Time = 200 ÷ 5/9 = 360 s.

**Answer:** **6 minutes**

### E6. Train crossing a platform

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Trains

**Problem:** A 200 m long train running at 72 km/h crosses a 300 m long platform. How long does it take?

**Solution:**

1. Speed = 20 m/s.
2. Distance = 200 + 300 = 500 m.
3. Time = 500/20 = 25 s.

**Answer:** **25 seconds**

### E7. Boat speed and stream speed

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Boats and Streams

**Problem:** A boat goes 24 km downstream in 2 hours and returns upstream in 3 hours. Find the speed of the boat in still water and the speed of the stream.

**Solution:**

1. Downstream = 24/2 = 12 km/h; upstream = 24/3 = 8 km/h.
2. u = (12 + 8)/2 = 10 km/h; v = (12 − 8)/2 = 2 km/h.

**Answer:** Boat **10 km/h**, stream **2 km/h**

### E8. Combining race margins

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Races

**Problem:** In a 1 km race, A beats B by 100 m. In another 1 km race, B beats C by 100 m. By how much would A beat C in a 1 km race?

**Solution:**

1. A : B = 1000 : 900; B : C = 1000 : 900.
2. When A runs 1000 m, B runs 900 m.
3. When B runs 900 m, C runs 900 × 900/1000 = 810 m.
4. A beats C by 1000 − 810 = 190 m.

**Answer:** **190 m**

### E9. Two trains passing each other

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Trains

**Problem:** Two trains, 120 m and 180 m long, run at 50 km/h and 40 km/h on parallel tracks. How long do they take to cross each other if they move (a) in opposite directions, (b) in the same direction?

**Solution:**

1. Distance = 120 + 180 = 300 m in both cases.
2. (a) Relative speed = 90 km/h = 25 m/s → 300/25 = 12 s.
3. (b) Relative speed = 10 km/h = 25/9 m/s → 300 ÷ 25/9 = 108 s.

**Answer:** (a) **12 s** (b) **108 s**

### E10. Round trip on a river

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Boats and Streams

**Problem:** A boat's speed in still water is 15 km/h and the stream flows at 3 km/h. How long does it take to go 30 km downstream and come back?

**Solution:**

1. Downstream = 18 km/h → 30/18 = 5/3 h.
2. Upstream = 12 km/h → 30/12 = 5/2 h.
3. Total = 5/3 + 5/2 = 10/6 + 15/6 = 25/6 h = 4 h 10 min.

**Answer:** **4 hours 10 minutes**

### E11. Circular track

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Races

**Problem:** A and B run on a 600 m circular track at 6 m/s and 4 m/s, starting together from the same point. When do they first meet if they run (a) in the same direction, (b) in opposite directions? When are they next together at the starting point?

**Solution:**

1. (a) Same direction: relative speed 2 m/s → 600/2 = 300 s.
2. (b) Opposite directions: relative speed 10 m/s → 600/10 = 60 s.
3. Lap times: 600/6 = 100 s and 600/4 = 150 s → both at the start after LCM(100, 150) = 300 s.

**Answer:** (a) **300 s** (b) **60 s**; together at the start after **300 s**.
