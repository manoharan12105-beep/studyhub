# Mixtures and Alligation — Solved Examples

### E1. Mixing two varieties

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Mixing two varieties

**Problem:** In what ratio must rice at ₹40/kg be mixed with rice at ₹55/kg to get a mixture worth ₹45/kg?

**Solution:**

1. c = 40, d = 55, m = 45.
2. Cheaper : dearer = (55 − 45) : (45 − 40) = 10 : 5 = 2 : 1.
3. Check: (2 × 40 + 1 × 55)/3 = 135/3 = 45 ✓.

**Answer:** **2 : 1**

### E2. Adding water to change a ratio

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Adding one component

**Problem:** 60 litres of a mixture contain milk and water in the ratio 2 : 1. How much water must be added to make the ratio 1 : 2?

**Solution:**

1. Milk = 40 L, water = 20 L.
2. Milk stays 40 L. For milk : water = 1 : 2, water must be 80 L.
3. Water to add = 80 − 20 = 60 L.

**Answer:** **60 litres**

### E3. Milkman's profit

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Profit by adding water

**Problem:** A milkman buys milk at ₹50 per litre, adds water, and sells the mixture at ₹50 per litre, gaining 25%. Find the ratio of milk to water in the mixture.

**Solution:**

1. Selling price = 125% of the mixture's cost → cost of mixture = 50/1.25 = ₹40 per litre.
2. Alligate milk (₹50) and water (₹0) around ₹40:
   water : milk = (50 − 40) : (40 − 0) = 10 : 40 = 1 : 4.
3. Milk : water = 4 : 1.

**Answer:** **4 : 1**

### E4. Repeated replacement

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Repeated replacement

**Problem:** A container holds 40 litres of milk. 4 litres are removed and replaced with water. This is done three times in total. How much milk is left?

**Solution:**

1. Each operation keeps 36/40 = 0.9 of the milk.
2. Milk left = 40 × 0.9³ = 40 × 0.729 = 29.16 L.

**Answer:** **29.16 litres**

> [!WARNING]
> 40 − 3 × 4 = 28 L is wrong: the second and third removals take out a mixture, which is only partly milk.

### E5. Mixing two mixtures

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Mixing two mixtures

**Problem:** Vessel A has milk and water in the ratio 3 : 2; vessel B has them in the ratio 7 : 3. In what ratio should A and B be mixed to get milk and water in the ratio 2 : 1?

**Solution:**

1. Milk fraction: A = 3/5 = 0.6, B = 7/10 = 0.7, target = 2/3.
2. A : B = (0.7 − 2/3) : (2/3 − 0.6) = (1/30) : (1/15) = 1 : 2.
3. Check: 1 L of A has 0.6 L milk; 2 L of B have 1.4 L milk → 2 L milk in 3 L = 2/3 ✓.

**Answer:** **1 : 2**

### E6. Alligation on profit percentages

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Alligation on percentages

**Problem:** A trader has 60 kg of rice. He sells part of it at a 10% profit and the rest at a 20% loss. Overall he makes a 5% profit. How much did he sell at a 10% profit?

**Solution:**

1. Values: −20 (loss) and +10 (profit); mean +5.
2. Loss part : profit part = (10 − 5) : (5 − (−20)) = 5 : 25 = 1 : 5.
3. Profit part = 5/6 × 60 = 50 kg.
4. Check (cost ₹1/kg): 50 × 1.1 + 10 × 0.8 = 55 + 8 = 63 = 1.05 × 60 ✓.

**Answer:** **50 kg**
