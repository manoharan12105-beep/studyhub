# Mixtures and Alligation — Practice

### P1. In what ratio must tea at ₹120/kg be mixed with tea at ₹180/kg to get a blend worth ₹160/kg?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Mixing two varieties

- A) 1 : 2
- B) 2 : 1
- C) 2 : 3
- D) 3 : 2

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** A) 1 : 2

**Explanation:** (180 − 160) : (160 − 120) = 20 : 40 = 1 : 2. Option B swaps the differences.

</details>

### P2. How many kg of sugar at ₹30/kg must be mixed with 30 kg of sugar at ₹45/kg to get a mixture worth ₹40/kg?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Mixing two varieties

- A) 10 kg
- B) 15 kg
- C) 20 kg
- D) 60 kg

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) 15 kg

**Explanation:** Cheaper : dearer = (45 − 40) : (40 − 30) = 5 : 10 = 1 : 2. With 30 kg of the dearer sugar, the cheaper is 15 kg.

</details>

### P3. A 40-litre mixture contains milk and water in the ratio 3 : 1. How much water must be added to make the ratio 3 : 2?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Adding one component

- A) 5 L
- B) 10 L
- C) 15 L
- D) 20 L

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) 10 L

**Explanation:** Milk 30 L, water 10 L. For 3 : 2 with milk 30 L, water must be 20 L → add 10 L.

</details>

### P4. A 30% acid solution and a 50% acid solution are mixed to make 20 litres of a 42% solution. How many litres of the 30% solution are used?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Concentrations

- A) 6 L
- B) 8 L
- C) 10 L
- D) 12 L

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) 8 L

**Explanation:** 30% : 50% = (50 − 42) : (42 − 30) = 8 : 12 = 2 : 3 → 30% solution = 2/5 × 20 = 8 L. Check: 0.3 × 8 + 0.5 × 12 = 2.4 + 6 = 8.4 = 42% of 20 ✓.

</details>

### P5. A milkman buys milk at ₹40/L, mixes it with water and sells the mixture at ₹40/L, making a 20% profit. What is the ratio of water to milk?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Profit by adding water

- A) 1 : 4
- B) 1 : 5
- C) 1 : 6
- D) 1 : 3

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) 1 : 5

**Explanation:** Mixture CP = 40/1.2 = 100/3. Water : milk = (40 − 100/3) : (100/3 − 0) = (20/3) : (100/3) = 1 : 5. Option A takes 20% of the mixture as water.

</details>

### P6. The average score of a class of 45 students is 70. Boys average 72 and girls average 66. How many boys are there?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Averages as alligation

- A) 15
- B) 25
- C) 30
- D) 35

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) 30

**Explanation:** Girls : boys = (72 − 70) : (70 − 66) = 2 : 4 = 1 : 2 → boys = 2/3 × 45 = 30.

</details>

### P7. A tank holds 80 litres of milk. 8 litres are drawn out and replaced with water; this is done once more. How much milk is now in the tank?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Repeated replacement

- A) 64 L
- B) 64.8 L
- C) 65.6 L
- D) 72 L

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) 64.8 L

**Explanation:** 80 × (72/80)² = 80 × 0.81 = 64.8 L. Option A subtracts 8 L twice.

</details>

### P8. How much water must be added to 60 litres of a 20% salt solution to make it a 15% salt solution?

### P9. A container is full of pure milk. One-fourth of it is removed and replaced with water. This is done three times in total. What is the final ratio of milk to water?

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Repeated replacement

- A) 27 : 37
- B) 27 : 64
- C) 1 : 3
- D) 37 : 27

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** A) 27 : 37

**Explanation:** Milk fraction = (3/4)³ = 27/64; water = 37/64. Option B is milk : total.

</details>

### P10. Two alloys contain gold and copper in the ratios 7 : 2 and 7 : 11. Equal quantities of the two alloys are melted together. What is the ratio of gold to copper in the new alloy?

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Mixing two mixtures

- A) 7 : 5
- B) 14 : 13
- C) 5 : 7
- D) 2 : 1

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** A) 7 : 5

**Explanation:** Take 18 kg of each (LCM of 9 and 18). First: gold 14, copper 4. Second: gold 7, copper 11. Total gold 21, copper 15 → 7 : 5. Option B adds the ratio terms directly (7 + 7 : 2 + 11), which ignores that the alloys have different total parts.

</details>

### P11. A trader has 60 kg of rice. He sells part of it at a 10% profit and the rest at a 20% loss, earning a 5% profit overall. How much rice did he sell at a loss?

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Alligation on percentages

- A) 10 kg
- B) 12 kg
- C) 20 kg
- D) 50 kg

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** A) 10 kg

**Explanation:** Loss part : profit part = (10 − 5) : (5 + 20) = 5 : 25 = 1 : 5 → loss part = 60/6 = 10 kg. Option D is the part sold at a profit.

</details>

### P12. Vessel A contains milk and water in the ratio 5 : 3; vessel B in the ratio 3 : 1. In what ratio must A and B be mixed to get milk and water in the ratio 2 : 1?

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Mixing two mixtures

- A) 1 : 2
- B) 2 : 1
- C) 3 : 2
- D) 1 : 1

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) 2 : 1

**Explanation:** Milk fractions: A = 5/8, B = 3/4 = 6/8, target = 2/3. A : B = (3/4 − 2/3) : (2/3 − 5/8) = (1/12) : (1/24) = 2 : 1. Check: 2 × 5/8 + 1 × 3/4 = 2, out of 3 → 2/3 ✓.

</details>
