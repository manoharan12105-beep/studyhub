# Problems on Ages — Solved Examples

### E1. "Times as old" in the future

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Times as old

**Problem:** A father is 30 years older than his son. In 5 years he will be three times as old as his son. Find their present ages.

**Solution:**

1. Son = S, father = S + 30.
2. In 5 years: S + 35 = 3(S + 5) = 3S + 15.
3. 2S = 20 → S = 10; father = 40.
4. Check: in 5 years, 45 = 3 × 15 ✓.

**Answer:** Son **10**, father **40**.

### E2. Ratio and sum

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Ratio

**Problem:** The ages of two sisters are in the ratio 4 : 5 and their sum is 54. Find their ages.

**Solution:**

1. Ages 4x and 5x → 9x = 54 → x = 6.
2. Ages = 24 and 30.

**Answer:** **24** and **30**

### E3. Ratios at two times (constant difference)

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Ratios at two times

**Problem:** The ratio of the present ages of P and Q is 3 : 5. After 8 years it will be 5 : 7. Find their present ages.

**Solution:**

1. Equation method: (3x + 8)/(5x + 8) = 5/7 → 21x + 56 = 25x + 40 → 4x = 16 → x = 4.
2. Ages = 12 and 20. Check: after 8 years, 20 : 28 = 5 : 7 ✓.

**Answer:** P = **12**, Q = **20**

> [!TIP]
> Both ratios have difference 2 (5 − 3 and 7 − 5). Q goes from 5 to 7 units — 2 units = 8 years → 1 unit = 4 → ages 12 and 20.

### E4. Ratio in the past and in the future

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Ratios at two times

**Problem:** Six years ago, the ratio of the ages of A and B was 6 : 5. Four years from now it will be 11 : 10. Find their present ages.

**Solution:**

1. Ages six years ago: 6x and 5x.
2. Four years from now is 10 years after that: (6x + 10)/(5x + 10) = 11/10.
3. 60x + 100 = 55x + 110 → x = 2.
4. Six years ago: 12 and 10 → present: 18 and 16.
5. Check: in 4 years, 22 : 20 = 11 : 10 ✓.

**Answer:** A = **18**, B = **16**

> [!WARNING]
> The variables 6x and 5x are ages six years ago, not present ages. Adding 6 at the end is the step most often forgotten.

### E5. Children born at equal intervals

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Equal intervals

**Problem:** The sum of the ages of 5 children born at intervals of 3 years each is 50. Find the age of the youngest.

**Solution:**

1. Ages: x, x + 3, x + 6, x + 9, x + 12.
2. Sum = 5x + 30 = 50 → x = 4.

**Answer:** **4 years**

### E6. Average age at a past time

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Family averages

**Problem:** The average age of a family of 5 is 24 years. The youngest member is 8 years old. What was the average age of the family at the time of the youngest member's birth?

**Solution:**

1. Present total = 5 × 24 = 120.
2. Total of the other 4 members now = 120 − 8 = 112.
3. 8 years ago each of them was 8 years younger: 112 − 4 × 8 = 80.
4. At that time the family had 4 members → average = 80/4 = 20.

**Answer:** **20 years**

### E7. Product of ages

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Product of ages

**Problem:** A is 2 years older than B. Three years ago, the product of their ages was 120. Find their present ages.

**Solution:**

1. B = x, A = x + 2. Three years ago: (x − 3)(x − 1) = 120.
2. x² − 4x + 3 = 120 → x² − 4x − 117 = 0.
3. x = [4 ± √(16 + 468)]/2 = (4 ± 22)/2 → x = 13 (rejecting −9).
4. B = 13, A = 15. Check: 10 × 12 = 120 ✓.

**Answer:** A = **15**, B = **13**

> [!TIP]
> 120 as a product of two numbers differing by 2 is 10 × 12 — so ages three years ago were 10 and 12.
