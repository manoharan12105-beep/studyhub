# Problems on Ages — Practice

### P1. A is twice as old as B. Ten years ago, A was three times as old as B. What is A's present age?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Times as old

- A) 20
- B) 30
- C) 40
- D) 50

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) 40

**Explanation:** A = 2B. 2B − 10 = 3(B − 10) → B = 20 → A = 40. Check: ten years ago 30 = 3 × 10 ✓.

</details>

### P2. The sum of the ages of a mother and daughter is 50. Five years ago, the mother was 7 times as old as the daughter. What is the daughter's present age?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Times as old

- A) 5
- B) 8
- C) 10
- D) 12

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) 10

**Explanation:** M + D = 50 and M − 5 = 7(D − 5) → M = 7D − 30 → 8D − 30 = 50 → D = 10 (M = 40). Check: 35 = 7 × 5 ✓.

</details>

### P3. The ages of A and B are in the ratio 5 : 7 and B is 8 years older than A. What is A's age?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Ratio

- A) 16
- B) 20
- C) 25
- D) 28

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) 20

**Explanation:** 7x − 5x = 8 → x = 4 → A = 20, B = 28.

</details>

### P4. The present ages of two brothers are in the ratio 7 : 9. After 6 years, the ratio will be 4 : 5. What is the elder brother's present age?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Ratios at two times

- A) 42
- B) 48
- C) 54
- D) 60

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) 54

**Explanation:** (7x + 6)/(9x + 6) = 4/5 → 35x + 30 = 36x + 24 → x = 6. Ages 42 and 54; check: 48 : 60 = 4 : 5 ✓. Option D is his age after 6 years.

</details>

### P5. Ten years ago, a father was four times as old as his son. Ten years from now, he will be twice as old as his son. What is the ratio of their present ages?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Times as old

- A) 4 : 1
- B) 5 : 2
- C) 7 : 3
- D) 9 : 4

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) 5 : 2

**Explanation:** F − 10 = 4(S − 10) → F = 4S − 30. F + 10 = 2(S + 10) → 4S − 20 = 2S + 20 → S = 20, F = 50. Ratio 50 : 20 = 5 : 2.

</details>

### P6. Three years ago, the average age of a husband, wife and their child was 27. Five years ago, the average age of the wife and child was 20. What is the husband's present age?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Family averages

- A) 35
- B) 37
- C) 40
- D) 42

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) 40

**Explanation:** Three years ago the total was 81 → present total = 81 + 3 × 3 = 90. Five years ago wife + child = 40 → present = 40 + 2 × 5 = 50. Husband = 90 − 50 = 40.

</details>

### P7. A man's age after 15 years will be 5 times his age 5 years ago. What is his present age?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Single person

- A) 8
- B) 10
- C) 12
- D) 15

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) 10

**Explanation:** x + 15 = 5(x − 5) → 4x = 40 → x = 10. Check: 25 = 5 × 5 ✓.

</details>

### P8. A is as much younger than B as he is older than C. If the sum of the ages of B and C is 50, what is A's age?

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Equal differences

- A) 20
- B) 25
- C) 30
- D) Cannot be determined

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) 25

**Explanation:** B − A = A − C → 2A = B + C = 50 → A = 25. A is the average of B and C.

</details>

### P9. A is 4 years older than B, and the product of their present ages is 221. What is A's age?

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Product of ages

- A) 13
- B) 15
- C) 17
- D) 19

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) 17

**Explanation:** B(B + 4) = 221. Two numbers differing by 4 with product 221: 13 × 17. So B = 13, A = 17.

</details>

### P10. The average age of a family of 6 members is 22 years. The youngest member is 7 years old. What was the average age of the family at the time of the youngest member's birth?

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Family averages

- A) 15
- B) 17
- C) 18
- D) 19

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) 18

**Explanation:** Present total = 132. Others now = 132 − 7 = 125. Seven years ago: 125 − 5 × 7 = 90 across 5 members → average 18.

</details>

### P11. Ravi's father was 32 years old when Ravi's elder brother was born. Ravi's mother was 25 years old when Ravi's younger sister was born. Ravi is 6 years younger than his brother and 3 years older than his sister. What is the difference between the ages of the father and the mother?

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Constant differences

- A) 7
- B) 10
- C) 13
- D) 16

<details>
<summary>Hint</summary>

Express everyone's age in terms of Ravi's age R.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** D) 16

**Explanation:** Brother = R + 6 → father = R + 6 + 32 = R + 38. Sister = R − 3 → mother = R − 3 + 25 = R + 22. Difference = 16. Option A (32 − 25) ignores the children's age gaps.

</details>

### P12. The ratio of the present ages of A and B is 5 : 3. Four years ago the ratio was 2 : 1. What will the ratio be four years from now?

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Ratios at two times

- A) 3 : 2
- B) 4 : 3
- C) 5 : 4
- D) 7 : 5

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** A) 3 : 2

**Explanation:** (5x − 4)/(3x − 4) = 2 → 5x − 4 = 6x − 8 → x = 4. Present ages 20 and 12; four years ago 16 : 8 = 2 : 1 ✓. Four years from now: 24 : 16 = 3 : 2.

</details>
