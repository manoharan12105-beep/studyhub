# Simple Interest — Practice

### P1. Find the simple interest on ₹8,000 at 6% per annum for 5 years.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Find SI

- A) ₹2,000
- B) ₹2,400
- C) ₹2,800
- D) ₹10,400

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) ₹2,400

**Explanation:** 8,000 × 6 × 5 / 100 = 2,400. Option D is the amount.

</details>

### P2. At what rate of simple interest will ₹2,500 earn ₹600 in 4 years?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Find R

- A) 5%
- B) 6%
- C) 6.5%
- D) 8%

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) 6%

**Explanation:** R = 100 × 600 / (2,500 × 4) = 6%.

</details>

### P3. ₹4,000 amounts to ₹4,960 in 3 years at simple interest. What is the rate?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Find R

- A) 7%
- B) 8%
- C) 9%
- D) 12%

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) 8%

**Explanation:** SI = 960. R = 100 × 960 / (4,000 × 3) = 8%.

</details>

### P4. At what rate of simple interest will a sum become three times itself in 20 years?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Sum becomes k times

- A) 5%
- B) 10%
- C) 15%
- D) 20%

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) 10%

**Explanation:** Interest needed = 2P → RT = 200 → R = 200/20 = 10%. Option C uses 3P instead of 2P.

</details>

### P5. A sum amounts to ₹6,000 in 2 years and to ₹7,500 in 5 years at simple interest. Find the sum.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Two amounts

- A) ₹4,500
- B) ₹5,000
- C) ₹5,500
- D) ₹6,000

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) ₹5,000

**Explanation:** Interest for 3 years = 1,500 → 500 per year. P = 6,000 − 2 × 500 = 5,000 (rate 10%).

</details>

### P6. In how many years will a sum of money become 2.5 times itself at 12.5% simple interest?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Sum becomes k times

- A) 10

- D) 20

<summary>Answer</summary>

</details>

### P7. The simple interest on a sum is 4/9 of the sum. If the rate (in %) equals the time (in years), what is the rate?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Rate equals time

- A) 4%
- B) 6%
- C) 6⅔ %
- D) 7½ %

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) 6⅔ %

**Explanation:** R²/100 = 4/9 → R² = 400/9 → R = 20/3 = 6⅔.

</details>

### P8. What principal gives ₹1,800 more interest in 5 years at 15% than at 12% simple interest?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Change in rate

- A) ₹10,000
- B) ₹12,000
- C) ₹15,000
- D) ₹18,000

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) ₹12,000

**Explanation:** Extra = P × 3 × 5 / 100 = 0.15P = 1,800 → P = 12,000.

</details>

### P9. ₹15,000 is lent in two parts — one at 8% and the other at 12% simple interest. The total interest after 2 years is ₹2,880. How much was lent at 12%?

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Two rates

- A) ₹5,000
- B) ₹6,000
- C) ₹7,500
- D) ₹9,000

<details>
<summary>Hint</summary>

Find the overall rate per year, then alligate.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) ₹6,000

**Explanation:** Overall rate = 2,880 × 100 / (15,000 × 2) = 9.6%. 8% : 12% = (12 − 9.6) : (9.6 − 8) = 2.4 : 1.6 = 3 : 2 → ₹9,000 at 8% and ₹6,000 at 12%. Check: 1,440 + 1,440 = 2,880 ✓.

</details>

### P10. The rate of simple interest is 6% for the first 3 years, 9% for the next 5 years, and 13% beyond 8 years. If a person pays ₹8,160 as interest for 11 years, what was the principal?

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Rate-years

- A) ₹6,000
- B) ₹7,500
- C) ₹8,000
- D) ₹9,000

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) ₹8,000

**Explanation:** Rate-years = 6 × 3 + 9 × 5 + 13 × 3 = 18 + 45 + 39 = 102. P = 8,160 × 100 / 102 = 8,000.

</details>

### P11. Had the rate of simple interest been 2% higher, a sum would have fetched ₹360 more in 3 years. Find the sum.

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Change in rate

- C) ₹6,000
- D) ₹18,000

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) ₹6,000

</details>

### P12. A sum lent at simple interest amounts to ₹1,240 in 3 years. If the rate is increased by 50%, it amounts to ₹1,360 in the same time. What is the original rate?

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Change in rate

- A) 6%
- B) 8%
- C) 10%
- D) 12%

<details>
<summary>Hint</summary>

Increasing the rate by 50% increases the interest by 50%.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) 8%

**Explanation:** Let SI = S. P + S = 1,240 and P + 1.5S = 1,360 → 0.5S = 120 → S = 240, P = 1,000. R = 240 × 100 / (1,000 × 3) = 8%.

</details>
