# Simple Interest — Solved Examples

### E1. Basic simple interest

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Find SI

**Problem:** Find the simple interest on ₹5,000 at 8% per annum for 3 years.

**Solution:**

1. SI = 5000 × 8 × 3 / 100.
2. = 120,000 / 100 = ₹1,200.

**Answer:** **₹1,200**

### E2. Principal from the amount

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Find P

**Problem:** A sum amounts to ₹7,800 in 3 years at 10% simple interest. Find the sum.

**Solution:**

1. In 3 years at 10%, interest = 30% of P → A = 1.3P.
2. P = 7,800 / 1.3 = ₹6,000.

**Answer:** **₹6,000**

### E3. Doubling and tripling

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Sum becomes k times

**Problem:** A sum doubles in 8 years at simple interest. Find the rate. In how many years will it triple at the same rate?

**Solution:**

1. Doubling: interest = P → RT = 100 → R = 100/8 = 12.5%.
2. Tripling: interest = 2P → RT = 200 → T = 200/12.5 = 16 years.

**Answer:** Rate **12.5%**; triples in **16 years**.

### E4. Two amounts at two times

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Two amounts

**Problem:** A sum amounts to ₹9,800 after 5 years and to ₹12,005 after 8 years at the same rate of simple interest. Find the principal and the rate.

**Solution:**

1. Interest for 3 years = 12,005 − 9,800 = 2,205 → one year = 735.
2. Interest for 5 years = 5 × 735 = 3,675 → P = 9,800 − 3,675 = 6,125.
3. R = 735 / 6,125 × 100 = 12%.

**Answer:** P = **₹6,125**, R = **12%**

### E5. A sum split at two rates

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Two rates

**Problem:** ₹10,000 is lent in two parts, one at 8% and the other at 10% simple interest. The total interest for one year is ₹920. Find the two parts.

**Solution:**

1. Overall rate = 920 / 10,000 × 100 = 9.2%.
2. Alligation: 8% part : 10% part = (10 − 9.2) : (9.2 − 8) = 0.8 : 1.2 = 2 : 3.
3. Parts = ₹4,000 and ₹6,000.
4. Check: 320 + 600 = 920 ✓.

**Answer:** **₹4,000 at 8%** and **₹6,000 at 10%**

### E6. Changing rates

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Rate-years

**Problem:** A bank pays 8% simple interest for the first 2 years, 10% for the next 3 years, and 12% after that. Find the interest on ₹5,000 for 7 years.

**Solution:**

1. Rate × years: 8 × 2 = 16, 10 × 3 = 30, 12 × 2 = 24 → total 70.
2. SI = 5,000 × 70 / 100 = ₹3,500.

**Answer:** **₹3,500**

### E7. Rate equal to time

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Rate equals time

**Problem:** The simple interest on a sum is 4/9 of the sum, and the rate (in %) is numerically equal to the time (in years). Find the rate.

**Solution:**

1. SI = P × R × R / 100 = 4P/9.
2. R² = 400/9 → R = 20/3.

**Answer:** **6⅔ %** (and the time is 6⅔ years)
