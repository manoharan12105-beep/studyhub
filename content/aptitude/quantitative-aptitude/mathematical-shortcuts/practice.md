### P9. Simplify 64 ÷ 8 × 4 − 3 × (5 − 2)².

- A) −25
- B) 5
- C) 23
- D) 59

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) 5

**Explanation:** (5 − 2)² = 9. 64 ÷ 8 = 8, × 4 = 32. 3 × 9 = 27. 32 − 27 = 5. Option A evaluates 64 ÷ (8 × 4) = 2 (multiplying before dividing), then 2 − 27.

</details>

### P11. Which of these equals 347 × 29?

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Choose the exact product

- A) 10,036
- B) 10,063
- C) 10,163
- D) 9,963

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) 10,063

**Explanation:** Unit digit: 7 × 9 = 63 → ends in 3 → A is out. Digit sums: 347 → 5, 29 → 2 → product 10 → 1. C → 11 → 2 ✗; D → 27 → 0 ✗; B → 10 → 1 ✓. Exact: 347 × 30 − 347 = 10,410 − 347 = 10,063.

</details>
