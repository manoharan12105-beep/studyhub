# Mathematical Shortcuts — Solved Examples

### E1. Multiplying by 25

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Multiplying by 5, 25, 125

**Problem:** Find 36 × 25.

**Solution:**

1. 36 × 25 = 36 × 100 / 4.
2. = 3,600 / 4 = 900.

**Answer:** **900**

### E2. Multiplying by 11

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Multiplying by 11

**Problem:** Find 72 × 11 and 68 × 11.

**Solution:**

1. 72 × 11 → 7 | 7 + 2 | 2 = 792.
2. 68 × 11 → 6 | 6 + 8 = 14 | 8 → write 4, carry 1 → 7 | 4 | 8 = 748.

**Answer:** **792** and **748**

### E3. Square of a number ending in 5

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Squaring numbers ending in 5

**Problem:** Find 65².

**Solution:**

1. Leading part 6 → 6 × 7 = 42.
2. Append 25 → 4,225.

**Answer:** **4,225**

### E4. Numbers near 100

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Base multiplication

**Problem:** Find 97 × 96 and 104 × 107.

**Solution:**

1. 97 × 96: differences −3 and −4. Left: 97 − 4 = 93. Right: 3 × 4 = 12 → 9,312.
2. 104 × 107: differences +4 and +7. Left: 104 + 7 = 111. Right: 4 × 7 = 28 → 11,128.

**Answer:** **9,312** and **11,128**

### E5. Symmetric pair

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Difference of squares

**Problem:** Find 47 × 53.

**Solution:**

1. 47 and 53 are 3 either side of 50.
2. 47 × 53 = 50² − 3² = 2,500 − 9 = 2,491.

**Answer:** **2,491**

### E6. Square root of a perfect square

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Square roots

**Problem:** Find √5,329.

**Solution:**

1. Last digit 9 → root ends in 3 or 7.
2. Remaining part 53: 7² = 49 ≤ 53 < 64 = 8² → tens digit 7.
3. Candidates 73 and 77. 75² = 5,625 > 5,329 → the root is below 75 → 73.
4. Check: 73² = 5,329 ✓.

**Answer:** **73**

### E7. Simplification with BODMAS

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Simplification

**Problem:** Simplify 18 ÷ 3 × 2 + 4² − (6 − 2).

**Solution:**

1. Brackets: (6 − 2) = 4.
2. Orders: 4² = 16.
3. Division and multiplication left to right: 18 ÷ 3 = 6, then 6 × 2 = 12.
4. Addition and subtraction left to right: 12 + 16 − 4 = 24.

**Answer:** **24**

### E8. Choosing the right product

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Choose the exact product

**Problem:** Which of these equals 236 × 57? A) 13,452 B) 13,542 C) 13,462 D) 13,352

**Solution:**

1. Digit sums: 236 → 2, 57 → 3 → product 6.
2. A → 15 → 6 ✓; B → 15 → 6 ✓; C → 16 → 7 ✗; D → 14 → 5 ✗.
3. A and B remain (digit sums cannot separate swapped digits). Both end in 2, as 6 × 7 = 42 requires.
4. Estimate: 236 × 57 = 240 × 57 − 4 × 57 = 13,680 − 228 = 13,452.

**Answer:** **A) 13,452**

### E9. Approximation

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Approximate value

**Problem:** Find the approximate value of 39.97% of 601.2 + 14.98 × 7.99.

**Solution:**

1. 39.97% ≈ 40%; 601.2 ≈ 600 → 240.
2. 14.98 × 7.99 ≈ 15 × 8 = 120.
3. Total ≈ 360.

**Answer:** **≈ 360**
