# Averages — Solved Examples

### E1. Evenly spaced numbers

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Consecutive numbers

**Problem:** Find the average of 12, 15, 18, 21 and 24.

**Solution:**

1. The numbers increase by 3 each time — evenly spaced.
2. Average = (first + last)/2 = (12 + 24)/2 = 18.
3. Check: sum = 90, 90/5 = 18 ✓.

**Answer:** **18**

### E2. Missing value

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Missing value

**Problem:** The average of five numbers is 20. Four of them are 15, 18, 22 and 25. Find the fifth.

**Solution:**

1. Total = 5 × 20 = 100.
2. Known sum = 15 + 18 + 22 + 25 = 80.
3. Fifth = 100 − 80 = 20.

**Answer:** **20**

### E3. A person joins

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Person joins

**Problem:** The average weight of 30 students is 40 kg. When the teacher's weight is included, the average rises by 1 kg. Find the teacher's weight.

**Solution:**

1. Old total = 30 × 40 = 1,200.
2. New total = 31 × 41 = 1,271.
3. Teacher = 1,271 − 1,200 = 71 kg.

**Answer:** **71 kg**

> [!TIP]
> Teacher = old average + new count × increase = 40 + 31 × 1 = 71.

### E4. Replacement

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Replacement

**Problem:** The average weight of 8 men increases by 2.5 kg when one of them, weighing 65 kg, is replaced by a new man. Find the new man's weight.

**Solution:**

1. The group total rises by 8 × 2.5 = 20 kg.
2. The only change is the swap, so new man − 65 = 20.
3. New man = 85 kg.

**Answer:** **85 kg**

### E5. Weighted average of two sections

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Weighted average

**Problem:** Section A has 30 students with average marks 60; section B has 20 students with average 75. Find the average of all 50 students.

**Solution:**

1. Total A = 30 × 60 = 1,800; total B = 20 × 75 = 1,500.
2. Combined average = 3,300 / 50 = 66.

**Answer:** **66**

> [!WARNING]
> (60 + 75)/2 = 67.5 is wrong — section A is larger, so the combined average is closer to 60.

### E6. Correcting a wrong entry

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Wrong entry

**Problem:** The mean of 50 observations was 36. It was later found that 48 had been recorded as 23. Find the correct mean.

**Solution:**

1. The recorded total is short by 48 − 23 = 25.
2. Correct mean = 36 + 25/50 = 36.5.

**Answer:** **36.5**

### E7. Batting average

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Innings averages

**Problem:** A batsman scores 85 runs in his 17th innings, which increases his average by 3. Find his average after the 17th innings.

**Solution:**

1. Let the average after 16 innings be x. Total = 16x.
2. After 17 innings: 16x + 85 = 17(x + 3) = 17x + 51.
3. x = 34. New average = 34 + 3 = 37.
4. Check: 16 × 34 + 85 = 629 = 17 × 37 ✓.

**Answer:** **37**

> [!TIP]
> New average = score − (old innings) × increase = 85 − 16 × 3 = 37.

### E8. Deviation method

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Assumed mean

**Problem:** Find the average of 47, 52, 49, 55 and 48 using the deviation method.

**Solution:**

1. Assume mean = 50.
2. Deviations: −3, +2, −1, +5, −2 → sum = +1.
3. Average = 50 + 1/5 = 50.2.

**Answer:** **50.2**
