# Averages — Practice

### P1. What is the average of the first 10 natural numbers?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Consecutive numbers

- A) 5
- B) 5.5
- C) 6
- D) 10

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) 5.5

**Explanation:** (n + 1)/2 = 11/2 = 5.5, or (1 + 10)/2.

</details>

### P2. The average of 6 numbers is 15. If the number 25 is removed, what is the average of the rest?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Person leaves

- A) 12
- B) 13
- C) 14
- D) 15

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) 13

**Explanation:** Total = 90. Remaining total = 65 over 5 numbers → 13.

</details>

### P3. What is the average of the first 20 even numbers?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Consecutive numbers

- A) 20
- B) 20.5

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) 21

**Explanation:** The first n even numbers average n + 1 = 21. Check: (2 + 40)/2 = 21.

</details>

### P4. The average age of 24 students is 12 years. When the teacher's age is included, the average becomes 13. How old is the teacher?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Person joins

- A) 25
- B) 36
- C) 37
- D) 38

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) 37

**Explanation:** 25 × 13 − 24 × 12 = 325 − 288 = 37. Or 12 + 25 × 1 = 37.

</details>

### P5. The average of 11 results is 50. The average of the first six is 49 and of the last six is 52. Find the sixth result.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Overlapping groups

- A) 50
- B) 54
- C) 56
- D) 58

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) 56

**Explanation:** First six total 294; last six total 312; all eleven total 550. The sixth result is counted twice: 294 + 312 − 550 = 56.

</details>

### P6. The average weight of 10 people increases by 1.5 kg when a person weighing 50 kg is replaced by a new person. What is the new person's weight?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Replacement

- A) 51.5 kg
- B) 60 kg
- C) 65 kg
- D) 66.5 kg

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) 65 kg

**Explanation:** Total increases by 10 × 1.5 = 15 → new person = 50 + 15 = 65 kg.

</details>

### P7. The average salary of all workers in a workshop is ₹8,000. The average salary of 7 technicians is ₹12,000 and of the rest is ₹6,000. How many workers are there?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Mixed groups

- A) 14
- B) 21
- C) 22
- D) 35

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) 21

**Explanation:** 7 × 12,000 + (n − 7) × 6,000 = 8,000n → 84,000 − 42,000 = 2,000n → n = 21.

</details>

### P9. A batsman scores 87 runs in his 17th innings and thereby increases his average by 3. What is his average after the 17th innings?

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Innings averages

- A) 36
- B) 37
- C) 39
- D) 40

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) 39

**Explanation:** 16x + 87 = 17(x + 3) → x = 36; new average = 39. Shortcut: 87 − 16 × 3 = 39. Option A is the old average.

</details>

### P10. The average of three numbers is 77. The first is twice the second, and the second is twice the third. Find the first number.

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Missing value

- A) 33
- B) 66
- C) 99
- D) 132

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** D) 132

**Explanation:** Third = x, second = 2x, first = 4x. 7x = 3 × 77 = 231 → x = 33 → first = 132.

</details>

### P11. The average temperature from Monday to Wednesday was 37 °C and from Tuesday to Thursday was 34 °C. Thursday's temperature was 4/5 of Monday's. What was Thursday's temperature?

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Overlapping groups

- A) 34 °C
- B) 35 °C
- C) 36 °C
- D) 45 °C

<details>
<summary>Hint</summary>

Subtract the two totals — Tuesday and Wednesday cancel.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) 36 °C

**Explanation:** Mon + Tue + Wed = 111; Tue + Wed + Thu = 102 → Mon − Thu = 9. With Thu = 0.8 Mon: 0.2 Mon = 9 → Mon = 45, Thu = 36. Option D is Monday's temperature.

</details>

### P12. The average marks of 30 students were 45. Later it was found that 64 had been entered as 46 and 70 had been entered as 79. What is the correct average?

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Wrong entry

- A) 44.7
- B) 45.3
- C) 45.6
- D) 45.9

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) 45.3

**Explanation:** Correction to the total = (64 − 46) + (70 − 79) = 18 − 9 = +9. Correct average = 45 + 9/30 = 45.3.

</details>
