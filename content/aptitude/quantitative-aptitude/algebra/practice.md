### P1. Solve 5x − 8 = 2x + 7.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Linear Equations

- A) 3
- B) 5
- C) −5
- D) 15

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) 5

**Explanation:** 5x − 2x = 7 + 8 → 3x = 15 → x = 5.

</details>

### P3. What are the roots of x² − 9x + 20 = 0?

### P6. If a − b = 3 and ab = 10, find a² + b².

- A) 19
- B) 23
- C) 29
- D) 39

<details>

### P8. Solve −3 ≤ 2x + 1 < 7.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Inequalities

- A) −2 ≤ x < 3
- B) −2 < x ≤ 3
- C) −1 ≤ x < 4
- D) −4 ≤ x < 6

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Explanation:** Subtract 1: −4 ≤ 2x < 6. Divide by 2: −2 ≤ x < 3. The signs keep their direction because 2 is positive.

</details>

### P12. 5 pens and 3 notebooks cost ₹110; 3 pens and 5 notebooks cost ₹130. What is the cost of one notebook?

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Symmetric equations

- A) ₹10
- B) ₹15
- C) ₹20
- D) ₹25

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) ₹20

**Explanation:** Add: 8(p + n) = 240 → p + n = 30. Subtract: 2(n − p) = 20 → n − p = 10. So n = 20, p = 10. Option A is the price of a pen.

</details>

### P13. Solve x² − x − 12 > 0.

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Inequalities

- A) −3 < x < 4
- B) x < −3 or x > 4
- C) x < −4 or x > 3
- D) −4 < x < 3

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) x < −3 or x > 4

**Explanation:** (x − 4)(x + 3) > 0 → positive outside the roots −3 and 4. Option A is the solution of "< 0".

</details>

### P14. I. x² = 16; II. y² − 9y + 20 = 0. What is the relationship between x and y?

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Comparing roots

- A) x > y
- B) x < y
- C) x ≤ y
- D) Relationship cannot be established

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) x ≤ y

**Explanation:** x = ±4; y = 4 or 5. Pairs: (−4, 4) <, (−4, 5) <, (4, 4) =, (4, 5) <. Never greater, sometimes equal → x ≤ y.

</details>
