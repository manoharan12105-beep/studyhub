# Percentages — Practice

### P1. What is 15% of 480?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Percentage of a number

- A) 62
- B) 68
- C) 72
- D) 82

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) 72

**Explanation:** 10% of 480 = 48, 5% = 24 → 15% = 72.

</details>

### P2. 40 is what percent of 250?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** What percent

- A) 10%
- B) 16%
- C) 20%
- D) 625%

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) 16%

**Explanation:** (40/250) × 100 = 16%. Option D comes from (250/40) × 100 — the base and the part swapped.

</details>

### P3. Express 3/8 as a percentage.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Fraction equivalents

- A) 33.33%
- B) 35%
- C) 37.5%
- D) 38%

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) 37.5%

**Explanation:** 1/8 = 12.5%, so 3/8 = 37.5%.

</details>

### P4. When a number is increased by 20%, it becomes 360. What is the number?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Reverse percentage

- A) 288
- B) 300
- C) 320
- D) 432

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) 300

**Explanation:** Number × 1.2 = 360 → number = 300. Option A (360 × 0.8) is the classic trap: it takes 20% of the final value instead of the original.

</details>

### P5. If A is 50% more than B, then B is what percent less than A?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Comparing two quantities

- A) 25%
- B) 33.33%
- C) 50%
- D) 66.67%

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) 33.33%

**Explanation:** Let B = 100, A = 150. B is 50 less than A; 50/150 × 100 = 33.33%.

</details>

### P6. A price increases by 10% and then again by 10%. What is the total percentage increase?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Successive changes

- A) 20%
- B) 20.5%
- C) 21%
- D) 22%

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) 21%

**Explanation:** 10 + 10 + (10 × 10)/100 = 21%. Or 1.1 × 1.1 = 1.21.

</details>

### P7. A salary is reduced by 20%. By what percent must the new salary be increased to restore the original salary?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Reverse percentage

- A) 20%
- B) 22.5%
- C) 25%
- D) 30%

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) 25%

**Explanation:** 100 → 80. To return to 100 the increase is 20 on a base of 80: 20/80 × 100 = 25%. Formula: r/(100 − r) × 100 = 20/80 × 100.

</details>

### P8. A person earns ₹40,000 a month and spends 75% of it. If income rises by 20% and expenditure rises by 10%, by what percent do savings increase?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Income, expenditure and savings

- A) 10%
- B) 30%
- C) 40%
- D) 50%

<details>
<summary>Hint</summary>

Compute old and new savings separately.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** D) 50%

**Explanation:** Old: expenditure 30,000, savings 10,000. New income 48,000, new expenditure 33,000, savings 15,000. Increase = 5,000/10,000 = 50%.

</details>

### P9. In an election between two candidates, 10% of the votes cast were invalid. The winner got 60% of the valid votes and won by 1,800 votes. How many votes were cast?

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Elections

- A) 9,000
- B) 9,900
- C) 10,000
- D) 12,000

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) 10,000

**Explanation:** Margin = 60% − 40% = 20% of valid votes = 1,800 → valid = 9,000. Valid votes are 90% of votes cast → cast = 9,000 / 0.9 = 10,000. Option A forgets the invalid votes.

</details>

### P10. A's income is 25% more than B's, and B's income is 20% more than C's. By what percent is C's income less than A's?

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Comparing two quantities

- A) 33.33%
- B) 40%
- C) 45%
- D) 50%

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** A) 33.33%

**Explanation:** Let C = 100 → B = 120 → A = 150. C is 50 less than A: 50/150 × 100 = 33.33%. Option C (25 + 20) adds percentages with different bases.

</details>

### P11. A machine worth ₹1,25,000 depreciates by 20% every year. What is its value after 3 years?

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Growth and depreciation

- A) ₹50,000
- B) ₹62,500
- C) ₹64,000
- D) ₹80,000

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) ₹64,000

**Explanation:** 1,25,000 × 0.8 × 0.8 × 0.8 = 1,25,000 × 0.512 = 64,000. Option A takes 20% of the original value three times (60% off); depreciation actually compounds on the reduced value each year.

</details>

### P12. The price of petrol rises by 25%. A person increases spending on petrol by only 10%. By what percent does their consumption change?

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Price and consumption

- A) 15% decrease
- B) 12% decrease
- C) 10% decrease
- D) 8% decrease

<details>
<summary>Hint</summary>

Consumption = expenditure ÷ price.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) 12% decrease

**Explanation:** New consumption = (1.10 / 1.25) × old = 0.88 × old → a 12% decrease. Option A subtracts 25 − 10, which ignores that the effects multiply.

</details>
