# Compound Interest — Solved Examples

### E1. Compound interest for 2 years

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Find CI

**Problem:** Find the compound interest on ₹10,000 at 10% per annum for 2 years, compounded annually.

**Solution:**

1. Year 1: interest 1,000 → amount 11,000.
2. Year 2: interest 10% of 11,000 = 1,100 → amount 12,100.
3. CI = 12,100 − 10,000 = 2,100.

**Answer:** **₹2,100**

### E2. Half-yearly compounding

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Find CI

**Problem:** Find the compound interest on ₹8,000 for 1 year at 10% per annum, compounded half-yearly.

**Solution:**

1. Rate per half-year = 5%; periods = 2.
2. A = 8,000 × 1.05² = 8,000 × 1.1025 = 8,820.
3. CI = 820.

**Answer:** **₹820** (annual compounding would give ₹800)

### E3. Principal from CI − SI

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** CI − SI

**Problem:** The difference between compound and simple interest on a sum for 2 years at 10% per annum is ₹50. Find the sum.

**Solution:**

1. CI − SI = P × (10/100)² = 0.01P.
2. 0.01P = 50 → P = 5,000.

**Answer:** **₹5,000**

### E4. Growth multiples

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Growth multiples

**Problem:** A sum doubles in 5 years at compound interest. In how many years will it become 8 times?

**Solution:**

1. Factor for 5 years = 2.
2. 8 = 2³ → three such periods → 15 years.

**Answer:** **15 years**

### E5. CI − SI for 3 years

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** CI − SI

**Problem:** Find the difference between CI and SI on ₹10,000 for 3 years at 10% per annum.

**Solution:**

1. Formula: P × (R/100)² × (3 + R/100) = 10,000 × 0.01 × 3.1 = 310.
2. Check: CI = 10,000 × 1.331 − 10,000 = 3,310; SI = 3,000; difference = 310 ✓.

**Answer:** **₹310**

### E6. Equal instalments

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Instalments

**Problem:** A loan of ₹2,100 at 10% compound interest is repaid in two equal annual instalments. Find each instalment.

**Solution:**

1. x/1.1 + x/1.21 = 2,100.
2. Common denominator 1.21: (1.1x + x)/1.21 = 2.1x/1.21 = 2,100.
3. x = 2,100 × 1.21 / 2.1 = 1,210.
4. Check: after year 1 the debt is 2,310; paying 1,210 leaves 1,100; after year 2 it is 1,210, cleared by the second instalment ✓.

**Answer:** **₹1,210**

### E7. Fractional years

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Find CI

**Problem:** Find the compound interest on ₹10,000 at 10% per annum for 2½ years, compounded annually.

**Solution:**

1. Two full years: × 1.21 → 12,100.
2. Remaining half year at simple interest: 5% of 12,100 = 605 → 12,705.
3. CI = 2,705.

**Answer:** **₹2,705**

### E8. Principal and rate from consecutive amounts

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Consecutive amounts

**Problem:** A sum at compound interest amounts to ₹8,820 in 2 years and ₹9,261 in 3 years. Find the rate and the sum.

**Solution:**

1. Year 3's interest = 9,261 − 8,820 = 441, earned on 8,820.
2. R = 441/8,820 × 100 = 5%.
3. P = 8,820 / 1.05² = 8,820 / 1.1025 = 8,000.

**Answer:** Rate **5%**, sum **₹8,000**
