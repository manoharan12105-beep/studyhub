# Mensuration — Solved Examples

### E1. Rectangle

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** 2D

**Problem:** A rectangle is 12 cm long and 5 cm wide. Find its area, perimeter and diagonal.

**Solution:**

1. Area = 12 × 5 = 60 cm².
2. Perimeter = 2(12 + 5) = 34 cm.
3. Diagonal = √(144 + 25) = √169 = 13 cm.

**Answer:** **60 cm²**, **34 cm**, **13 cm**

### E2. Cube

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** 3D

**Problem:** Find the volume and total surface area of a cube with edge 5 cm.

**Solution:**

1. Volume = 5³ = 125 cm³.
2. Surface area = 6 × 5² = 150 cm².

**Answer:** **125 cm³** and **150 cm²**

### E3. Heron's formula

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** 2D

**Problem:** Find the area of a triangle with sides 13 cm, 14 cm and 15 cm.

**Solution:**

1. s = (13 + 14 + 15)/2 = 21.
2. s − a = 8, s − b = 7, s − c = 6.
3. Area = √(21 × 8 × 7 × 6) = √7056 = 84 cm².

**Answer:** **84 cm²**

### E4. Path around a garden

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Paths

**Problem:** A rectangular garden is 30 m by 20 m. A path 2 m wide runs around it on the outside. Find the area of the path.

**Solution:**

1. Outer rectangle = (30 + 4) × (20 + 4) = 34 × 24 = 816 m².
2. Garden = 600 m².
3. Path = 816 − 600 = 216 m².

**Answer:** **216 m²**

> [!WARNING]
> The width is added on both sides, so each dimension grows by 2w = 4 m, not 2 m.

### E5. Wheel revolutions

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Wheels

**Problem:** A wheel has a radius of 35 cm. How many revolutions does it make to cover 2.2 km?

**Solution:**

1. Circumference = 2 × 22/7 × 35 = 220 cm = 2.2 m.
2. 2.2 km = 2,200 m → 2,200 / 2.2 = 1,000 revolutions.

**Answer:** **1,000**

### E6. Cylinder

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** 3D

**Problem:** Find the volume and curved surface area of a cylinder with radius 7 cm and height 10 cm.

**Solution:**

1. Volume = 22/7 × 7² × 10 = 22 × 7 × 10 = 1,540 cm³.
2. Curved surface = 2 × 22/7 × 7 × 10 = 440 cm².

**Answer:** **1,540 cm³** and **440 cm²**

### E7. Percentage change in area

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Percentage change

**Problem:** Each side of a rectangle is increased by 20%. By what percentage does its area increase?

**Solution:**

1. Area factor = 1.2 × 1.2 = 1.44.
2. Increase = 44%.

**Answer:** **44%**

### E8. Cone

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** 3D

**Problem:** A cone has base radius 6 cm and height 8 cm. Find its slant height, curved surface area and volume (in terms of π).

**Solution:**

1. l = √(36 + 64) = 10 cm.
2. Curved surface = πrl = π × 6 × 10 = 60π cm².
3. Volume = ⅓ × π × 36 × 8 = 96π cm³.

**Answer:** l = **10 cm**, CSA = **60π cm²**, V = **96π cm³**

### E9. Melting and recasting

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Melting and recasting

**Problem:** A metal sphere of radius 6 cm is melted and recast into a cylinder of radius 4 cm. Find the height of the cylinder.

**Solution:**

1. Sphere volume = (4/3)π × 216 = 288π.
2. Cylinder volume = π × 16 × h.
3. 16πh = 288π → h = 18 cm.

**Answer:** **18 cm**

### E10. Cutting a cube

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Scaling

**Problem:** A cube of edge 12 cm is cut into cubes of edge 3 cm. How many small cubes are formed, and how does the total surface area change?

**Solution:**

1. Cubes along each edge = 12/3 = 4 → 4³ = 64 small cubes.
2. Original surface = 6 × 144 = 864 cm².
3. New total surface = 64 × 6 × 9 = 3,456 cm².
4. Ratio = 3,456/864 = 4.

**Answer:** **64** cubes; total surface area becomes **4 times** the original.
