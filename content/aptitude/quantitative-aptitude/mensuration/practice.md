# Mensuration — Practice

### P1. The diagonal of a square is 10 cm. What is its area?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** 2D

- A) 25 cm²
- B) 50 cm²
- C) 100 cm²
- D) 50√2 cm²

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) 50 cm²

**Explanation:** Area = d²/2 = 100/2 = 50. Option C squares the diagonal as if it were the side.

</details>

### P2. The circumference of a circle is 44 cm. What is its area? (π = 22/7)

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** 2D

- A) 77 cm²
- B) 144 cm²
- C) 154 cm²
- D) 616 cm²

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) 154 cm²

**Explanation:** 2πr = 44 → r = 7. Area = 22/7 × 49 = 154. Option D uses 14 (the diameter) as the radius.

</details>

### P3. The volume of a cube is 343 cm³. What is its total surface area?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** 3D

- A) 196 cm²
- B) 245 cm²
- C) 294 cm²
- D) 343 cm²

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) 294 cm²

**Explanation:** Edge = ∛343 = 7. Surface = 6 × 49 = 294. Option A is the lateral surface (4a²).

</details>

### P4. The diagonals of a rhombus are 24 cm and 10 cm. What is the length of each side?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** 2D

- A) 12 cm
- B) 13 cm
- C) 17 cm
- D) 26 cm

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) 13 cm

**Explanation:** The diagonals bisect each other at right angles: side = √(12² + 5²) = 13. Option D uses the full diagonals.

</details>

### P8. What is the area of a sector of a circle of radius 14 cm with central angle 90°? (π = 22/7)

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** 2D

- A) 77 cm²
- B) 154 cm²
- C) 308 cm²
- D) 616 cm²

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

</details>

### P9. A solid metal sphere of radius 3 cm is melted and recast into small spheres of radius 1 cm. How many small spheres are made?

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Melting and recasting

- A) 3
- B) 9
- C) 27
- D) 81

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) 27

**Explanation:** Volumes scale as r³: (3/1)³ = 27. Option B uses r² (surface area).

</details>

### P10. A cylinder and a cone have the same base radius and the same height. What is the ratio of their volumes?

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** 3D

- A) 1 : 3
- B) 2 : 1
- C) 3 : 1
- D) 3 : 2

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) 3 : 1

**Explanation:** πr²h : ⅓πr²h = 3 : 1.

</details>

### P11. Find the total surface area of a solid hemisphere of radius 21 cm. (π = 22/7)

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** 3D

- A) 1,386 cm²
- B) 2,772 cm²
- C) 4,158 cm²
- D) 5,544 cm²

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) 4,158 cm²

**Explanation:** 3πr² = 3 × 22/7 × 441 = 4,158. Option B is only the curved surface (2πr²).

</details>

### P12. The radius of a cylinder is doubled and its height is halved. What happens to its volume?

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Scaling

- A) Unchanged
- B) Doubled
- C) Halved
- D) Four times

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) Doubled

**Explanation:** V ∝ r²h → 2² × ½ = 2.

</details>

### P13. Water flows through a cylindrical pipe of internal radius 7 cm at 5 m per second. How many litres flow out per second? (π = 22/7)

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Flow through a pipe

- A) 7.7 L
- B) 38.5 L
- C) 77 L
- D) 770 L

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) 77 L

**Explanation:** Cross-section = 22/7 × 7² = 154 cm². Speed 5 m/s = 500 cm/s. Volume per second = 154 × 500 = 77,000 cm³ = 77 L.

</details>
