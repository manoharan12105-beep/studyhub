# Data Interpretation — Solved Examples

### E1. Reading a table

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Tables

**Problem:** Marks of three students:

| Student | Maths | Science | English |
|---------|-------|---------|---------|
| Asha | 85 | 78 | 72 |
| Bala | 92 | 81 | 65 |
| Chitra | 76 | 88 | 90 |

(a) Who has the highest total? (b) What is Chitra's average?

**Solution:**

1. Totals: Asha 85 + 78 + 72 = 235; Bala 92 + 81 + 65 = 238; Chitra 76 + 88 + 90 = 254.
2. Highest total: Chitra.
3. Chitra's average = 254/3 ≈ 84.67.

**Answer:** (a) **Chitra** (b) **≈ 84.67**

### E2. Growth and share from bar data

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Bar Graphs

**Problem:** A bar graph shows a company's exports (₹ crore): 2019 — 250, 2020 — 300, 2021 — 270, 2022 — 360. Find (a) the percentage increase from 2021 to 2022, and (b) the share of 2022 in total exports over the four years.

**Solution:**

1. (a) Increase = 90 on a base of 270 → 90/270 × 100 = 33.33%.
2. (b) Total = 250 + 300 + 270 + 360 = 1,180 → 360/1,180 × 100 ≈ 30.5%.

**Answer:** (a) **33.33%** (b) **≈ 30.5%**

### E3. Comparing two lines

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Line Graphs

**Problem:** A line graph shows students passing from two schools:

| Year | 2019 | 2020 | 2021 | 2022 |
|------|------|------|------|------|
| School A | 120 | 140 | 160 | 150 |
| School B | 100 | 130 | 150 | 180 |

(a) In which year was the gap between the schools largest? (b) What was School B's percentage increase from 2019 to 2022?

**Solution:**

1. (a) Gaps: 20, 10, 10, 30 (B ahead in 2022) → largest in 2022.
2. (b) (180 − 100)/100 × 100 = 80%.

**Answer:** (a) **2022** (b) **80%**

### E4. Pie chart from one known value

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Pie Charts

**Problem:** The cost of publishing a book is split as: Paper 25%, Printing 20%, Binding 15%, Royalty 15%, Transport 10%, Promotion 15%. If the royalty is ₹30,000, find (a) the printing cost and (b) the central angle for binding.

**Solution:**

1. (a) Printing : Royalty = 20 : 15 → printing = 30,000 × 20/15 = ₹40,000.
2. (b) 15 × 3.6 = 54°.

**Answer:** (a) **₹40,000** (b) **54°**

> [!TIP]
> No need to find the total cost — the ratio of percentages gives printing directly.

### E5. Pie chart with pricing

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Pie Charts

**Problem:** Using the data of E4, 5,000 copies are printed. The marked price of each copy is 20% above its cost. Find the marked price per copy.

**Solution:**

1. Royalty ₹30,000 is 15% → total cost = 30,000 × 100/15 = ₹2,00,000.
2. Cost per copy = 2,00,000/5,000 = ₹40.
3. Marked price = 40 × 1.2 = ₹48.

**Answer:** **₹48**

### E6. Caselet

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Caselet DI

**Problem:** A company has 800 employees, 60% of whom are men. 25% of the men and 40% of the women work in IT. The remaining employees work in HR or Sales. Among non-IT men, HR : Sales = 1 : 2; non-IT women are split equally between HR and Sales. Find (a) the number of employees in Sales and (b) the percentage of IT employees who are women.

**Solution:**

1. Men = 480, women = 320.
2. IT: men 25% of 480 = 120; women 40% of 320 = 128 → IT total 248.
3. Non-IT men = 360 → HR 120, Sales 240. Non-IT women = 192 → HR 96, Sales 96.

| | IT | HR | Sales | Total |
|--|----|----|-------|-------|
| Men | 120 | 120 | 240 | 480 |
| Women | 128 | 96 | 96 | 320 |

4. (a) Sales = 240 + 96 = 336.
5. (b) 128/248 × 100 ≈ 51.6%.

**Answer:** (a) **336** (b) **≈ 51.6%**
