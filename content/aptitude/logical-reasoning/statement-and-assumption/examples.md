# Statement and Assumption — Solved Examples

Options used throughout: A) Only I is implicit · B) Only II is implicit · C) Either I or II is implicit · D) Neither I nor II is implicit · E) Both I and II are implicit.

### E1. Notice

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Notice or instruction

**Problem:**
Statement: A notice in an office building: "Please use the stairs during the power cut."
Assumptions: I. Lifts do not work during a power cut. II. People are able to use the stairs.

**Solution:**

1. Negate I: lifts work during power cuts → the notice would be pointless → I is implicit.
2. Negate II: people cannot use the stairs → the notice would be pointless → II is implicit.

**Answer:** **E) Both I and II are implicit**

### E2. Advertisement

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Advertisement

**Problem:**
Statement: "Buy XYZ toothpaste for whiter teeth."
Assumptions: I. People want whiter teeth. II. No other toothpaste can whiten teeth.

**Solution:**

1. The advert offers whiter teeth as a reason to buy → it assumes people want them → I ✓.
2. The advert does not need other toothpastes to fail; "no other" is an extreme claim → II ✗.

**Answer:** **A) Only I is implicit**

### E3. Policy decision

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Policy decision

**Problem:**
Statement: The government has decided to increase the number of buses on city routes to reduce traffic congestion.
Assumptions: I. More people will use buses if more buses are available. II. Traffic congestion will disappear completely.

**Solution:**

1. If people did not switch to the extra buses, the plan could not reduce congestion → I ✓.
2. "Disappear completely" is an extreme outcome, not something the decision needs → II ✗.

**Answer:** **A) Only I is implicit**

### E4. Advice

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Advice

**Problem:**
Statement: "If you want to stay healthy, eat green vegetables daily."
Assumptions: I. Green vegetables help keep people healthy. II. Only green vegetables keep people healthy.

**Solution:**

1. The advice makes sense only if green vegetables are beneficial → I ✓.
2. Other foods may also help; "only" is not needed → II ✗.

**Answer:** **A) Only I is implicit**

### E5. Neither is implicit

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Notice or instruction

**Problem:**
Statement: "The school will remain closed tomorrow on account of the festival."
Assumptions: I. All students celebrate the festival. II. Teachers do not want to work tomorrow.

**Solution:**

1. Schools close for festivals as a general holiday; it is not necessary that every student celebrates → I ✗ ("all").
2. Teachers' wishes are not the stated reason and are not needed → II ✗.

**Answer:** **D) Neither I nor II is implicit**

### E6. Both options assumed to work

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Advice

**Problem:**
Statement: "To stay profitable, the company should either cut costs or increase prices."
Assumptions: I. Cutting costs can help the company stay profitable. II. Increasing prices can help the company stay profitable.

**Solution:**

1. The speaker offers cutting costs as a way to stay profitable → it must be assumed to work → I ✓.
2. The same applies to increasing prices → II ✓.

**Answer:** **E) Both I and II are implicit**

> [!NOTE]
> "Either … or" in the statement does not make the answer "either I or II". The speaker presents both actions as workable, so both assumptions are implicit.
