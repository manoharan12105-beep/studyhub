# Statement and Assumption — Practice

Every question uses the same five options.

### P1. Statement: A notice at a swimming pool: "Children below 12 must be accompanied by an adult." Assumptions: I. Children below 12 may face risks in the pool without supervision. II. All adults are good swimmers.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Notice or instruction

- A) Only I is implicit
- B) Only II is implicit
- C) Either I or II is implicit
- D) Neither I nor II is implicit
- E) Both I and II are implicit

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** A) Only I is implicit

**Explanation:** The rule exists because unsupervised young children may be at risk. "All adults are good swimmers" is extreme and not needed — an adult can supervise without being an expert swimmer.

</details>

### P8. Statement: Despite the rise in fuel prices, the bus company has decided not to increase fares this year. Assumptions: I. The company can manage its costs this year without raising fares. II. Fuel prices will fall next year.

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Policy decision

- A) Only I is implicit
- B) Only II is implicit
- C) Either I or II is implicit
- D) Neither I nor II is implicit
- E) Both I and II are implicit

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** A) Only I is implicit

**Explanation:** A company would not commit to unchanged fares unless it could cope with the costs (I). The decision covers this year only and does not depend on next year's prices (II).

</details>

### P10. Statement: The city council has decided to plant 10,000 trees along the highway. Assumptions: I. The city has no parks. II. Trees along highways cause accidents.

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Policy decision

- A) Only I is implicit
- B) Only II is implicit
- C) Either I or II is implicit
- D) Neither I nor II is implicit
- E) Both I and II are implicit

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** D) Neither I nor II is implicit

**Explanation:** Planting trees along a highway does not require the city to lack parks (I). II would argue against the decision, so the council cannot be assuming it.

</details>
