# Blood Relations — Practice

### P1. A is the father of B. B is the sister of C. How is A related to C?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Direct chain

- A) Uncle
- B) Father
- C) Grandfather
- D) Brother

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) Father

**Explanation:** B and C are siblings, so they share the parent A.

</details>

### P2. X is the mother of Y. Y is the husband of Z. How is X related to Z?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Direct chain

- A) Mother
- B) Aunt
- C) Mother-in-law
- D) Sister-in-law

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) Mother-in-law

**Explanation:** X is the mother of Z's husband.

</details>

### P3. Q is the son of P's father's only brother. How is Q related to P?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Direct chain

- A) Brother
- B) Nephew
- C) Cousin
- D) Uncle

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) Cousin

**Explanation:** P's father's brother is P's paternal uncle; the uncle's son is P's cousin.

</details>

### P4. A is B's sister. C is B's mother. D is C's father. How is A related to D?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Direct chain

- A) Daughter
- B) Granddaughter
- C) Niece
- D) Grandmother

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) Granddaughter

**Explanation:** A is a child of C (as B's sister), and C is D's child → A is D's grandchild; A is female (sister).

</details>

### P5. Introducing a man, a woman said, "His wife is the only daughter of my father." How is the man related to the woman?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Pointing to a person

- A) Brother
- B) Husband
- C) Father-in-law
- D) Brother-in-law

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) Husband

**Explanation:** The woman is a daughter of her father; since he has only one daughter, that is her. So the man's wife is the woman herself.

</details>

### P6. Pointing to a photograph, Kiran, who is male and has no brothers, said, "This is the only son of my grandfather's only son." Whose photograph is it?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Pointing to a photo

- A) Kiran's father
- B) Kiran's brother
- C) Kiran's cousin
- D) Kiran himself

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** D) Kiran himself

**Explanation:** Grandfather's only son = Kiran's father. The only son of Kiran's father is Kiran, because Kiran is male and has no brothers.

</details>

### P7. P # Q means P is the father of Q; P @ Q means P is the wife of Q; P $ Q means P is the brother of Q. If M @ N # O $ R, how is M related to R?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Coded relations

- A) Sister
- B) Aunt
- C) Mother
- D) Grandmother

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) Mother

**Explanation:** M is N's wife; N is O's father; O is R's brother → R is also N's child. M is the wife of R's father → mother.

</details>

### P8. Pointing to a lady, a man said, "The son of her only brother is the brother of my wife." How is the lady related to the man?

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Pointing to a person

- A) Mother's sister
- B) Sister of his father-in-law
- C) Grandmother
- D) Mother-in-law

<details>
<summary>Hint</summary>

The son of her brother is the brother of the man's wife — so who is her brother to the man's wife?

</details>

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) Sister of his father-in-law

**Explanation:** The lady's brother's son is the brother of the man's wife → the lady's brother is the wife's father, i.e. the man's father-in-law. The lady is his father-in-law's sister.

</details>

### P9. In a family of six — A, B, C, D, E and F — there are two married couples. D is the grandmother of A and the mother of B. C is the wife of B and the mother of F. F is the granddaughter of E. How is A related to F?

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Family puzzles

- A) Brother
- B) Sister
- C) Brother or sister — gender not given
- D) Cousin

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) Brother or sister — gender not given

**Explanation:** Couples: B = C, and D = E (E is the other grandparent). A is D's grandchild through B; F is the child of B and C. So A and F are siblings. A's gender is never stated.

</details>

### P10. A + B means A is the son of B; A − B means A is the wife of B; A × B means A is the brother of B. If P × Q + R − S, how is P related to S?

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Coded relations

- A) Son
- B) Brother
- C) Nephew
- D) Son-in-law

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** A) Son

**Explanation:** P is Q's brother; Q is R's son → P is also R's son. R is S's wife → S is the husband of P's mother, i.e. P's father. P is S's son.

</details>
