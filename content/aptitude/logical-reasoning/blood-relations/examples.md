# Blood Relations — Solved Examples

### E1. Direct chain

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Direct chain

**Problem:** A is B's brother. B is C's mother. How is A related to C?

**Solution:**

1. B is C's mother → B is one generation above C.
2. A is B's brother → A is on B's level, male.
3. A is the brother of C's mother.

**Answer:** **Maternal uncle**

### E2. In-laws

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Direct chain

**Problem:** X is the husband of Y. Z is X's sister. How is Z related to Y?

**Solution:**

1. Z is the sister of Y's husband.
2. A spouse's sister is a sister-in-law.

**Answer:** **Sister-in-law**

### E3. Pointing to a person

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Pointing to a photo

**Problem:** Pointing to a woman, Arun said, "She is the daughter of my grandfather's only son." How is the woman related to Arun?

**Solution:**

1. Arun's grandfather's only son is Arun's father.
2. The woman is a daughter of Arun's father and is not Arun (Arun is pointing at her), so she is Arun's sister.

**Answer:** **Sister**

### E4. "Only daughter" points back to the speaker

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Pointing to a photo

**Problem:** Pointing to a man, Neha, a woman, said, "His mother is the only daughter of my mother." How is the man related to Neha?

**Solution:**

1. Neha is a woman, so she is a daughter of her mother.
2. Her mother has only one daughter → that daughter is Neha.
3. So the man's mother is Neha.

> [!WARNING]
> Without "a woman", the answer changes: a male speaker's mother's only daughter would be his sister, making the man his nephew.

**Answer:** **Son**

### E5. Coded relations

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Coded relations

**Problem:** P × Q means P is the brother of Q; P + Q means P is the mother of Q; P ÷ Q means P is the father of Q. What does A + B ÷ C × D mean?

**Solution:**

1. A + B → A is the mother of B.
2. B ÷ C → B is the father of C.
3. C × D → C is the brother of D, so D is also B's child.
4. A is the mother of D's father.

**Answer:** A is D's **paternal grandmother**.

### E6. Family puzzle

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Family puzzles

**Problem:** A is the grandfather of F. B is F's mother. C is A's wife. D is C's son and B's husband. E is D's sister. (a) How is E related to F? (b) How is C related to B?

**Solution:**

1. Couples: A = C and D = B.
2. D is the son of C (and A); B is D's wife; F is the child of D and B.
3. E is D's sister → E is the sister of F's father.

```text
      A(+) = C(−)
        │
   D(+) — E(−)
   D(+) = B(−)
        │
        F
```

4. (a) E is F's father's sister → paternal aunt.
5. (b) C is the mother of B's husband → mother-in-law.

**Answer:** (a) **Aunt (father's sister)** (b) **Mother-in-law**

### E7. Gender not given

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Direct chain

**Problem:** A is the son of B. B is the only sibling of C. How is C related to A?

**Solution:**

1. B is A's parent (gender of B not stated).
2. C is B's sibling → C is a sibling of A's parent.
3. C's gender is never given.

**Answer:** **Uncle or aunt — the exact relation cannot be determined** without C's gender.
