# Ranking and Order — Practice

### P1. Kavya is 15th from the left and 11th from the right in a row. How many people are in the row?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Total from two positions

- A) 24
- B) 25
- C) 26
- D) 27

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) 25

**Explanation:** 15 + 11 − 1 = 25. Option C forgets to subtract the double count.

</details>

### P2. In a class of 50, a student ranks 18th from the top. What is the student's rank from the bottom?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Position from the other end

- A) 31st
- B) 32nd
- C) 33rd
- D) 34th

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) 33rd

**Explanation:** 50 − 18 + 1 = 33.

</details>

### P3. A is taller than B. C is taller than A. D is shorter than B. Who is the shortest?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Comparison chains

- A) A
- B) B
- C) C
- D) D

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** D) D

**Explanation:** C > A > B > D.

</details>

### P4. In a row of 40 students, Raj is 12th from the left and Simran is 18th from the right. How many students are between them?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Number between

- A) 9
- B) 10
- C) 11
- D) 12

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) 10

**Explanation:** Simran from the left = 40 − 18 + 1 = 23. Between = 23 − 12 − 1 = 10.

</details>

</details>

### P6. A is 14th from the left and B is 17th from the right in a row. When they interchange positions, A becomes 22nd from the left. How many people are in the row?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Interchanging positions

- A) 36
- B) 37
- C) 38
- D) 39

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) 38

**Explanation:** A's new position (22nd from left) was B's old position, which was 17th from the right → 22 + 17 − 1 = 38.

</details>

### P7. P is older than Q but younger than R. S is younger than Q. T is older than R. Who is the third oldest?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Comparison chains

- A) R
- B) P
- C) Q
- D) T

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) P

**Explanation:** T > R > P > Q > S → third oldest is P.

</details>

### P8. In a queue, P is 8th from the front and Q is 10th from the back. There are 3 people between them. What is the minimum number of people in the queue?

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Minimum number

- A) 13
- B) 14
- C) 20
- D) 21

<details>
<summary>Hint</summary>

Try Q ahead of P as well as behind.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** A) 13

**Explanation:** Q behind P: Q is 12th from the front → 12 + 10 − 1 = 21. Q ahead of P: Q is 4th from the front → 4 + 10 − 1 = 13. Minimum = 13 (check: positions 5–7 are between Q and P).

</details>

### P9. In a row of girls, Rita is 12th from the left and Mona is 6th from the right. When they interchange positions, Rita becomes 15th from the left. How many girls are in the row?

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Interchanging positions

- A) 17
- B) 18
- C) 20
- D) 21

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) 20

**Explanation:** Mona's old position was 15th from the left and 6th from the right → 15 + 6 − 1 = 20.

</details>

### P10. Among the students who passed, Ravi ranks 7th from the top and 26th from the bottom. 6 students failed and 2 were absent. How many students are in the class?

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Excluded students

- A) 32
- B) 38
- C) 40
- D) 41

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) 40

**Explanation:** Passed = 7 + 26 − 1 = 32. Class = 32 + 6 + 2 = 40. Option A counts only those who passed.

</details>
