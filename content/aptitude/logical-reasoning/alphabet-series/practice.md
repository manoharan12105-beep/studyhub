# Alphabet Series — Practice

### P1. D, G, J, M, ?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Constant skip

- A) N
- B) O
- C) P
- D) Q

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) P

**Explanation:** 4, 7, 10, 13 → +3 → 16 → P.

</details>

### P3. Which letter is 7th from the right end of the English alphabet?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Position-based

- A) S
- B) T
- C) U
- D) G

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) T

**Explanation:** 27 − 7 = 20 → T. Option D is the 7th letter from the left.

</details>

### P5. BD, GI, LN, QS, ?

- B) VX

- D) WY

<summary>Answer</summary>

</details>

### P6. AB, DEF, HIJK, ?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Letter groups

- A) LMNOP
- B) MNOPQ
- C) LMNO
- D) MNOP

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) MNOPQ

**Explanation:** Groups grow by one letter (2, 3, 4 → 5) and one letter is skipped between groups: AB (skip C) DEF (skip G) HIJK (skip L) MNOPQ.

</details>

### P8. ACE, BDF, CEG, ?

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Letter groups

- A) DFG
- B) DEH
- C) DFH
- D) EFH

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) DFH

**Explanation:** Each letter moves +1 between groups: A→B→C→D, C→D→E→F, E→F→G→H. Inside each group, letters skip one (x, x + 2, x + 4).

</details>

### P9. FAG, GAF, HAI, IAH, ?

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Letter groups

- A) JAK
- B) KAJ
- C) JAI
- D) IAJ

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** A) JAK

**Explanation:** The terms come in pairs: FAG and GAF use F and G (then swapped); HAI and IAH use H and I. The next pair uses J and K, starting with the smaller letter: JAK.

</details>

### P10. Which letter is 5th to the right of the 9th letter from the right end of the alphabet?

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Position-based

- A) V
- B) W
- C) X
- D) M

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) W

**Explanation:** 9th from the right = 27 − 9 = 18th from the left (R). 5th to its right = 18 + 5 = 23 → W. Option C counts R itself as the first step.

</details>
