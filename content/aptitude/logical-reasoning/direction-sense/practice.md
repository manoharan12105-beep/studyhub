# Direction Sense — Practice

### P1. A person is facing west. He turns left. Which direction is he facing now?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Final facing direction

- A) North
- B) South
- C) East
- D) North-west

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) South

**Explanation:** Left is anticlockwise: West → South.

</details>

### P2. Meera walks 4 km north, then turns right and walks 3 km. How far is she from her starting point?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Distance and direction

- A) 3 km
- B) 5 km
- C) 7 km
- D) 1 km

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) 5 km

**Explanation:** Net displacement (3, 4) → √(9 + 16) = 5 km. Option C adds the path lengths.

</details>

### P3. Which direction is exactly opposite to north-east?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Directions

- A) North-west
- B) South-east
- C) South-west
- D) South

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) South-west

**Explanation:** Opposite of north is south and of east is west.

</details>

### P4. A man walks 20 m south, turns left and walks 15 m, then turns left again and walks 20 m. Where is he now relative to the start?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Distance and direction

- A) 15 m west
- B) 15 m east
- C) 25 m south-east
- D) 20 m north

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) 15 m east

**Explanation:** South 20 → (0, −20). Facing south, left = east → (15, −20). Facing east, left = north; 20 m → (15, 0).

</details>

### P5. Arjun walks 6 km west, then turns right and walks 8 km. How far and in which direction is he from the start?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Distance and direction

- A) 10 km north-east
- B) 14 km north-west
- C) 10 km north-west
- D) 10 km south-west

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) 10 km north-west

**Explanation:** West 6 → (−6, 0). Facing west, right = north → (−6, 8). Distance √(36 + 64) = 10; x < 0, y > 0 → north-west.

</details>

### P6. A boy is facing west. He turns 135° anticlockwise. Which direction is he facing now?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Rotation by angles

- A) North-east
- B) South-east
- C) South-west
- D) North-west

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) South-east

**Explanation:** West = 270°. Anticlockwise subtracts: 270 − 135 = 135° → south-east.

</details>

### P7. B is 10 m west of A. C is 10 m north of A. In which direction is C from B?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Relative positions

- A) North
- B) North-east
- C) North-west
- D) East

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) North-east

**Explanation:** B = (0, 0), A = (10, 0), C = (10, 10). From B, C is 10 east and 10 north → north-east.

</details>

### P8. Sunil walks 15 m north, turns right and walks 10 m, turns right and walks 25 m, then turns right and walks 10 m. Where is he relative to the start?

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Distance and direction

- A) 10 m north
- B) 10 m south
- C) 25 m south
- D) At the start

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) 10 m south

**Explanation:** (0, 15) → right = east 10 → (10, 15) → right = south 25 → (10, −10) → right = west 10 → (0, −10).

</details>

### P9. One evening, Raj stands so that his shadow falls exactly to his left. Which direction is he facing?

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Shadows

- A) North
- B) South
- C) East
- D) West

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) South

**Explanation:** Evening → sun in the west → shadows point east. His left is east → he faces south.

</details>

### P10. A woman walks 5 km north, turns right and walks 4 km, then turns right again and walks 8 km. How far and in which direction is she from the start?

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Distance and direction

- A) 5 km south-east
- B) 5 km north-east
- C) 3 km south
- D) 17 km south-east

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** A) 5 km south-east

**Explanation:** (0, 5) → right = east 4 → (4, 5) → right = south 8 → (4, −3). Distance √(16 + 9) = 5; x > 0, y < 0 → south-east.

</details>
