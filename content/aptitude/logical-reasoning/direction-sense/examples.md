# Direction Sense — Solved Examples

### E1. Final facing direction

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Final facing direction

**Problem:** A man is facing north. He turns right, then right again, then left. Which direction is he facing now?

**Solution:**

1. North → right → East.
2. East → right → South.
3. South → left → East.

**Answer:** **East**

### E2. Back-and-forth path

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Distance and direction

**Problem:** Priya walks 10 m north, turns right and walks 6 m, then turns right again and walks 10 m. Where is she relative to her starting point?

**Solution:**

1. North 10 → (0, 10).
2. Facing north, right = east; 6 m → (6, 10).
3. Facing east, right = south; 10 m → (6, 0).

**Answer:** **6 m east** of the start

### E3. Pythagorean distance

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Distance and direction

**Problem:** A person walks 3 km east and then turns left and walks 4 km. How far and in which direction is the person from the start?

**Solution:**

1. East 3 → (3, 0). Facing east, left = north; 4 km → (3, 4).
2. Distance = √(3² + 4²) = 5 km.
3. x > 0, y > 0 → north-east.

**Answer:** **5 km, north-east**

### E4. Rotation by angles

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Rotation by angles

**Problem:** A girl faces north. She turns 90° clockwise and then 135° anticlockwise. Which direction is she facing?

**Solution:**

1. North = 0°. +90° → 90° (east).
2. −135° → −45° = 315° → north-west.

**Answer:** **North-west**

### E5. Multi-step path

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Distance and direction

**Problem:** Ravi walks 10 km south, turns left and walks 6 km, turns left and walks 2 km, turns right and walks 4 km, then turns left and walks 8 km. How far and in which direction is he from the start?

**Solution:**

| Move | Facing | Position |
|------|--------|----------|
| 10 km | South | (0, −10) |
| left, 6 km | East | (6, −10) |
| left, 2 km | North | (6, −8) |
| right, 4 km | East | (10, −8) |
| left, 8 km | North | (10, 0) |

**Answer:** **10 km east** of the start

### E6. Shadows

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Shadows

**Problem:** One morning, A and B stand facing each other. A's shadow falls exactly to B's right. Which direction is B facing?

**Solution:**

1. Morning → sun in the east → shadows fall towards the west.
2. The shadow is on B's right → B's right is west.
3. A person whose right is west is facing south.

**Answer:** **South**
