# Clocks — Practice

### P1. What is the angle between the hands of a clock at 6:00?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Angle at a time

- A) 90°
- B) 120°
- C) 150°
- D) 180°

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** D) 180°

**Explanation:** 30 × 6 = 180°.

</details>

### P2. What is the angle between the hands at 9:00?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Angle at a time

- A) 90°
- B) 180°
- C) 270°
- D) 45°

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** A) 90°

**Explanation:** 30 × 9 = 270°, which is more than 180° → 360 − 270 = 90°.

</details>

### P4. What is the angle between the hands at 2:30?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Angle at a time

- A) 90°
- B) 100°
- C) 105°
- D) 120°

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) 105°

**Explanation:** |60 − 5.5 × 30| = |60 − 165| = 105°. Option D ignores the hour hand's movement (it is halfway between 2 and 3).

</details>

### P8. At what time between 7 and 8 o'clock do the hands coincide?

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Time for an angle

- A) 35 minutes past 7
- B) 38 2/11 minutes past 7
- C) 36 4/11 minutes past 7
- D) 38 minutes past 7

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) 38 2/11 minutes past 7

**Explanation:** M = (2/11)(30 × 7) = 420/11 = 38 2/11. Option A assumes the hour hand stays on the 7.

</details>

### P9. At what time between 7 and 8 o'clock are the hands exactly opposite each other?

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Time for an angle

- A) 5 5/11 minutes past 7
- B) 5 minutes past 7
- C) 10 10/11 minutes past 7
- D) 54 6/11 minutes past 7

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** A) 5 5/11 minutes past 7

**Explanation:** M = (2/11)(210 ± 180). With −: 60/11 = 5 5/11 ✓. With +: 780/11 ≈ 70.9 > 60 — not between 7 and 8.

</details>

### P10. What is the angle between the hands at 10:25?

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Angle at a time

- A) 152.5°
- B) 157.5°
- C) 162.5°
- D) 197.5°

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) 162.5°

**Explanation:** |300 − 5.5 × 25| = |300 − 137.5| = 162.5° (already below 180°). Option D is 360° − 162.5°.

</details>

### P11. A clock gains 4 minutes every hour. It is set right at 8 a.m. What is the correct time when it shows 2 p.m. the same day?

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Fast and slow clocks

- A) 1:36 p.m.
- B) 1:37:30 p.m.
- C) 1:40 p.m.
- D) 2:24 p.m.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) 1:37:30 p.m.

**Explanation:** Real time = 360 × 60/64 = 337.5 minutes = 5 h 37.5 min after 8 a.m. Option A subtracts 6 × 4 = 24 minutes, which assumes 6 real hours have passed — but only 6 clock hours have.

</details>
