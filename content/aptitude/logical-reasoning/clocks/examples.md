# Clocks — Solved Examples

### E1. Angle on the hour

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Angle at a time

**Problem:** What is the angle between the hands at 3:00?

**Solution:**

1. |30 × 3 − 5.5 × 0| = 90°.

**Answer:** **90°**

### E2. Angle at a general time

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Angle at a time

**Problem:** What is the angle between the hands at 3:40?

**Solution:**

1. |30 × 3 − 5.5 × 40| = |90 − 220| = 130°.
2. 130° ≤ 180°, so it stands.

**Answer:** **130°**

### E3. Hands coinciding

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Time for an angle

**Problem:** At what time between 4 and 5 o'clock do the hands coincide?

**Solution:**

1. θ = 0 → M = (2/11)(30 × 4) = 240/11.
2. 240/11 = 21 9/11.

**Answer:** **21 9/11 minutes past 4**

### E4. Right angle

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Time for an angle

**Problem:** At what time between 2 and 3 o'clock are the hands at right angles?

**Solution:**

1. M = (2/11)(60 ± 90).
2. With +: (2/11)(150) = 300/11 = 27 3/11 minutes ✓.
3. With −: (2/11)(−30) < 0 — not valid.

**Answer:** **27 3/11 minutes past 2**

### E5. Mirror image

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Mirror image

**Problem:** A clock shows 3:40. What time does its mirror image show? What is the angle between the hands in the image?

**Solution:**

1. 11:60 − 3:40 = 8:20.
2. Angle at 8:20 = |240 − 110| = 130° — the same as at 3:40 (see E2), as expected for a mirror image.

**Answer:** **8:20**, angle **130°**

### E6. Fast clock

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Fast and slow clocks

**Problem:** A clock gains 4 minutes every hour. It is set right at 8 a.m. What is the correct time when the clock shows 2 p.m. the same day?

**Solution:**

1. The clock shows 6 hours = 360 minutes elapsed.
2. For every 60 real minutes it shows 64 → real time = 360 × 60/64 = 337.5 minutes = 5 h 37.5 min.
3. 8 a.m. + 5 h 37 min 30 s = 1:37:30 p.m.

**Answer:** **1:37:30 p.m.**
