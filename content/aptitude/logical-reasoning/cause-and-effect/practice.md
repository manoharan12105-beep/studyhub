# Cause and Effect — Practice

Every question uses the same five options.

### P1. I. The power supply in the market area was cut for six hours. II. Many shops in the market ran their generators.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Natural events and consequences

- A) I is the cause, II its effect
- B) II is the cause, I its effect
- C) Both are independent causes
- D) Both are effects of independent causes
- E) Both are effects of some common cause

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** A) I is the cause, II its effect

**Explanation:** Shops ran generators because the power was cut.

</details>

### P9. I. Fruit prices in the city market rose this month. II. Vegetable prices in the city market rose this month.

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Simultaneous price rises

- A) I is the cause, II its effect
- B) II is the cause, I its effect
- C) Both are independent causes
- D) Both are effects of independent causes
- E) Both are effects of some common cause

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** E) Both are effects of some common cause

**Explanation:** Fruit prices do not drive vegetable prices or vice versa; a shared reason (for example, higher transport costs or a supply disruption) explains both.

</details>
