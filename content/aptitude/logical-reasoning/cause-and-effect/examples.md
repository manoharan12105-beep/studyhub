# Cause and Effect — Solved Examples

Options used throughout: A) I is the cause, II its effect · B) II is the cause, I its effect · C) Both are independent causes · D) Both are effects of independent causes · E) Both are effects of some common cause.

### E1. Cause stated first

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Natural events and consequences

**Problem:** I. Heavy rain lashed the city all night. II. Several low-lying areas were waterlogged in the morning.

**Solution:**

1. Waterlogging in the morning happened because of the night-long rain.
2. Time order: rain (night) → waterlogging (morning).

**Answer:** **A) I is the cause, II its effect**

### E2. Effect stated first

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Natural events and consequences

**Problem:** I. The prices of vegetables have risen sharply. II. Unseasonal rain destroyed crops in many states.

**Solution:**

1. Prices rose because crop destruction reduced supply.
2. The cause is statement II.

**Answer:** **B) II is the cause, I its effect**

### E3. Common cause

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Simultaneous closures

**Problem:** I. Schools and colleges in the city were closed today. II. Government offices in the city were closed today.

**Solution:**

1. Schools did not close because offices closed, nor the reverse.
2. Both are naturally explained by one event — a public holiday or a city-wide shutdown.

**Answer:** **E) Both are effects of some common cause**

### E4. Unrelated events

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Unrelated news

**Problem:** I. The government has increased the price of cooking gas. II. Many students in the city performed poorly in the board exams.

**Solution:**

1. Neither event explains the other.
2. No single plausible event explains both.

**Answer:** **D) Both are effects of independent causes**

### E5. Business decision

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Policy and response

**Problem:** I. The company announced a 20% bonus for all its employees. II. The company made record profits this year.

**Solution:**

1. Test "I because of II": the bonus was announced because profits were high — natural.
2. Test "II because of I": profits caused by a bonus — not natural.

**Answer:** **B) II is the cause, I its effect**

### E6. Weather and transport

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Natural events and consequences

**Problem:** I. Several flights were cancelled at the city airport. II. Dense fog covered the city in the early morning.

**Solution:**

1. Flights are cancelled when visibility is poor; fog does not result from cancellations.
2. The fog (II) caused the cancellations (I).

**Answer:** **B) II is the cause, I its effect**
