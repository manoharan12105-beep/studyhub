# Statement and Conclusion — Solved Examples

Options used throughout: A) Only I follows · B) Only II follows · C) Either I or II follows · D) Neither I nor II follows · E) Both I and II follow.

### E1. Reason in the statement

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Reason given

**Problem:**
Statement: The city's air pollution has doubled in the last five years, mainly because of the increase in vehicles.
Conclusions: I. The number of vehicles in the city has increased in the last five years. II. Pollution will double again in the next five years.

**Solution:**

1. The statement gives the increase in vehicles as the main reason → I is stated directly ✓.
2. II predicts the future; nothing in the statement guarantees it ✗.

**Answer:** **A) Only I follows**

### E2. "Only" statement

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** "Only" statements

**Problem:**
Statement: Only those who pass the written test will be called for the interview.
Conclusions: I. Everyone who passes the written test will be called for the interview. II. No one who fails the written test will be called for the interview.

**Solution:**

1. "Only those who pass will be called" means being called requires passing. It does not promise that every passer is called → I ✗.
2. Failing means not meeting the requirement → not called → II ✓.

**Answer:** **B) Only II follows**

### E3. One-way rule

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Rule applied to a case

**Problem:**
Statement: Twelve students scored above 90%, and all of them were awarded scholarships. Ravi was awarded a scholarship.
Conclusions: I. Ravi scored above 90%. II. Some students who were awarded scholarships scored above 90%.

**Solution:**

1. The rule says scorers above 90% got scholarships; it does not say only they did. Ravi may have got one for another reason → I ✗.
2. The twelve high scorers all received scholarships → some scholarship holders scored above 90% → II ✓.

**Answer:** **B) Only II follows**

### E4. Neither follows

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Added information

**Problem:**
Statement: The company's profits fell this year.
Conclusions: I. The company will shut down. II. The company's sales fell this year.

**Solution:**

1. I is a prediction not supported by the statement ✗.
2. Profits can fall even when sales rise (for example, if costs rise) → II ✗.

**Answer:** **D) Neither I nor II follows**

### E5. Both follow

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Restatement

**Problem:**
Statement: Regular exercise improves heart health, and doctors recommend at least 30 minutes of exercise every day.
Conclusions: I. Exercise is beneficial for the heart. II. Doctors consider daily exercise important.

**Solution:**

1. I restates the first part ✓.
2. Recommending daily exercise shows doctors consider it important ✓.

**Answer:** **E) Both I and II follow**

### E6. Either–or

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Either–or

**Problem:**
Statement: Raju will go either to Delhi or to Mumbai for his vacation this year, but not to both.
Conclusions: I. Raju will go to Delhi. II. Raju will go to Mumbai.

**Solution:**

1. Neither destination is certain on its own.
2. Exactly one of them must happen.

**Answer:** **C) Either I or II follows**
