# Statement and Conclusion — Practice

Every question uses the same five options.

### P1. Statement: All employees of the firm get a bonus at the end of the year. Meena is an employee of the firm. Conclusions: I. Meena gets a bonus at the end of the year. II. The firm is making losses.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Rule applied to a case

- A) Only I follows
- B) Only II follows
- C) Either I or II follows
- D) Neither I nor II follows
- E) Both I and II follow

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** A) Only I follows

**Explanation:** The rule applied to Meena gives I. Nothing in the statement concerns losses.

</details>

### P2. Statement: The train was cancelled because of heavy rain. Conclusions: I. It rained heavily. II. Trains are always cancelled when it rains.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Reason given

- A) Only I follows
- B) Only II follows
- C) Either I or II follows
- D) Neither I nor II follows
- E) Both I and II follow

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** A) Only I follows

**Explanation:** The stated reason confirms I. II generalises with "always" from a single event.

</details>

### P3. Statement: Smoking is injurious to health. Conclusions: I. Non-smokers are always healthy. II. Smoking can harm a person's health.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Restatement

- A) Only I follows
- B) Only II follows
- C) Either I or II follows
- D) Neither I nor II follows
- E) Both I and II follow

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) Only II follows

**Explanation:** II restates the statement. I uses "always" and says something about non-smokers that the statement does not.

</details>

### P4. Statement: Most of the students in the class passed the exam. Conclusions: I. Some students in the class passed the exam. II. All the students in the class passed the exam.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Percentages and "most"

- A) Only I follows
- B) Only II follows
- C) Either I or II follows
- D) Neither I nor II follows
- E) Both I and II follow

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** A) Only I follows

**Explanation:** "Most" means more than half, so some passed. It does not mean all.

</details>

### P5. Statement: The new metro line has reduced travel time between the two suburbs from 90 minutes to 30 minutes. Conclusions: I. The metro line connects the two suburbs. II. Everyone travelling between the suburbs now uses the metro.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Added information

- A) Only I follows
- B) Only II follows
- C) Either I or II follows
- D) Neither I nor II follows
- E) Both I and II follow

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** A) Only I follows

**Explanation:** Reducing travel time between the suburbs means the line connects them. "Everyone" is not supported.

</details>

### P7. Statement: The price of onions has risen sharply after floods destroyed crops in the major onion-growing regions. Conclusions: I. Floods affected onion crops. II. The supply of onions has fallen.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Direct consequence

- A) Only I follows
- B) Only II follows
- C) Either I or II follows
- D) Neither I nor II follows
- E) Both I and II follow

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** E) Both I and II follow

**Explanation:** The floods destroyed crops in the onion-growing regions (I). Destroyed crops directly reduce the supply of onions (II), which is the stated cause of the price rise.

</details>

### P9. Statement: Raju will spend his vacation either in Delhi or in Mumbai, but not in both. Conclusions: I. Raju will spend his vacation in Delhi. II. Raju will spend his vacation in Mumbai.

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Either–or

- A) Only I follows
- B) Only II follows
- C) Either I or II follows
- D) Neither I nor II follows
- E) Both I and II follow

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) Either I or II follows

**Explanation:** Neither is certain alone, but exactly one must be true.

</details>

### P10. Statement: Every candidate who scored above 75 in the test was shortlisted. Kiran was not shortlisted. Conclusions: I. Kiran did not score above 75 in the test. II. Kiran did not take the test.

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Rule applied to a case

- A) Only I follows
- B) Only II follows
- C) Either I or II follows
- D) Neither I nor II follows
- E) Both I and II follow

<details>
<summary>Hint</summary>

If Kiran had scored above 75, what would have happened?

</details>

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** A) Only I follows

**Explanation:** Scoring above 75 guarantees shortlisting; Kiran was not shortlisted, so Kiran did not score above 75. Kiran may have taken the test and scored lower, so II is not certain.

</details>

### P11. Statement: Many people in the town have started using solar panels. Conclusions: I. Electricity from the grid is expensive in the town. II. The town gets plenty of sunshine throughout the year.

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Added information

- A) Only I follows
- B) Only II follows
- C) Either I or II follows
- D) Neither I nor II follows
- E) Both I and II follow

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** D) Neither I nor II follows

**Explanation:** Both are possible reasons for using solar panels, but neither is stated or forced by the statement.

</details>
