# Alphanumeric Series — Practice

### P1. A2, C4, E6, G8, ?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Letter–number pairs

- A) H10
- B) I10
- C) I9
- D) J10

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) I10

**Explanation:** Letters +2 (G → I); numbers +2 (8 → 10).

</details>

### P4. C2, F5, J9, O14, ?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Different rules

- A) T19
- B) U20
- C) U21
- D) V20

<details>

### P5. Which element is 6th to the right of the 8th element from the left?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Position from both ends

- A) W
- B) 9
- C) L
- D) 3

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) 9

**Explanation:** 8th from the left is position 8 (Q); 8 + 6 = 14 → 9.

</details>

### P6. Which element is 5th to the left of the 7th element from the right?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Position from both ends

- A) 7
- B) #
- C) 3
- D) E

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) #

**Explanation:** 7th from the right = 22 − 7 + 1 = 16 (&). 16 − 5 = 11 → #.

</details>

### P7. How many numbers are immediately followed by a letter?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Neighbour conditions

- A) 3
- B) 4
- C) 5
- D) 6

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) 5

**Explanation:** 5 → K ✓, 8 → B ✓, 2 → @ ✗, 7 → # ✗, 3 → W ✓, 9 → L ✓, 4 → H ✓, 1 → * ✗, 6 → (end) ✗. Total 5.

</details>

### P8. How many symbols are immediately preceded by a letter?

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Neighbour conditions

- A) 1
- B) 2
- C) 3
- D) 4

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) 2

**Explanation:** % (K before ✓), @ (2 before ✗), # (7 before ✗), & (L before ✓), * (1 before ✗). Total 2.

</details>

### P9. How many letters are immediately preceded by a number and immediately followed by a number?

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Neighbour conditions

- A) 1
- B) 2
- C) 3
- D) 4

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) 3

**Explanation:** B (8, 2) ✓; W (3, 9) ✓; H (4, 1) ✓. K, Q, E, L and Z fail at least one side. Total 3.

</details>

### P10. If all the numbers are removed, which element is 4th from the left?

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Removing a category

- A) B
- B) @
- C) Q
- D) %

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) @

**Explanation:** Without numbers: K % B @ Q E # W L & H * Z. The 4th from the left is @.

</details>
