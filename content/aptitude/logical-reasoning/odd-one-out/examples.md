# Odd One Out — Solved Examples

### E1. Category

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Category misfit

**Problem:** Mercury, Venus, Moon, Mars

**Solution:**

1. Mercury, Venus and Mars are planets.
2. The Moon is a natural satellite of Earth.

**Answer:** **Moon**

### E2. Primes

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Number property

**Problem:** 2, 3, 5, 9, 11

**Solution:**

1. 2, 3, 5 and 11 are prime.
2. 9 = 3 × 3 is composite.

**Answer:** **9**

### E3. Letter gaps

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Letter gaps

**Problem:** BD, FH, JL, NQ

**Solution:**

1. Positions: B 2 → D 4 (+2); F 6 → H 8 (+2); J 10 → L 12 (+2); N 14 → Q 17 (+3).
2. NQ breaks the +2 gap.

**Answer:** **NQ**

### E4. Pair relationship

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Pair relationship

**Problem:** Car : Garage, Aeroplane : Hangar, Horse : Stable, Ship : Sea

**Solution:**

1. A car is kept in a garage; an aeroplane in a hangar; a horse in a stable.
2. A ship travels on the sea; it is kept in a dock.

**Answer:** **Ship : Sea**

### E5. Cubes

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Number property

**Problem:** 8, 27, 64, 100, 125

**Solution:**

1. 8 = 2³, 27 = 3³, 64 = 4³, 125 = 5³.
2. 100 = 10² is a square, not a cube.

**Answer:** **100**

> [!NOTE]
> 64 is both a square and a cube, so "squares" cannot be the property — only "cubes" isolates exactly one item.

### E6. Letter groups

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Letter gaps

**Problem:** AEI, BFJ, CGK, DHM

**Solution:**

1. AEI: 1, 5, 9 (+4, +4). BFJ: 2, 6, 10. CGK: 3, 7, 11.
2. DHM: 4, 8, 13 (+4, +5) — DHL would fit.

**Answer:** **DHM**
