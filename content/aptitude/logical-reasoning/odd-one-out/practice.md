# Odd One Out — Practice

### P1. Find the odd one out.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Category misfit

- A) Rose
- B) Lily
- C) Lotus
- D) Mango

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** D) Mango

**Explanation:** Rose, lily and lotus are flowers; mango is a fruit.

</details>

### P2. Find the odd one out.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Number property

- A) 10
- B) 15
- C) 23
- D) 25

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) 23

**Explanation:** 10, 15 and 25 are multiples of 5; 23 is not.

</details>

### P6. Find the odd one out.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Letter gaps

- A) CF
- B) GJ
- C) KN
- D) OS

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** D) OS

**Explanation:** C → F, G → J and K → N are all +3; O (15) → S (19) is +4.

</details>

### P7. Find the odd pair.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Pair relationship

- A) Eye : See
- B) Ear : Hear
- C) Nose : Smell
- D) Hand : Glove

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** D) Hand : Glove

**Explanation:** The others are organ : function. A glove is something worn on the hand, not its function.

</details>

### P8. Find the odd one out.

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Number property

- A) 27
- B) 64
- C) 144
- D) 216

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) 144

**Explanation:** 27 = 3³, 64 = 4³, 216 = 6³ are cubes; 144 = 12² is not a cube. (64 is also a square, so "squares" would not isolate one item.)

</details>

### P9. Find the odd one out.

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Number property

- A) 13
- B) 17
- C) 21
- D) 23

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) 21

**Explanation:** 13, 17 and 23 are prime; 21 = 3 × 7.

</details>

### P10. Find the odd one out.

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Letter gaps

- A) ACE
- B) BDF
- C) CEG
- D) DFI

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** D) DFI

**Explanation:** Each group should go +2, +2: ACE, BDF, CEG. DFI goes D → F (+2) but F → I (+3); DFH would fit.

</details>

### P11. Find the odd one out.

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Composition

- A) Brass
- B) Bronze
- C) Steel
- D) Copper

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** D) Copper

**Explanation:** Brass, bronze and steel are alloys; copper is a pure metal (an element).

</details>
