# Data Sufficiency — Practice

Every question uses the same five options.

### P1. What is Rahul's age? I. Rahul is 5 years older than Seema. II. Seema is 20 years old.

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Arithmetic word problems

- A) I alone is sufficient, but II alone is not
- B) II alone is sufficient, but I alone is not
- C) Each statement alone is sufficient
- D) Both together are sufficient, but neither alone is
- E) Both together are not sufficient

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** D) Both together are sufficient, but neither alone is

**Explanation:** I needs Seema's age; II says nothing about Rahul. Together: 20 + 5 = 25.

</details>

### P4. n is an integer. Is n divisible by 6? I. n is divisible by 3. II. n is divisible by 2.

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Divisibility and parity

- A) I alone is sufficient, but II alone is not
- B) II alone is sufficient, but I alone is not
- C) Each statement alone is sufficient
- D) Both together are sufficient, but neither alone is
- E) Both together are not sufficient

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** D) Both together are sufficient, but neither alone is

**Explanation:** 3 alone: 9 (no) or 12 (yes). 2 alone: 4 (no) or 12 (yes). Together, divisible by 2 and 3 → divisible by 6 → definite yes.

</details>

### P8. Is x positive? I. x³ > 0. II. x² > 0.

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Squares and signs

- A) I alone is sufficient, but II alone is not
- B) II alone is sufficient, but I alone is not
- C) Each statement alone is sufficient
- D) Both together are sufficient, but neither alone is
- E) Both together are not sufficient

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** A) I alone is sufficient, but II alone is not

**Explanation:** A cube keeps the sign: x³ > 0 → x > 0 (definite yes). x² > 0 only says x ≠ 0; x could be 2 or −2.

</details>

### P9. What is the value of a + b? I. a² − b² = 24. II. a − b = 4.

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Equations

- A) I alone is sufficient, but II alone is not
- B) II alone is sufficient, but I alone is not
- C) Each statement alone is sufficient
- D) Both together are sufficient, but neither alone is
- E) Both together are not sufficient

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** D) Both together are sufficient, but neither alone is

**Explanation:** a² − b² = (a + b)(a − b). Together: (a + b) × 4 = 24 → a + b = 6. Alone, neither fixes a + b.

</details>

### P10. What is the profit percentage on an article? I. Its cost price is ₹400. II. Its selling price is 25% more than its cost price.

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Arithmetic word problems

- A) I alone is sufficient, but II alone is not
- B) II alone is sufficient, but I alone is not
- C) Each statement alone is sufficient
- D) Both together are sufficient, but neither alone is
- E) Both together are not sufficient

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) II alone is sufficient, but I alone is not

**Explanation:** Profit % is calculated on CP, so SP = 1.25 × CP means a 25% profit whatever the CP. I alone gives no SP. Choosing D is the common trap — the actual CP is not needed.

</details>

### P11. What is the value of x? I. x + y = 10. II. 2x + 2y = 20.
