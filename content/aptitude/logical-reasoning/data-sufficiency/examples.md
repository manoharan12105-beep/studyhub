# Data Sufficiency — Solved Examples

Options used throughout: A) I alone sufficient · B) II alone sufficient · C) Each alone sufficient · D) Both together needed · E) Not sufficient even together.

### E1. A square hides the sign

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Squares and signs

**Problem:** What is the value of x? I. 2x + 3 = 11. II. x² = 16.

**Solution:**

1. I alone: x = 4 — unique ✓.
2. II alone: x = 4 or −4 — not unique ✗.

**Answer:** **A**

### E2. Parity

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Divisibility and parity

**Problem:** n is an integer. Is n even? I. n² is even. II. n + 1 is odd.

**Solution:**

1. I alone: if n were odd, n² would be odd. So n is even → definite "yes" ✓.
2. II alone: n + 1 odd → n even → definite "yes" ✓.

**Answer:** **C**

### E3. Two equations

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Equations

**Problem:** What is the price of one pen? I. 3 pens and 2 pencils cost ₹40. II. 2 pens and 3 pencils cost ₹35.

**Solution:**

1. Each statement alone has two unknowns ✗.
2. Together: 3p + 2q = 40 and 2p + 3q = 35 are independent → unique p (₹10) ✓.

**Answer:** **D**

### E4. Relation

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Reasoning data

**Problem:** How is A related to B? I. A is the son of C. II. C is the mother of B.

**Solution:**

1. I alone says nothing about B ✗. II alone says nothing about A ✗.
2. Together: A and B are both C's children; A is male → A is B's brother ✓.

**Answer:** **D**

### E5. Rectangle

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Equations

**Problem:** What is the area of a rectangle? I. Its perimeter is 20 cm. II. Its diagonal is √52 cm.

**Solution:**

1. I alone: l + b = 10 — many rectangles ✗. II alone: l² + b² = 52 — many rectangles ✗.
2. Together: (l + b)² = l² + b² + 2lb → 100 = 52 + 2lb → lb = 24 — unique ✓.

**Answer:** **D**

### E6. Yes/no with signs

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Squares and signs

**Problem:** Is x > y? (x and y are real numbers.) I. x² > y². II. x > 0.

**Solution:**

1. I alone: x = 3, y = 1 → yes; x = −3, y = 1 → no ✗.
2. II alone: says nothing about y ✗.
3. Together: x > 0 and x² > y² → x = |x| > |y| ≥ y → always yes ✓.

**Answer:** **D**

### E7. Duplicate information

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Equations

**Problem:** What is the value of x? I. x − y = 2. II. 3x − 3y = 6.

**Solution:**

1. II is I multiplied by 3 — the same information.
2. One equation in two unknowns → x is not fixed, alone or together ✗.

**Answer:** **E**
