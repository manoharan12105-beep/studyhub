# Puzzles — Solved Examples

### E1. Scheduling

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Scheduling

**Problem:** Four lectures — Maths, Physics, Chemistry and Biology — are held on Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday, one per day. Physics is on the day after Maths. Biology is not on Monday. Chemistry is on Thursday. On which day is Biology?

**Solution:**

1. Chemistry = Thursday.
2. Maths and Physics are consecutive within Monday–Wednesday: (Mon, Tue) or (Tue, Wed).
3. If Maths = Tue and Physics = Wed, Biology must be Monday — not allowed.
4. So Maths = Mon, Physics = Tue, Biology = Wed.

**Answer:** **Wednesday**

### E2. Attribute matching

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Attribute matching

**Problem:** Asha, Bala and Chitra each have a different favourite colour (red, blue, green) and a different profession (doctor, engineer, teacher).

- Asha does not like red and is not a teacher.
- The engineer likes blue.
- Bala likes green.
- Chitra is not a doctor.

What is Bala's profession?

**Solution:**

1. Bala = green. Asha is not red → Asha = blue → Chitra = red.
2. The engineer likes blue → Asha = engineer.
3. Chitra is not a doctor → Chitra = teacher → Bala = doctor.
4. Check: Asha is not a teacher ✓.

**Answer:** **Doctor**

### E3. Floor puzzle

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Floor puzzle

**Problem:** Five people A, B, C, D and E live on floors 1 to 5 of a building (floor 1 is the lowest), one person per floor.

- C lives on an even-numbered floor.
- A lives immediately above C.
- D lives on floor 1.
- B lives on a floor above A.
- E lives on an odd-numbered floor.

Who lives on the top floor, and who lives immediately below B?

**Solution:**

1. C is on floor 2 or 4.
2. Case C = 4 → A = 5 → no floor above A for B ✗.
3. So C = 2 and A = 3. D = 1.
4. B is above A → floor 4 or 5. E is on an odd floor and only 5 remains odd → E = 5, B = 4.
5. Bottom to top: D, C, A, B, E. Check every clue ✓.

**Answer:** **E** is on the top floor; **A** lives immediately below B.
