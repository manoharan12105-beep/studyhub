# Puzzles — Practice

Questions come in sets that share one puzzle. Solve the puzzle first, then answer.

## Set 1: Floor puzzle

Six people P, Q, R, S, T and U live on floors 1 to 6 of a building (floor 1 is the lowest), one per floor.

- Q lives on floor 4.
- Exactly one person lives between Q and T, and T lives above Q.
- R lives on floor 1.
- U does not live immediately above or immediately below Q.
- S lives on a floor below P.

### P1. Who lives on the top floor?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Floor puzzle

- A) P
- B) S
- C) T
- D) U

<details>
<summary>Hint</summary>

One person between Q (floor 4) and T, with T above, fixes T's floor.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) T

**Explanation:** T is two floors above Q → floor 6. R = 1. U cannot be on 3 or 5 → U = 2. P and S take 3 and 5 with S below P → S = 3, P = 5. Bottom to top: R, U, S, Q, P, T.

</details>

### P2. Who lives immediately above Q?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Floor puzzle

- A) P
- B) S
- C) T
- D) U

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** A) P

**Explanation:** Floor 5 is P.

</details>

### P3. How many people live between R and Q?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Floor puzzle

- A) 1
- B) 2
- C) 3
- D) 4

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) 2

**Explanation:** R is on floor 1 and Q on floor 4; U (2) and S (3) live between them.

</details>

### P4. Who lives on floor 2?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Floor puzzle

- A) S
- B) U
- C) P
- D) R

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) U

**Explanation:** U cannot live on 3 or 5 (next to Q), and 1, 4 and 6 are taken → U = 2.

</details>

## Set 2: Attribute matching

Four friends W, X, Y and Z each own a different pet (cat, dog, parrot, fish) and live in different cities (Pune, Delhi, Chennai, Kolkata).

- X owns the parrot.
- The person who lives in Chennai owns the fish.
- W lives in Delhi and does not own the dog.
- Y does not live in Chennai.
- Z does not own the cat.
- The dog's owner lives in Pune.

### P5. Who lives in Kolkata?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Attribute matching

- A) W
- B) X
- C) Y
- D) Z

<details>
<summary>Hint</summary>

W lives in Delhi, so W cannot own the fish (Chennai) or the dog (Pune).

</details>

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) X

**Explanation:** X = parrot. W (Delhi) cannot own the fish or the dog → W = cat. Y and Z share dog and fish. Y is not in Chennai, so Y cannot own the fish → Y = dog (Pune), Z = fish (Chennai). The remaining city, Kolkata, belongs to X.

</details>

### P6. Which pet does Z own?

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Attribute matching

- A) Cat
- B) Dog
- C) Parrot
- D) Fish

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** D) Fish

**Explanation:** From the solution: W cat (Delhi), X parrot (Kolkata), Y dog (Pune), Z fish (Chennai).

</details>

### P7. Which combination is correct?

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Attribute matching

- A) W – dog – Pune
- B) Y – dog – Pune
- C) Z – cat – Chennai
- D) X – fish – Kolkata

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) Y – dog – Pune

**Explanation:** Y owns the dog, and the dog's owner lives in Pune.

</details>
