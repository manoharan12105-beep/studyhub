# Seating Arrangement — Solved Examples

**Linear arrangement for E1–E3** (from the lesson): six friends sit in a row facing north — final order from left to right: **T S R Q U P**.

### E1. Position from an end

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Linear

**Problem:** Who sits second from the right end?

**Solution:**

1. From the right: P (1st), U (2nd).

**Answer:** **U**

### E2. People between two persons

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Linear

**Problem:** Who sits between R and P?

**Solution:**

1. R is at position 3 and P at position 6.
2. Positions 4 and 5 are Q and U.

**Answer:** **Q and U**

### E3. Immediate neighbour

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Linear

**Problem:** Who sits immediately to the left of R?

**Solution:**

1. Facing north, left of position 3 is position 2.
2. Position 2 is S.

**Answer:** **S**

**Circular arrangement for E4–E6** (from the lesson): six friends sit around a table facing the centre — clockwise from the top: **A, E, C, D, B, F**.

### E4. Opposite seat

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Circular

**Problem:** Who sits opposite B?

**Solution:**

1. Seats clockwise: A(1), E(2), C(3), D(4), B(5), F(6).
2. Opposite means 3 seats apart: B is seat 5 → seat 2 → E.

**Answer:** **E**

### E5. Immediate right in a circle

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Circular

**Problem:** Who sits immediately to the right of F?

**Solution:**

1. Facing the centre, right = anticlockwise.
2. Anticlockwise from F (seat 6) is seat 5 → B.

**Answer:** **B**

### E6. Second to the left

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Circular

**Problem:** Who sits second to the left of C?

**Solution:**

1. Facing the centre, left = clockwise.
2. Clockwise from C (seat 3): D (seat 4), then B (seat 5).

**Answer:** **B**
