# Seating Arrangement — Practice

Questions come in sets that share one arrangement. Solve the arrangement first, then answer the questions.

## Set 1: Linear

Five friends J, K, L, M and N sit in a row facing north.

- L sits at the extreme left end.
- N sits in the middle.
- K sits immediately to the right of N.
- J sits somewhere to the right of M.

### P1. Who sits at the extreme right end?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Linear

- A) K
- B) J
- C) M
- D) N

<details>
<summary>Hint</summary>

Fill positions 1, 3 and 4 first; only two seats remain for J and M.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) J

**Explanation:** L = 1, N = 3, K = 4. J and M take 2 and 5; J is to the right of M → M = 2, J = 5. Order: L M N K J.

</details>

### P2. Who sits between L and N?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Linear

- A) J
- B) K
- C) M
- D) No one

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) M

**Explanation:** L M N K J — position 2, between L (1) and N (3), is M.

</details>

### P3. How many people sit between L and K?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Linear

- A) 1
- B) 2
- C) 3
- D) 4

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) 2

**Explanation:** L is at 1 and K at 4; M and N sit between them.

</details>

## Set 2: Circular

Six friends P, Q, R, S, T and U sit around a circular table facing the centre.

- P sits opposite S.
- Q sits immediately to the left of P.
- R sits second to the left of S.
- T is not an immediate neighbour of Q.

### P4. Who sits opposite Q?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Circular

- A) R
- B) S
- C) T
- D) U

<details>
<summary>Hint</summary>

Facing the centre, left = clockwise. Place P at the top, then S, Q and R.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) T

**Explanation:** Clockwise from the top: P, Q, U, S, T, R. (Q is clockwise of P; R is two seats clockwise of S; of the two remaining seats, the one next to Q goes to U because T cannot sit beside Q.) Opposite Q is T.

</details>

### P5. Who sits immediately to the right of S?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Circular

- A) P
- B) T
- C) U
- D) R

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) U

**Explanation:** Right = anticlockwise. Anticlockwise from S is U (order clockwise: P, Q, U, S, T, R).

</details>

### P6. What is the position of U with respect to R?

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Circular

- A) Immediately to the left
- B) Second to the right
- C) Opposite
- D) Immediately to the right

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) Opposite

**Explanation:** Clockwise seats: P(1), Q(2), U(3), S(4), T(5), R(6). U and R are three seats apart → opposite.

</details>

### P7. If P and T exchange seats, who will sit opposite P?

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Circular

- A) Q
- B) R
- C) S
- D) U

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** A) Q

**Explanation:** P moves to T's seat (seat 5). The seat opposite seat 5 is seat 2, where Q sits.

</details>
