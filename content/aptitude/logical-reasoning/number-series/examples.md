# Number Series — Solved Examples

### E1. Arithmetic series

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Constant difference

**Problem:** 3, 7, 11, 15, ?

**Solution:**

1. Differences: 4, 4, 4.
2. Next = 15 + 4 = 19.

**Answer:** **19**

### E2. Geometric series

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Constant ratio

**Problem:** 2, 6, 18, 54, ?

**Solution:**

1. Ratios: 6/2 = 3, 18/6 = 3, 54/18 = 3.
2. Next = 54 × 3 = 162.

**Answer:** **162**

### E3. Differences in a pattern

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Differences in a pattern

**Problem:** 4, 6, 10, 16, 24, ?

**Solution:**

1. Differences: 2, 4, 6, 8 — increasing by 2.
2. Next difference = 10 → 24 + 10 = 34.

**Answer:** **34**

### E4. Squares plus one

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Squares and cubes

**Problem:** 2, 5, 10, 17, 26, ?

**Solution:**

1. Differences: 3, 5, 7, 9 (odd numbers) — a hint of squares.
2. Terms = 1² + 1, 2² + 1, 3² + 1, 4² + 1, 5² + 1.
3. Next = 6² + 1 = 37.

**Answer:** **37**

### E5. Alternating series

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Alternating series

**Problem:** 1, 10, 3, 9, 5, 8, ?

**Solution:**

1. Odd positions: 1, 3, 5 → +2.
2. Even positions: 10, 9, 8 → −1.
3. The 7th term is an odd position → 5 + 2 = 7.

**Answer:** **7**

### E6. Fibonacci-type

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Special sequences

**Problem:** 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, ?

**Solution:**

1. Each term is the sum of the previous two: 1 + 1 = 2, 1 + 2 = 3, 2 + 3 = 5, 3 + 5 = 8.
2. Next = 5 + 8 = 13.

**Answer:** **13**

### E7. Multiply and add

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Mixed operations

**Problem:** 3, 7, 15, 31, 63, ?

**Solution:**

1. Differences: 4, 8, 16, 32 — doubling.
2. Equivalent rule: each term = previous × 2 + 1.
3. Next = 63 × 2 + 1 = 127.

**Answer:** **127**

### E8. Increasing operations

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Mixed operations

**Problem:** 5, 6, 14, 45, 184, ?

**Solution:**

1. 5 × 1 + 1 = 6; 6 × 2 + 2 = 14; 14 × 3 + 3 = 45; 45 × 4 + 4 = 184.
2. Next = 184 × 5 + 5 = 925.

**Answer:** **925**

### E9. Wrong number

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Wrong number

**Problem:** Find the wrong number: 2, 5, 10, 17, 26, 38, 50

**Solution:**

1. Most terms are n² + 1: 2, 5, 10, 17, 26, —, 50.
2. The 6th term should be 6² + 1 = 37, not 38.
3. Check: replacing 38 with 37 gives differences 3, 5, 7, 9, 11, 13 — all consistent.

**Answer:** **38**
