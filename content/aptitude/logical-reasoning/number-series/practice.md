# Number Series — Practice

### P1. 5, 11, 17, 23, ?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Constant difference

- A) 27
- B) 28
- C) 29
- D) 31

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) 29

**Explanation:** +6 each time → 23 + 6 = 29.

</details>

### P2. 3, 12, 48, 192, ?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Constant ratio

- A) 384
- B) 576
- C) 768
- D) 960

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) 768

### P4. 2, 3, 5, 7, 11, 13, ?

- A) 15

### P6. 120, 99, 80, 63, 48, ?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Squares and cubes

- A) 33
- B) 35
- C) 36
- D) 37

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) 35

**Explanation:** 11² − 1, 10² − 1, 9² − 1, 8² − 1, 7² − 1 → 6² − 1 = 35. (Differences −21, −19, −17, −15 → −13.)

### P7. 2, 4, 12, 48, 240, ?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Changing ratio

- A) 480
- B) 960
- C) 1,200
- D) 1,440

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** D) 1,440

**Explanation:** ×2, ×3, ×4, ×5 → ×6 → 240 × 6 = 1,440.

</details>

### P8. 7, 26, 63, 124, ?

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Squares and cubes

- A) 189
- B) 215
- C) 216
- D) 217

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) 215

**Explanation:** 2³ − 1, 3³ − 1, 4³ − 1, 5³ − 1 → 6³ − 1 = 215.

</details>

### P9. 4, 9, 8, 18, 16, 27, ?

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Alternating series

- A) 24
- B) 32
- C) 36
- D) 54

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) 32

**Explanation:** Odd positions: 4, 8, 16 (×2) → 32. Even positions: 9, 18, 27 (+9). The 7th term is in the odd series.

</details>

### P10. Find the wrong number: 3, 5, 9, 17, 33, 66, 129

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Wrong number

- A) 9
- B) 33
- C) 66
- D) 129

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) 66

**Explanation:** Rule: × 2 − 1 → 3, 5, 9, 17, 33, 65, 129. The sixth term should be 65.

</details>

### P11. 1, 2, 6, 15, 31, ?

### P12. 10, 12, 16, 24, 40, ?

- A) 56
- B) 64

- D) 80

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>
