# Syllogisms — Solved Examples

Options used throughout: A) Only I follows · B) Only II follows · C) Either I or II follows · D) Neither I nor II follows · E) Both I and II follow.

### E1. Chain of "All"

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Chain of "All"

**Problem:**
Statements: All cats are animals. All animals are living beings.
Conclusions: I. All cats are living beings. II. Some living beings are cats.

**Solution:**

1. Cats ⊂ Animals ⊂ Living beings.
2. I: every cat is inside "living beings" ✓.
3. II: cats exist and are living beings, so some living beings are cats ✓.

**Answer:** **E) Both I and II follow**

### E2. Some + All

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Some + All

**Problem:**
Statements: Some pens are books. All books are bags.
Conclusions: I. Some pens are bags. II. All bags are pens.

**Solution:**

1. The pens that are books are inside "bags" → I ✓.
2. Bags may contain many things that are not pens → II ✗.

**Answer:** **A) Only I follows**

### E3. Negative statement

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Negative statements

**Problem:**
Statements: No apple is a banana. Some bananas are fruits.
Conclusions: I. Some fruits are not apples. II. No apple is a fruit.

**Solution:**

1. The fruits that are bananas cannot be apples → I ✓.
2. Apples could still overlap with fruits outside the banana circle → II is not certain ✗.

**Answer:** **A) Only I follows**

### E4. Either–or

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Either–or

**Problem:**
Statements: Some dogs are cats. Some cats are rats.
Conclusions: I. Some dogs are rats. II. No dog is a rat.

**Solution:**

1. The dogs that are cats may or may not overlap with the rats → I is not certain, II is not certain.
2. I and II are about the same two terms; in any situation, either some dogs are rats or none are.

**Answer:** **C) Either I or II follows**

### E5. Possibility that is impossible

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Possibility

**Problem:**
Statements: All pens are pencils. No pencil is an eraser.
Conclusions: I. No pen is an eraser. II. Some erasers being pens is a possibility.

**Solution:**

1. Pens ⊂ Pencils, and pencils share nothing with erasers → pens share nothing with erasers → I ✓.
2. Since I is certain, "some erasers are pens" can never happen → II ✗.

**Answer:** **A) Only I follows**

### E6. Possibility that follows

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Possibility

**Problem:**
Statements: All A are B. Some B are C.
Conclusions: I. Some C being A is a possibility. II. All C are B.

**Solution:**

1. The part of B that overlaps C may include part of A — a valid diagram exists → I ✓.
2. C may extend outside B → II ✗.

**Answer:** **A) Only I follows**
