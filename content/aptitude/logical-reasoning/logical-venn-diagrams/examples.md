# Logical Venn Diagrams — Solved Examples

Diagram types refer to the table in the lesson: 1 all separate · 2 nested · 3 two separate inside a third · 4 two overlap, third separate · 5 one inside another, third overlaps both · 6 all overlap · 7 two overlap inside a third · 8 one inside another, third separate.

### E1. Nested classes

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Choose the diagram

**Problem:** Which diagram represents *India, Tamil Nadu, Chennai*?

**Solution:**

1. Chennai is part of Tamil Nadu; Tamil Nadu is part of India.
2. Each class lies inside the next.

**Answer:** **Type 2 — nested**

### E2. Two separate classes inside a third

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Choose the diagram

**Problem:** Which diagram represents *Men, Women, Human beings*?

**Solution:**

1. Men and women share no members → separate.
2. Both are human beings → both inside the third circle.

**Answer:** **Type 3**

### E3. Subset with an overlapping class

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Choose the diagram

**Problem:** Which diagram represents *Doctors, Women, Mothers*?

**Solution:**

1. All mothers are women → Mothers ⊂ Women.
2. Some doctors are women, some are not → Doctors overlap Women.
3. Some doctors are mothers → Doctors overlap Mothers too.

**Answer:** **Type 5**

### E4. Regions from a survey

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Count from regions

**Problem:** In a survey: Only Cricket 12, Only Football 9, Only Hockey 7, Cricket and Football only 4, Football and Hockey only 3, Cricket and Hockey only 5, all three 2. How many play (a) exactly two sports, (b) cricket?

**Solution:**

1. (a) Exactly two = 4 + 3 + 5 = 12.
2. (b) Cricket = 12 + 4 + 5 + 2 = 23.

**Answer:** (a) **12** (b) **23**

### E5. Two-class formula

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Formula questions

**Problem:** In a class of 50, 30 students like tea, 25 like coffee and 10 like both. How many like neither?

**Solution:**

1. At least one = 30 + 25 − 10 = 45.
2. Neither = 50 − 45 = 5.

**Answer:** **5**

### E6. Three-class formula

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Formula questions

**Problem:** Of 100 students, 50 study Maths, 40 Physics and 35 Chemistry. 15 study Maths and Physics, 12 Physics and Chemistry, 10 Maths and Chemistry, and 5 study all three. How many study none of the three?

**Solution:**

1. At least one = 50 + 40 + 35 − 15 − 12 − 10 + 5 = 93.
2. None = 100 − 93 = 7.

**Answer:** **7**
