# Analogy — Solved Examples

### E1. Worker and workplace

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Complete the analogy

**Problem:** Doctor : Hospital :: Teacher : ?

**Solution:**

1. Relationship: a doctor works in a hospital.
2. A teacher works in a school.

**Answer:** **School**

### E2. Tool and function

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Complete the analogy

**Problem:** Pen : Write :: Knife : ?

**Solution:**

1. A pen is used to write.
2. A knife is used to cut.

**Answer:** **Cut**

### E3. Animal and young one

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Complete the analogy

**Problem:** Cow : Calf :: Dog : ?

**Solution:**

1. A calf is the young one of a cow.
2. A puppy is the young one of a dog.

**Answer:** **Puppy**

### E4. Cube

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Number analogy

**Problem:** 3 : 27 :: 4 : ?

**Solution:**

1. 27 = 3³.
2. 4³ = 64.

**Answer:** **64**

### E5. Letter shift

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Letter analogy

**Problem:** ACE : BDF :: GIK : ?

**Solution:**

1. A → B, C → D, E → F: each letter +1.
2. G → H, I → J, K → L.

**Answer:** **HJL**

### E6. Square minus one

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Number analogy

**Problem:** 6 : 35 :: 8 : ?

**Solution:**

1. 35 = 6² − 1.
2. 8² − 1 = 63.

**Answer:** **63**

### E7. Choose the analogous pair

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Choose the analogous pair

**Problem:** Carpenter : Saw :: ? — A) Farmer : Field B) Farmer : Plough C) Tailor : Cloth D) Painter : Picture

**Solution:**

1. Relationship: a carpenter uses a saw as a tool.
2. A) field is a workplace; C) cloth is material; D) picture is the product.
3. B) a plough is a farmer's tool.

**Answer:** **B) Farmer : Plough**
