# Analogy — Practice

### P1. Eye : See :: Ear : ?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Organ and function

- A) Sound
- B) Hear
- C) Noise
- D) Music

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) Hear

**Explanation:** The eye's function is to see; the ear's function is to hear. "Sound" is what is heard, not the function.

</details>

### P2. Bird : Nest :: Bee : ?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Creature and home

- A) Honey
- B) Flower
- C) Hive
- D) Insect

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) Hive

**Explanation:** A nest is a bird's home; a hive is a bee's home.

</details>

### P3. 5 : 25 :: 7 : ?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Pattern:** Number analogy

- A) 35
- B) 42
- C) 49
- D) 343

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) 49

**Explanation:** 25 = 5² → 7² = 49.

</details>

### P4. Soldier : Army :: Sailor : ?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Individual and group

- A) Ship
- B) Navy
- C) Sea
- D) Captain

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) Navy

**Explanation:** A soldier is a member of an army; a sailor is a member of a navy. "Ship" is a workplace.

</details>

### P5. Hot : Cold :: Ascend : ?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Antonyms

- A) Climb
- B) Rise
- C) Descend
- D) Mount

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) Descend

**Explanation:** Hot and cold are opposites; the opposite of ascend is descend. The other options are synonyms of ascend.

</details>

### P6. CAT : DBU :: DOG : ?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Letter analogy

- A) EPH
- B) EOH
- C) CNF
- D) FQI

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** A) EPH

**Explanation:** Each letter +1: D → E, O → P, G → H.

</details>

### P7. 2 : 9 :: 3 : ?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Pattern:** Number analogy

- A) 10
- B) 27
- C) 28
- D) 30

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) 28

**Explanation:** 9 = 2³ + 1 → 3³ + 1 = 28. (2 : 9 does not fit a square rule: 2² = 4.)

</details>

### P8. Paper : Tree :: Glass : ?

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Product and raw material

- A) Window
- B) Sand
- C) Mirror
- D) Bottle

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) Sand

**Explanation:** Paper is made from wood from trees; glass is made mainly from sand. The other options are products made of glass.

</details>

### P9. Thermometer : Temperature :: ?

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Choose the analogous pair

- A) Speedometer : Car
- B) Barometer : Pressure
- C) Clock : Wall
- D) Scale : Ruler

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** B) Barometer : Pressure

**Explanation:** A thermometer measures temperature; a barometer measures (atmospheric) pressure. A speedometer measures speed, not a car.

</details>

### P10. 7 : 50 :: 9 : ?

**Difficulty:** Hard · **Pattern:** Number analogy

- A) 72
- B) 81
- C) 82
- D) 90

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Answer:** C) 82

**Explanation:** 50 = 7² + 1 → 9² + 1 = 82. Option B forgets the +1.

</details>
